---
title: Memorising and casting spells
source: pg
section: "1.8.1"
---
Casters cast only spells they have memorised into spell slots; how many slots of each spell level a caster has is on the class table (`class_lookup`), and the spells themselves are in `spell_lookup`. Spell level is not character level: a 6th-level cleric cannot use 6th-level cleric spells.

**Slots.**

- A spell fills one empty slot of its own level, no higher and no lower.
- Casting a spell wipes it from memory; to cast it twice the caster needs it in two slots.
- A caster may forget a memorised spell without casting it to free the slot.
- A reversible spell is fixed as normal or reversed when memorised; holding both versions takes two slots.

**Memorisation.** Usually it is enough that the caster had an undisturbed night's sleep (a night attack breaks it). When the exact time matters: first rest at least 4 hours somewhere quiet without interruption, then spend 15 minutes per spell level memorised. One 3rd-level, two 2nd and four 1st-level spells take 165 minutes after the rest. Magic-users and illusionists need their spellbooks to memorise.

**General rules.**

- The spell's power must be able to travel in a straight line from its point of origin to its target, unless the description says otherwise.
- A creature can be under only one instance of the same spell at a time: two blesses give one bonus. Instantaneous spells are the exception; several cure light wounds can land in one segment.
- Recasting a spell with a duration does not strengthen it; it counts as a fresh casting, so a target gets a fresh save, and passing it does not end the earlier casting.
