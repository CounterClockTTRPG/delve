---
title: Weapon specialisation
source: pg
section: "1.3.13"
---
An optional rule, open only to fighters, paladins and rangers, and only at character creation (later only through a wish or similar gift).

**Cost.** Two proficiency slots for a melee weapon or a crossbow; three for any other missile weapon. The weapon must be one exact type.

**Benefit.** +1 to hit and +2 damage with that weapon. These bonuses are not magical and do not let the wielder harm creatures that need magic weapons to hit.

**More attacks.** With a specialised melee weapon the attack rate follows its own progression instead of the normal one (the two do not stack):

- levels 1–6: 3 attacks every 2 rounds;
- levels 7–12: 2 per round;
- levels 13 and up: 5 every 2 rounds.

A rate of 5/2 means three attacks in one round (segments 1, 5 and 10) and two the next (segments 1 and 10), whatever the initiative roll.

With a specialised missile weapon the rate of fire rises instead: bows 2, then 3 at 7th, then 4 at 13th; light crossbow 1, then 2 in odd rounds at 7th, then 2 at 13th; heavy crossbow one shot every other round, then 1 at 7th, then 2 in odd rounds at 13th. Extra shots come in the shooter's own initiative segment.

**Double specialisation** (optional, a third slot, not for pole arms or two-handed swords): +3 to hit and +3 damage.
