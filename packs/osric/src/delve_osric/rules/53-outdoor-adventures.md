---
title: Wilderness travel day by day
source: pg
section: "1.7.2"
---
Wilderness means any open country, sea included. The unit of time is the day. New DMs should resolve each day separately; letting days run together is for later.

**Each day:**

1. **Setup.** The DM gives the weather; the party chooses a direction.
2. **Navigation.** The DM checks in secret whether the party has strayed (see wilderness adventuring).
3. **Encounter check** (`encounter_check`).
4. **Move and act.** Travel at the daily rate for the terrain (see overland movement); brief static actions fit around it.
5. **Encounter.** Resolve any meeting.
6. **Camp.** The DM describes the possible campsites; the party makes camp and sets watches.
7. **Night check.** Roll for encounters again, and play out any that come.

Outdoors, encounter distance is measured in yards rather than feet: 1d4+4 × 10 yards, or 1d3 × 10 yards when either side is surprised. Light and terrain still matter: open plains reveal a party from far off, forests hide both sides.

Because the scale is large, most short actions take no noticeable time; what matters is miles covered, food and water, the state of mounts and the chance of trouble. A route along a road, river or other clear landmark keeps the party from getting lost.
