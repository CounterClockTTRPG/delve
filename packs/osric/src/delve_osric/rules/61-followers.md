---
title: Followers
source: gmg
section: "2.2.5"
---
Followers come when a character reaches "name" level and builds a stronghold or meets the class's other conditions. Clerics and fighters gain theirs as their class descriptions say (`class_lookup`). Druids, illusionists, magic-users, monks and paladins get none automatically.

**Assassins** gain followers only by taking over a guild. Of its 6d4 members, a quarter stay on as followers. For each, d100 gives the level: 01–20 1st, 21–45 2nd, 46–65 3rd, 66–80 4th, 81–90 5th, 91–95 6th, 96–00 7th.

**Thieves** of 10th level who kill or oust the head of the local guild inherit its 6d4 thieves. A character who founds a rival guild attracts 6d4 too, and is at once in a deadly feud with the old one. Each thief's level on d100: 01–10 1st, 11–20 2nd, 21–45 3rd, 46–65 4th, 66–80 5th, 81–90 6th, 91–00 7th.

**Rangers** choose one of two bands:

- **Merry folk:** 6d6 humans and demi-humans, half of them zero-level and half 1st-level characters of assorted classes. They may serve as henchmen without counting against the henchman limit.
- **Magical creatures,** picked by the DM: 1d4 magical beasts (each a giant owl, giant eagle, dire wolf, bear or giant badger); one steed (centaur, hippogriff, pegasus or unicorn); 1d3−1 sylvan folk (brownies, dryads, naiads, pixies, satyrs); and if that roll gives 0 or 1, a guardian as well (young copper dragon, treant, werebear or weretiger, the were-creatures perhaps casting as druids of level 1d6). They use their normal statistics, but they can speak and magically know Common (perhaps elvish or fey tongues too), and any with less than low Intelligence is raised to low.

Followers are loyal by default (status 0 in the loyalty modifiers) but still respond to pay, treatment and leadership.
