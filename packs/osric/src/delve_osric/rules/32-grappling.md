---
title: Grappling results
source: pg
section: "1.6.12"
---
After an unarmed hit to grapple, roll d8. Modifiers: −1 with only one arm, +1 with more than two; size −4 (tiny) to +10 (gargantuan); the defender's equivalents count the other way. Strength adds to damage only. Most damage is temporary, healing at 1 hp a round.

**Holds.** The highest current roll in a grapple is the **controlling** hold (on a tie, whoever got it first); anything lower is an **inferior** hold, which does lesser damage and no special effect unless stated. A new, better hold pushes the opponent's controlling hold down to its inferior form.

- **1 or less, awkward scuffling:** 1 temporary damage; this alone puts no one in the grappling condition.
- **2–4, arm grab** (inferior: elbow bash): damage equal to the roll, 1 real. The grabber may try to disarm; if that fails, the target breaks free.
- **5, waist lock** (leg hold): 5 damage, 1 real; the holder may let go and stand.
- **6, rear choke** (head butt): 6 damage, 2 real; the victim cannot shout or cast, and an ally may force a potion down its throat.
- **7, arm lock** (wicked elbow bash): 7 damage, 2 real; may force the victim prone.
- **8, head lock** (gouge): 8 damage, 2 real; may force it prone.
- **9 or more, decisive throw:** breaks every hold, 9 damage (3 real), the victim loses its next action, and the thrower re-rolls a new hold at +2 (at most 8).

Each later round a grappler keeps its hold (same damage, automatically) or rolls a new unarmed attack for a better one. A grabbed defender can use only dagger-sized or natural weapons. A creature holds only one foe, but others joining in hit on anything but a natural 1, so piling on knocks a victim out fast.
