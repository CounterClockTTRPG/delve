---
title: Listening
source: pg
section: "1.7.1.5"
---
Anyone can listen, usually at a door before opening it. It only works if the whole party keeps still and quiet and the listener takes off any helmet; otherwise only the loudest sounds carry.

- **Chance:** 10% for most characters; 15% for elves, gnomes, halflings and half-orcs. Thieves and assassins use their skill (`skill_check`).
- **Time:** a round per try. A character may try up to three times at one spot; after that, further tries fail until a turn's rest.
- **Secrecy:** the DM rolls hidden, since silence could mean failure or an empty room, and rolls even when nothing is there so the dice give nothing away.

A success means something is heard only if something is there to hear. Describe the sound, not its source: "a dry scuttling", not "a giant spider". Characters who know monster tongues may catch words. Some monsters, such as bugbears, move silently and cannot be heard. Monsters listen for the party too.

If constant listening slows the game, point out the time and the bother of helmets coming off, and use silent monsters or misleading echoes; talk to the players frankly before anything harsher. In truly deadly places caution is fair play and should not be punished.
