---
title: Missile fire and ranges
source: pg
section: "1.6.2.6"
---
Missile attacks (bows, crossbows, slings, thrown weapons) are rolled like melee attacks: d20 plus modifiers against the number needed for the target's Armour Class.

**Modifiers and limits:**

- Add the Dexterity missile to-hit adjustment. Strength adds to damage only with **hurled** weapons, not launched ones (unless a specially made bow says otherwise).
- With a missile weapon ready, Dexterity also shifts the shooter's initiative segment.
- Shooting into a melee: the target is chosen at random among everyone in it, friend or foe.
- You can shoot over an ally only if the ally is at least one size smaller than you.
- A character who fires a launched missile cannot move that round; throwing a weapon allows movement.
- Heavy crossbows cannot be used from horseback.

**Range increments.** Each weapon has an increment (longbow 70 ft, short bow 50 ft, composite long 60 ft, composite short 50 ft, heavy crossbow 80 ft, light crossbow 60 ft, sling 35 ft, javelin 20 ft, dart and spear 15 ft, hand axe, club, dagger and light hammer 10 ft). The first increment is free; each further one is −2 to hit. Past five full increments the target cannot be hit; longbows top out at 350 ft and short bows at 250 ft.

**Grenade-like missiles** (oil, acid, holy water): 10-ft increment. A flask not prepared beforehand has a 25% chance not to break; preparing takes a round and leaves it leaking. A miss lands in an adjacent square chosen by d8. Acid does 2d4 on a direct hit and 1 point splash within 5 ft; holy water 2d4 to undead, 2 splash; burning oil 2d6, then 1d6 the next round, 1d3 splash. Unlit oil needs a torch hit (treat as AC 10 [10]) to ignite.
