---
title: Scrolls, rods, staves and wands
source: gmg
section: "2.13.3"
---
**Scrolls** come in tubes, plain or ornate. A tube may be magically sealed, needing read magic to read the words that open it; reading the tube also shows whether it is cursed or trapped. An arcane spell scroll then needs read magic again to understand; once a user has read a scroll this way, that user need not do so again for it. Read magic is what sets off a cursed scroll; ordinary scrolls are not triggered by it. At the DM's option, a scroll left long in its tube unread can lose its writing, at most on a 1–3 on a d10. Only the right class can use a spell scroll.

**Scrolls of warding** create a 10-ft radius around the reader that moves with it, unless stated. Creatures of the warded kind cannot enter, but if the reader pushes the protected zone onto one of them, the ward ends. Different wards can run together; two of the same kind do not add. Each has its own reading time, duration and limit (some ward only so many Hit Dice; excess creatures may come in and attack): acid, demons, devils, elementals, lycanthropes and shape-changers, magic, petrifaction, polymorph, possession and undead.

**Rods, staves and wands.** Wands are about a foot long; rods about 3 ft; staves about 6 ft and wooden. Most use charges, which the DM keeps secret unless the party finds a way to learn them:

- wands: 101 minus 1d20 (81–100);
- rods: 51 minus 1d10 (41–50);
- staves: 26 minus 1d6 (20–25).

A high-level magic-user can recharge them, but a device that reaches zero charges crumbles and is lost. Unless noted, each needs a command word, so silence stops it. Saving throws against them use the aimed magic items category.
