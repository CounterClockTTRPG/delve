---
title: Helpless creatures
source: pg
section: "1.6.10.10"
---
**Held (paralysed)**, usually by hold person or hold monster, and **magically asleep** creatures are physically helpless and cannot move or act.

- An attacker holding a hand weapon that can harm the creature, with no other enemy in its melee range, simply kills it.
- If another enemy is in reach, the attacker still hits automatically with any weapon, unarmed or touch attack that can affect the creature, and may choose maximum damage instead of rolling.
- An attacker whose every target this round is held, magically asleep or unconscious makes twice its normal number of attacks.

**Unconscious** creatures (from a spell, or from dropping to 0 hp or below) cannot act and can be slain or struck as if magically asleep.

**Normally asleep** creatures are not helpless in the same way and cannot be killed automatically. The attacker rolls to hit at +4, ignoring shield and Dexterity. On a hit it may then try to kill outright, as an assassin would: 50%, plus 5% per own level or Hit Die, minus 5% for every two levels or Hit Dice of the sleeper, rounded down. When the sleeper has both levels and Hit Dice, the DM picks which to count. Success kills regardless of hit points; failure still deals the weapon's damage.

Stunned creatures, by contrast, cannot act but are not helpless: attackers get +4 and the target loses shield and Dexterity bonuses, but each attack is rolled normally.
