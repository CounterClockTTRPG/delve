---
title: Researching new spells
source: gmg
section: "2.14.2"
---
High-level magic-users and illusionists can invent spells by working out new words, gestures and components; clerics and druids can do the same through study and prayer. Every new spell needs the DM's approval, since it can upset the game.

**Time:** at least one week per spell level, plus one more week.

**Cost:** 200 gp for each spell level, and 1d4 × 100 gp more every week, assuming a proper laboratory (arcane) or shrine (divine). Without one, the base cost is 2,000 gp per spell level, plus the same weekly sum.

**Success roll.** When the minimum time and money are spent, roll d100. The chance is:

10% + Intelligence (magic-users, illusionists) or Wisdom (clerics, druids) + the researcher's level − twice the new spell's level.

A 12th-level magic-user with 17 Intelligence researching a 4th-level spell has 10 + 17 + 12 − 8 = 31%.

**Spending to improve the odds.** Each extra 2,000 gp per spell level paid during research raises the 10% base by 10%. This can be bought up to four times, for a base of 50% at an extra 8,000 gp per spell level.

**Failure.** Research may continue: after another week, paying again 200 gp for each spell level and 1d4 × 100 gp, roll again, and so on for as long as time and money last.

The DM sets the spell's level, range, duration and components with the player, judging them against existing spells of similar power (`spell_lookup`).
