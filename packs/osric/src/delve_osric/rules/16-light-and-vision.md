---
title: Light and infravision
source: pg
section: "1.5.4"
---
Underground the party needs light unless every member has infravision.

**Light sources:**

- **Torch:** lights 40 ft, lasts 6 turns (an hour); can be put out by wind, water or magic and cannot be shuttered without dousing it.
- **Hooded lantern:** 30 ft radius, a pint of oil every 4 hours; the hood can be closed to hide it (and then it gives no light).
- **Bullseye lantern:** a beam 80 ft long and 10 ft wide, a pint every 4 hours, also coverable.
- **Candle:** 20 ft, half an hour, blown out more easily than a torch.
- **Magic weapon:** usually glows when drawn, 10 ft for a dagger to 20 ft for a longsword, forever, and cannot be covered.

Other sources are the DM's call, using these as a guide. Light is visible far beyond the area it illuminates, so a lit party warns the dungeon of its coming; the DM should adjust surprise accordingly.

**Infravision** lets a creature see in darkness, normally to 60 ft, though some deep dwellers see further. It does not work inside the radius of any light, shows no colours, and is poor for searching or close examination: a character relying on it sees only vague shapes of furniture, may take undead for statues, and cannot spot secret doors or read writing. For that reason even orcs and other infravision users may prefer torches.
