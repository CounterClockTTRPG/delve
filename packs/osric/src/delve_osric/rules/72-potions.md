---
title: Potions and mixing them
source: gmg
section: "2.13.2"
---
**Basics.** A potion is one dose, usually unlabelled. A sip hints at what it is, unreliably, since brewers differ. Unless the description says otherwise, a potion starts working 1d4+1 segments after it is drunk and lasts 1d4 turns. Half a dose gives the effect for half the time. Brewing needs a magic-user with an alchemist (alone above 11th level) and a sample to work from.

**Mixing.** Two potions mix if poured together (external) or if one is drunk while another is still working (internal). Some combinations have fixed results:

- with clairaudience or clairvoyance: the drinker is stunned for 1d4 days and must be watched;
- with longevity: acts as slow on the drinker for an hour;
- with polymorph: a deadly poison, save at −4 or die;
- with delusion: always a delusion, so the drinker is sure the other potion worked.

Otherwise roll d100:

| d100 | Result |
|---|---|
| 01 | Explosion: 6d10 to the drinker and 1d10 to everyone within 10 ft; if mixed outside a body, 4d6 to all within 10 ft, no save |
| 02–03 | Poison: drunk, instant death, no save; mixed outside, a 10-ft cloud, save vs. poison or die |
| 04–07 | Sickness: −1d4 STR and DEX for 4d6 rounds, no save; one potion void, the other at half strength |
| 08–12 | Both cancelled |
| 13–20 | One cancelled, the other at half strength and duration |
| 21–30 | Both work at half |
| 31–90 | Both work normally |
| 91–99 | One works at 150%, the other is cancelled |
| 00 | One effect becomes permanent, for better or worse |

Where one potion is singled out, pick it at random.
