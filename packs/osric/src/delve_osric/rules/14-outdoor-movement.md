---
title: Overland movement and forced marches
source: pg
section: "1.5.3.2"
---
**Daily travel on foot** is the movement rate times a terrain factor, in miles per day:

- level ground: × 0.2 (a rate of 120 gives 24 miles);
- rugged ground: × 0.15;
- very rugged ground: × 0.1.

Use the encumbered rate if the traveller is loaded; a mounted party uses the mounts' rate. For outdoor fights the DM may triple movement and missile ranges (but not spell areas).

**Forced march.** A party may push up to double speed. The chance of exhaustion, checked once for the whole group, equals the percentage added: going at 130% risks 30%. An exhausted party must rest a full day before moving on. If it presses on anyway, horses and mules ridden have a 10% chance of dying unless allowed to walk unloaded, and characters and monsters act as one level (or Hit Die) lower until rested; reaching zero means death.

**Escaping pursuit outdoors.** Base chance 80% per hour that the fleeing side gets away, modified:

- slowest creature among the pursuers +10%; same slowest speed on both sides 0; slowest among the fleeing −10%
- plains, desert or water −50%; rough, hills or marsh +10%; forest or mountains +30%
- fleeing group under 6 +10%, 12–50 −20%, over 50 −50%
- pursuers under 12 −20%; pursuers include a flyer −20%
- day −30%, twilight −10%, night +25%

If the pursuers can simply catch up, or the chance is 0%, there is a fight. Otherwise roll d100 each hour; at or under the chance, the trail is lost.
