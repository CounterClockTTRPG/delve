---
title: Casting time and interruption
source: pg
section: "1.8.1.1"
---
**Timing.** A caster starts casting in its side's initiative segment; that segment is the first of the casting time. A spell measured in segments goes off in the start segment plus the casting time, minus one: a 4-segment spell begun in segment 5 lands in segment 8. If the count runs past 10, it carries over into the next round (an 8-segment spell begun in segment 6 lands in the following round's third segment).

- **Fast** (1 segment): takes effect in the segment it is cast.
- **1 round:** takes effect next round, in the same segment number it was begun.
- **1 turn:** takes effect nine rounds after the round of casting, again in the same segment.

**While casting** the caster can only chant, gesture and handle components. It cannot move (a moving ship's deck is fine), and it loses its Dexterity bonus to AC. It may move in a round when it only finishes a spell begun earlier.

**Interruption.** The spell fails if, before it completes, the caster:

1. loses any hit points;
2. cannot perform a component, as when grappled (gestures) or silenced or gagged (words);
3. moves, or is moved 5 ft or more;
4. or another rule says so.

The spell is then gone from memory with no effect. Crucially, the danger starts at the declaration, not the start segment: a caster who declared a spell and is hit by an enemy acting earlier loses it, even before beginning. Casting in melee range is allowed but risky.

**Continuing casting** lasts only while the caster chants; it may walk at half speed meanwhile. **Concentration** is different: no other spell, no fighting, usually no moving; it breaks if the caster is killed, knocked out, stunned, charmed or mesmerised, and at the DM's option after heavy damage.
