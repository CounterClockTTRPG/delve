---
title: Falling
source: pg
section: "1.5.5"
---
Falls shorter than 10 ft do no damage. Beyond that the damage grows steeply:

| Height | Damage |
|---|---|
| 10 ft | 1d6 |
| 20 ft | 3d6 |
| 30 ft | 6d6 |
| 40 ft | 10d6 |
| 50 ft | 15d6 |
| more than 50 ft | 20d6 |

As an option, the DM may grant a saving throw to halve the damage; which category to use is the DM's call (a Dexterity-flavoured save may take the agility modifier).

Gear can break in a fall: if the character's own save is failed, each carried item checks against the "fall" column of the item saving throw table once per 10 ft fallen, or only once in all if the landing is soft (water, a heap of leaves). See item saving throws.

Overland, pits and cliffs are common; traps that drop victims into pits use the same damage, usually with spikes or poison added on top.
