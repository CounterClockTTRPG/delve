---
title: Surprise
source: pg
section: "1.6.1.1"
---
Surprise is rolled once, at the start of an encounter, by each side that could be caught off guard; a group the party has plainly alerted (by bashing at a door, say) does not roll. Being wary is not enough to skip the roll.

Each side rolls a d6:

- **1:** surprised for one segment.
- **2:** surprised for two segments.
- **3–6:** not surprised, unless the enemy surprises on a higher number (a monster that "surprises on 1–3" catches a side that rolls 3, for three segments; one that surprises on 1–4 can win four, and so on).

Anyone not surprised may act in each segment the other side is still surprised. If the party rolls 1 and the monsters 2, the party is frozen one segment, the monsters two: the party gets one free segment. If the party rolls 2 and the monsters 5, the monsters get two free segments. If both are surprised for the same time, nobody gains.

**Individual adjustments.** A character's Dexterity surprise modifier changes only how long that character is frozen: a +2 cancels two segments, a penalty adds them. It never creates surprise on the other side. Monsters that surprise on more than 1–2 do change the other side's chance. Heavy encumbrance strips bonuses (see encumbrance); some classes and monsters cannot be surprised at all.

**What fits in a segment.** Anything brief: a short shout, an attack, a charge, closing, fleeing, setting a spear, starting a spell. A spell only completes if it takes one segment. Movement in one segment is a tenth of the round's move.
