---
title: Damage, dying and death
source: pg
section: "1.6.6"
---
Damage comes off hit points. At **0 hp** a character falls unconscious and bleeds, losing 1 hp each round, and dies at **−10**. Any further damage taken while unconscious, other than the bleeding, kills at once. Bleeding stops the round someone gives aid.

**Coming round.** Even when brought back to 1 hp or more by spell, potion or rest, a character who went to 0 or below stays in a coma for 1d6 turns and then needs at least a week of rest before any strenuous activity, of body or mind. A wound that took the character to −6 or worse leaves lasting scars.

**Death.** A dead character can be raised by a cleric of enough level. Raise dead and resurrection fail on elves and half-orcs, whose spirits are not souls in the human sense. Without a party cleric, a temple's high priest may do it for a fee, usually at least 1,000 gp. Each raising needs a resurrection survival roll by Constitution and lowers Constitution by 1; the original Constitution is the lifetime limit on returns (`resurrection_check`).

**Damage types.** Weapons do blunt, piercing or slashing damage; unarmed blows mostly do temporary damage, which non-living things ignore. Other types are cold, corrosive, electrical, fire, poison and magical (as from spiritual weapon, which bypasses immunities). Immunity to "non-magical weapons" normally covers only blunt, piercing and slashing damage from mundane weapons and fists, so a torch's fire still burns such a creature.

The guide words the bleeding rule for characters; whether a monster at 0 hp is dying or simply dead is for the DM to decide.
