title: Strongholds and siege damage
source: gmg

High-level characters build strongholds; for fighters and clerics this is how followers arrive (see followers).

**Building.** The DM prices the plan from the cost table, adding up the parts. Time is about a month for each 2,000 gp of work or part thereof. Some DMs let clerics build at half cost, helped by their church. Sample costs and structural hit points:

- stone keep (150 × 150 × 30 ft): 8,000 gp, 175 SHP
- stone barbican: 5,000 gp, 150 SHP
- stone gatehouse: 2,500 gp, 120 SHP
- stone wall, 100 ft long, 30 ft high: 1,000 gp, 30 SHP
- stone towers: 1,000 gp (20 ft square, 40 SHP) or 1,500 gp (30 ft square, 60 SHP)
- stone building 600 gp (30 SHP); wooden 300 gp (12 SHP)
- gate 600 gp (12 SHP); drawbridge 500 gp (15 SHP)
- moat, 100 ft: 200 gp; tunnel, per 10 ft: 250 gp
- wooden palisade or earth rampart, 100 ft: 150 gp each (8 and 20 SHP)

**Structural hit points (SHP)** work like hit points for buildings, but only large-scale force harms them: siege engines, giants' boulders and the blows of creatures such as iron and stone golems or earth elementals (1d3 SHP), and certain powerful spells and items.

**War machines** need crews of up to six trained soldiers and are too slow to aim at small moving targets. Anyone standing where a catapult or trebuchet stone lands must save vs. death or take ten times the SHP rolled as hit points.

Running a domain beyond this (taxes, garrison, upkeep) is left to the DM; remember that tax income gives no XP.
