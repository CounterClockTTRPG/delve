---
title: Poison
source: pg
section: "1.6.9.1"
---
Venom, plant toxins on needles and traps, and poisonous gas all call for a saving throw vs. poison when the poison is swallowed, breathed or enters the blood. Weak poisons (a large spider's) may give up to +2; deadly ones down to −4.

**Failure means death, but not instantly.** The victim is usually incapacitated at once, leaving a window, set by the caster's level, for slow poison or neutralise poison to save them. A character killed by poison cannot be raised until the poison is cleared from the body.

**Damaging poisons.** Many poisons only do damage instead of killing, usually halved by a successful save.

**Poison in player hands** should be rare, because it tilts the game badly.

- Venom milked from a creature starts weakening at once and is useless within hours.
- Plant toxins that some assassins learn to brew keep longer if stored properly, but are dangerous to handle; the DM may call for a save after any slip with them.
- Paladins and some other good characters refuse to use poison and object to companions who do.
- Very large or alien creatures, dragons above all, are normally unaffected by poisons.

Poison checks use `saving_throw` in the death, paralysis and poison category; monster entries (`monster_lookup`) give their own poison's modifier and effect.
