title: Disease and insanity
source: pg
section: "1.6.9.2"
---
**Disease** mostly comes from monsters (mummies, giant rats); their entries give the chance and effect.

**Plague.** On contact, save vs. poison. Success gives +4 on later saves against that disease. Failure: after 2d8 days of incubation, −1d6 to all abilities and to all rolls for a further 2d8 days. If either of those duration dice shows an 8, the victim dies at the end; otherwise the penalty eases by 1 a day until gone. Later saves against the same disease are at +4 either way.

**Infection.** Where the DM thinks it fits, usually when someone with an open wound wades through filth such as a sewer, call for a save vs. poison. Failure works like plague but with the times in hours rather than days.

**Insanity** from spells or monsters: the DM picks one that suits the cause, or rolls d6. It lasts until cured.

1. **Decline:** lose 1d6 each of INT, WIS and CHA.
2. **Amnesia:** 50% to fail to recall a spell when casting; each morning 50% to forget a weapon proficiency for the day.
3. **Split personality:** a second self, perhaps of another alignment or class; 25% each morning or under stress to switch.
4. **Apathy:** 25% at the start of a fight to do nothing until attacked.
5. **Compulsive lying,** however absurd.
6. **Phobia** of a thing, place or situation; forced contact gives 25% to become confused as the spell.
