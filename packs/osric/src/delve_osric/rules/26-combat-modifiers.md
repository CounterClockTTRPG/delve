---
title: Cover, concealment and other combat modifiers
source: pg
section: "1.6.3"
---
**Concealment** blocks sight but not blows: fog, smoke, foliage. Attackers take −1 for a quarter concealed, −2 for half, −3 for three-quarters and −4 if wholly hidden. It does not add to invisibility.

**Cover** physically stops attacks: walls, battlements, arrow slits. Attackers take −2 against 25% cover (a flimsy fence), −4 against 50% (a tree trunk), −7 against 75% (a low wall) and −10 against 90% (an arrow slit). Anyone with at least 25% cover also gains +2 on saving throws where dodging matters.

**Rear attacks** are at +2 and ignore the target's shield and Dexterity bonus.

**Two weapons:** main hand −2, off hand −4 (dagger or hand axe only); see melee attacks.

**No helmet.** Against a bareheaded target, each attack has a 1 in 6 chance of landing on the head, which has AC 10 [10] instead of the armour's. This is chance, not an aimed blow, and does no extra damage. It gives nothing against someone already AC 10.

**Conditions, in short:**

- invisible: −4 to attack if its rough position is known, otherwise unattackable; +4 on its saves;
- prone or stunned: attackers +4, no shield or Dexterity bonus;
- staggered: attackers +2;
- blinded: −4 to hit and on saves, AC 4 worse;
- held, magically asleep or unconscious: automatic hits, maximum damage, or an outright kill if no other enemy is in reach (see conditions).
