---
title: Level drain
source: pg
section: "1.6.7"
---
Some undead, spells and items drain life energy; "level drain" and "energy drain" mean the same thing.

- Each level drained costs a player character one full experience level. The character drops to the lowest experience total of the new level, losing the hit points, spells and abilities of the level taken.
- A multi-classed or dual-classed character loses a level from the class with the highest level; if two are tied, pick one at random.
- A character drained below 1st level dies, and may rise later as an undead of the kind that slew it, as the monster entry says.
- NPCs and monsters lose a level or a Hit Die, whichever applies.

Restoration spells can give drained levels back (`spell_lookup`); without them, the levels must be earned again.

A related loss comes from exhaustion on a forced march: an exhausted creature acts a level or a Hit Die lower until it rests, and the loss returns after rest. That one is temporary, unlike true drain.

When narrating a drain, make it felt: cold, weakness, years stripped away. Players should know at once that something has been taken.
