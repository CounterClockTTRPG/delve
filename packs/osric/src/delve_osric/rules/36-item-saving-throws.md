---
title: Item saving throws
source: pg
section: "1.6.4"
---
Gear is at risk only when its owner fails a save against breath, fire, lightning, acid or a long fall. Then the DM rolls for the items (the DM picks which), by kind of harm and nearest material:

| Harm | Crystal or glass | Leather | Metal | Paper | Pottery or bone | Rope or cloth | Stone or gem | Wood |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acid | 5 | 10 | 7 | 16 | 10 | 12 | 3 | 10 |
| Cold (magic) | 5 | 3 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Crushing blow | 19 | 4 | 6 | 11 | 17 | 10 | 17 | 10 |
| Disintegrate | 20 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 18 | 20 |
| Fall (per 10 ft) | 10 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Fire (magic) | 5 | 6 | 2 | 20 | 6 | 15 | 2 | 7 |
| Fire (normal) | 3 | 4 | 1 | 18 | 3 | 10 | 1 | 5 |
| Lightning | 15 | 13 | 11 | 20 | 5 | 14 | 14 | 10 |

Roll d20; at or above the number the item survives.

- Every magic item gets +2; an item with a plus gets its plus +1 instead (+3 for a +2 item).
- Artifacts and relics save on 2 or 3 in everything, and a failure only puts them out of action for a while, usually 1d6 days.
- A fall checks once per 10 ft dropped, or just once if the landing is soft.
