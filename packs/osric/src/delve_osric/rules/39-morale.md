title: Morale
source: pg
section: "1.6.8"

Morale decides when monsters and NPCs stop fighting. Player characters never check. Fearless creatures (most undead, mindless monsters, trolls and similar) never check either; a monster with morale "N/A" fights on, though an intelligent one may still choose to withdraw.

**Rating.** Use the monster entry's figure; otherwise 50% plus 5% per Hit Die (an 8+1 HD monster: 90%). Hirelings and henchmen use their loyalty, adjusted by their leader's Charisma and the loyalty modifiers (see loyalty).

**When.** The DM calls for a check once it is plain the side is losing, or its losses grow too heavy to bear. No more than two checks per battle; a side that passes the second is fanatical and fights to the death.

**The roll.** d100 with these situational modifiers (all cumulative; a plus makes failure more likely), then `morale_check`: at or under the rating, they keep fighting.

| Situation | Modifier |
|---|---|
| each friend killed, fled or surrendered | +5% |
| own side lost 25% | +5% |
| own side lost 50% | +15% |
| outnumbered | +10% |
| outnumbered 2 to 1 or worse | +20% |
| leader down | +25% |
| each foe killed, fled or surrendered | −5% |
| inflicted 25% losses | −5% |
| inflicted 50% losses | −15% |
| outnumbering the enemy | −10% |

**Failure.** Missing by 25 or less: the creatures try to retreat. Missing by 51 or more: they surrender. Between those, the DM decides. Weigh what the creatures know: a cornered band, or one that knows it can be outrun, gives up rather than running. Any creature that has seen surrender end in execution will never surrender, and fights to the death if it cannot get away.
