---
title: Movement underground and indoors
source: pg
section: "1.5.3.1"
---
A character's movement rate (a base from ancestry, cut by encumbrance and capped by armour) is how far it moves in one combat round, and also how far it moves in one turn of careful exploration underground.

| Pace | Distance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cautious exploring | movement rate per turn | mapping possible |
| Fast, through known ground or by map | 5 × rate per turn | no mapping; only through areas already explored |
| Fleeing / combat | rate per round (10 × per turn) | no mapping; afterwards the party needs a double rest |

So a character with 120 explores 120 ft a turn, travels 600 ft a turn through known halls, and covers 120 ft in a combat round. The party moves at its slowest member's pace.

**Marching order.** The players give the DM a standing order (who is in front, middle and rear), and may give set formations for doors or wide passages. If it is unclear, the DM may decide positions by a roll. In a 10-ft corridor three can walk abreast, fewer if they swing big weapons such as two-handed swords or flails. The second rank can only fight with long weapons such as spears or pole arms, or shoot over a shorter ally in front.

**Pursuit underground.** Move both sides round by round. If pursuers end a round within 10 ft, fight. After five rounds of chase with no gain, monsters have a 50% chance to give up; once the party leaves their senses, they keep going five more rounds and then quit unless they find the party again.

City streets use the same rates.
