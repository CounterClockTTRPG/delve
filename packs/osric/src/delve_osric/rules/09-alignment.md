Alignment is a creature's broad outlook, tied to two pairs of opposed cosmic powers: Law against Chaos, and Good against Evil. Combining the two axes gives nine alignments, from lawful good to chaotic evil, with true neutral at the centre.

- **Law** values order, hierarchy and rules; a lawful character tends to respect legitimate authority.
- **Chaos** values the individual and personal loyalty over general rules; a chaotic character follows people it trusts, not systems.
- **Neutral** on that axis is pragmatic: laws are followed when they serve, broken when they don't, and oaths matter little.
- **Good** cares for others, helps the needy and protects the weak.
- **Evil** serves itself and will harm anyone in the way.

Some flavour notes worth keeping in play: lawful evil makes the ideal henchman for a villain, keeping the letter of the law; chaotic characters often keep their word as personal honour; neutral good obeys the law only where it serves the good; true neutral looks after itself, kin and friends regardless of law or morals.

For most mortals alignment is a choice and can drift with conduct; creatures from other planes, such as demons (chaotic evil), are bound to theirs by nature. Classes may require an alignment (`class_lookup`); a character who strays may lose class powers, as the DM decides.

**Alignment tongue.** Every creature that can speak knows the secret signs, gestures and code words of its alignment, enough to communicate with others of the same alignment. Only an assassin can learn another alignment's tongue.
