title: Conditions

OSRIC 3 gathers the states a creature can be in. The helpless ones (held, asleep, unconscious) have their own entry.

- **Blinded:** if it depends on sight, −4 to hit and on saves, and its AC is 4 worse.
- **Confused:** acts at random, as the druid spell confusion describes.
- **Dead:** only raise dead, resurrection or reincarnation help, and not every ancestry can be raised. Death by poison can still be undone if slow poison, neutralise poison or an antidote comes in time.
- **Deafened:** no rules effect beyond not hearing what others say.
- **Engaged in melee:** within 10 ft of an enemy (see spacing).
- **Ethereal:** invisible, passes through solids, and immune to attacks and spells from the material world unless they reach the ethereal; it cannot attack or cast into the material world either, though it can see it.
- **Grappling:** cannot fend off unarmed attacks or cast spells, and can only use weapons the size of a dagger or smaller (claws and bites work normally).
- **Invisible:** attacked at −4 and only if its general location is known; cannot be attacked from flank or rear; +4 on all saves. Some monsters can sense it (`monster_lookup`).
- **Overborne:** no penalty itself, but the victim cannot get up or steady itself while it lasts (see overbearing).
- **Prone:** cannot attack; attackers get +4; may stand as its action unless overborne.
- **Staggered:** melee and missiles against it +2.
- **Stunned:** cannot act; no shield or Dexterity bonus; attackers +4, but they must still roll to hit.
