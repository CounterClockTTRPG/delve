---
title: Choosing an ancestry
source: pg
section: "1.2"
---
OSRIC says "ancestry" for what older books called race: dwarf, elf, gnome, half-elf, halfling, half-orc or human. `race_lookup` holds each one's adjustments, score ranges, classes, level limits and languages.

**Order of work.** Roll the scores, apply the ancestry's adjustments, then check every score sits inside that ancestry's range. A player may lower a score to qualify; raising one needs the DM's leave. A score over the cap is cut down to it, and the DM may let the spare points go elsewhere.

**Adjustments:** dwarf +1 CON −1 CHA; elf +1 DEX −1 CON; halfling +1 DEX −1 STR; half-orc +1 STR +1 CON −2 CHA; gnome, half-elf and human none. Humans take anything from 3 to 18.

**Languages.** Each demi-human starts with a fixed set of tongues, plus its alignment tongue, whatever its Intelligence. Dwarfs, gnomes and half-orcs can add at most two more, however clever. Elves learn one extra per point of Intelligence above 15; half-elves and halflings one per point above 16.

**Features.** Ancestries bring infravision, movement rates (humans 120 ft, dwarfs 90 ft), saving-throw bonuses (the dwarf and gnome "stalwart" bonus by Constitution against aimed magic items, poison and spells), combat edges against particular foes, and detection talents such as a dwarf's sense for stonework or an elf's knack for secret doors.

**Age.** Starting age depends on class. Crossing an age band adds its modifiers to the scores, but age never pushes a score outside the ancestry's range, except that Wisdom may climb past it.

Only demi-humans multi-class; only humans dual-class.
