---
title: Initiative
source: pg
section: "1.6.1.3"
---
After surprise and declarations, each side rolls a d6. The number is the segment in which that side first acts, so lower is better. Delve rolls it each round in `setup_combat` and `combat_round`.

- **Delaying.** A side may act later than its segment, but every action must happen by segment 6 or it is lost. Segments 7–10 are only for spells and other effects finishing. Holding is not changing one's mind: the declared action simply waits for the right moment.
- **Missiles.** A character with a missile weapon ready adjusts the segment by the Dexterity initiative modifier for missiles; quick hands may shoot before their side, clumsy ones later. It does not apply to melee or spells.
- **Multiple attacks.** Fighters, paladins and rangers with extra attacks strike in fixed segments (usually 1 and 10) regardless of the roll, if they are attacking.
- **Spells** begin in the caster's segment and take effect at the end of the casting time.

**Ties.** When both sides act in the same segment, everything is simultaneous: all attacks land, so two foes can kill each other. The one exception is **weapon speed**: when both combatants use weapons, the lower speed factor strikes first, and if that blow kills, the slower weapon does no harm. Against a creature using claws or teeth, speed factor does not apply.

Outside a tie, effects are immediate: a creature slain in segment 2 does not get its segment-5 attack.

Individual initiative is a workable variant, but group initiative is OSRIC's way.
