---
title: Opening doors
source: pg
section: "1.7.1.6"
---
Most doors simply open when someone says so. In some dungeons many are stuck, locked, barred, spiked, or held by magic such as wizard lock. The DM notes which doors are which, and where the keys are.

- **Stuck:** forcing takes a round per try, using the Strength chance for minor feats (a d6, `ability_lookup`). On a wide door two can try together; either success opens it. Retries cost only time and noise, and the noise may draw wanderers. After even one failure, nobody behind the door can be surprised.
- **Locked:** needs the key, a knock spell, exceptional Strength, a thief's lock picking (a round to a turn per try, typically 1–4 rounds), or breaking it down.
- **Chopping down** a standard door takes a turn at least, is loud enough to call for a wandering monster check at a raised chance, and rules out surprising whatever is behind it.

**Keeping doors open or shut.** Monsters open dungeon doors without trouble unless stopped. Iron spikes wedge a door either way, but a spiked door may still slip; the chance is the DM's, often 20–30%.
