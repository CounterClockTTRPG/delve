---
title: Overbearing results
source: pg
section: "1.6.12"
---
After an unarmed hit to overbear, roll d6. Strength adds to damage, not the roll; temporary damage heals 1 hp a round.

**Modifiers.** Attacker: +1 for Strength 16–18 or 4–8 HD, +2 for exceptional Strength or 9+ HD; size from −4 (tiny) through 0 (medium) and +2 (large) to +6 (huge) and +10 (gargantuan); +2 for moving on four legs. Defender: the same scale reversed (−1 / −2 for Strength or HD, +4 tiny to −10 gargantuan). A four-legged defender cannot be overborne at all.

- **1 or less:** the attacker falls prone; the target stays on its feet. No follow-up.
- **2:** the attacker hits the ground clutching the target's legs and may keep hold: the target loses its Dexterity bonuses and 30 ft of movement, but the attacker counts as grappling and the target does not, so the target can strike it normally (with the bonus against a prone foe). No follow-up.
- **3–4:** the target is staggered and overborne but stays up. The attacker may follow at once with a second move: a melee or brawling attack, or a straight roll on the grappling table with no new to-hit.
- **5–6:** the target is knocked prone and overborne and takes damage equal to the die (1 real, the rest temporary); second move as above.
- **7 or more:** as above, but the target also loses its next round, 2 of the damage is real, and a follow-up grappling roll gets +2.

Being overborne ends when the victim is larger than the attacker and no other enemy is in reach (it lasts only that round), when a grapple result lets it rise, or when the attacker dies, is knocked out, is itself overborne or leaves melee range.
