---
title: Magic items and cursed items
source: gmg
section: "2.13.1"
---
Random magic needs the DM's judgement: lucky rolls should not lift the party far beyond its level. Where a loot class calls for "any" magic item, roll d20 for the kind: 1–3 armour or shield, 4–6 miscellaneous magic, 7–9 miscellaneous weapon, 10–13 potion, 14 ring, 15 rod, staff or wand, 16–18 scroll, 19–20 sword. Then roll on that kind's tables; `item_lookup` has the items themselves.

**Armour and shields** roll a type with a base value, then a bonus on d20: +1 (1–10), +2 (11–15), +3 (16), +4 or +5 (17), cursed (18), special (19–20). The plus multiplies the base value for sale. Kept items earn a tenth of that value in XP (see experience).

**Identifying.** The guides give no general procedure. Potions can be sampled (see potions); scrolls and tubes need read magic; spells such as detect magic and identify help, and sages or hired casters in town can be paid for it. Otherwise the party learns by use. The DM decides how much a test reveals.

**Intelligent swords** have an alignment, languages and powers and may argue with their wielder; see the sword tables.

**Cursed items** are seldom instant death. Most impose a drawback, from nuisance to real burden, and many mix a benefit with the curse, forcing a hard choice.

- A cursed weapon usually has a to-hit penalty (−1 or −2), and once grasped it cannot be put down until the curse is lifted.
- Cursed armour usually worsens AC and cannot be taken off until the curse is removed.
- Other curses can be rolled on d100 on the common curses table, or taken from the named cursed items.

Remove curse (or a wish) ends most curses. Artifacts are beyond these rules: unique, dangerous, and the DM's to design.
