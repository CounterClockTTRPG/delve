---
title: Turning undead
source: pg
section: "1.6.5"
---
Clerics present a holy symbol to turn undead and lesser fiends (from 3rd level a paladin can too, as a cleric two levels lower). Good and neutral clerics drive them off or destroy them; evil clerics pacify or command them. `turning_undead` reads the table by cleric level and the undead's Type (1 skeleton to 13 fiend).

**Procedure.**

- Turning is the round's action and takes effect in the cleric's segment, like a one-segment spell, but cannot be disrupted. One d20 roll covers it.
- Range 100 ft in a 180° arc ahead, against undead that directly perceive the symbol; a wall blocks it, even for incorporeal undead.
- Once a day per group in the area: a failure cannot be retried against them that encounter, though undead outside the arc can be tried separately.

**Results** for each type present:

- **—:** no chance.
- **Number:** roll that or more to turn 2d6 (1d2 fiends or paladins). They flee as fast and far as they can for 3d4 rounds, fighting only what blocks their way; attacking them ends it.
- **T:** 2d6 turned automatically.
- **D:** 2d6 destroyed (good or neutral cleric). **D\*:** 1d6+6 destroyed.

**Evil clerics** roll d100 for each affected group instead: 01–60 they are lastingly non-hostile, 61–00 they obey. The cleric can command no more undead than its Charisma's follower limit. Evil clerics can turn paladins (Type 8 at 1st–2nd level, one Type higher per two levels, 13 from 11th), but never destroy or control them.

Fiends of more than 10 HD or 65% magic resistance are immune. A turned creature cannot be turned again until it recovers. Destroying undead by turning earns no XP; bypassing them may, if the DM counts it as a defeat.
