title: Melee attacks

A melee attack is made with a weapon held in hand: sword, mace, halberd, dagger. Bows, crossbows and thrown weapons are missile attacks.

**The roll.** Roll a d20, add bonuses and subtract penalties. If the total meets or beats the number the attacker needs against the target's Armour Class, it hits; roll the weapon's damage and take it from the target's hit points. The needed number comes from the attacker's class table by level (`class_lookup`), or the monster table by Hit Dice; Delve stores it on the character as to-hit. Groups using ascending AC may use the Base To-Hit Bonus formula instead (see Armour Class).

**Common modifiers:** Strength to-hit and damage; magic weapon bonuses; specialisation; ancestry edges (a dwarf's +1 against orcs and goblin-kin); charging +2; attacking from behind +2; and the condition modifiers (prone, stunned, held, invisible).

**Reach and targets.**

- Every melee attack reaches 10 ft, whatever the weapon.
- You cannot strike through an ally's square unless you have a reach weapon, and never through an enemy's square to a foe beyond, even with one.
- Where each figure is one creature, the attacker picks the target; in a mass melee where one figure stands for many, the victim is random.

**Example of the arithmetic.** A 5th-level fighter needs 10 against AC 6 [14]. With +1 for Strength and +1 for an ancestral grudge, a roll of 9 becomes 11 and hits. A light warhammer does 1d4+1, plus 1 for Strength.

**Two weapons.** The off hand may hold only a dagger or hand axe. The main weapon attacks at −2, the off-hand at −4; the missile Dexterity adjustment applies to both but can only reduce penalties, never become a bonus. The off-hand weapon cannot parry.
