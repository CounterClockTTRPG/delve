title: Money and starting equipment

Prices in OSRIC are far above real medieval ones: gold is common in the game world, so treasure can come in heaps without wrecking the economy. The gold piece is the unit of account.

**Coins:** 1 pp = 5 gp; 1 gp = 2 ep = 10 sp = 100 cp. Every coin weighs the same, a tenth of a pound (ten to the pound).

**Starting gold by class:**

- fighter, paladin, ranger: 5d4 × 10 gp
- cleric, druid: 3d6 × 10 gp
- thief, assassin: 2d6 × 10 gp
- magic-user, illusionist: 2d4 × 10 gp
- monk: 5d4 gp (not multiplied)

Each character also starts with ordinary clothes, and a magic-user or illusionist with a spellbook. Prices, weights and weapon statistics are in `item_lookup`.

**Weights.** A weight of "—" means the item does not count (very light things, worn clothing). "N/A" means it has weight but is not normally carried (a horse). A figure like 0 (5) means the item weighs nothing while worn but its bracketed weight when carried as a spare.

**Weapons on the table** carry a speed factor (used to break ties, see initiative), the side-to-side space needed to swing them in a line, a damage type (blunt, piercing, slashing) and damage against small-or-medium and against large foes.

**Armour** sets Armour Class and a movement cap; bought armour includes a helmet. Magic armour worn does not count towards encumbrance, and raises the movement cap by 30 ft to at most 120 ft.
