---
title: Dual-classing
source: pg
section: "1.3.12"
---
Only humans dual-class. A dual-classed character leaves one class and starts over in a second, advancing in one at a time.

**Requirements.** At least 15 in each prime requisite of the class being left, and at least 17 in each prime requisite of the new class. Any pair of classes is allowed if the scores are there; a paladin turning ranger would need 17 Strength, Intelligence and Wisdom.

**Starting over.** The character keeps the hit points already earned but is otherwise a 1st-level member of the new class. All experience goes to the new class, and no hit points are gained for levels in it until its level passes the old class's level.

**Using the old class too early.** Until the new class's level exceeds the old one, using the old class's abilities forfeits all experience from that adventure. It is an emergency measure.

**Once past.** When the new class is higher, the character rolls hit points as the new class and may use both classes' abilities freely, but armour still limits them: no arcane spells in armour, no druid spells in armour a druid may not wear, no thief abilities except reading languages in armour a thief may not wear, and no monk abilities in armour at all (monkish thief skills follow the thief's armour limits).
