---
title: Charging, closing and setting against a charge
source: pg
section: "1.6.2.1"
---
**Charging** lets an attacker move and attack in one round.

- The charger moves at double its movement rate and must end within 10 ft of the target.
- If the defender's weapon is longer, the defender strikes first, unless surprised or already done for the round. Against that blow the charger loses its Dexterity AC bonus, and a charger with no bonus is 1 AC worse.
- A charger who survives gets +2 to hit. The attack may be melee, a thrown weapon, or unarmed. A defender who declared missile fire may shoot.
- One charge per turn (every ten rounds). A character in the heaviest encumbrance band cannot charge on foot.
- A lance from a warhorse or other battle-trained mount doubles the damage dice on the charge round; Strength, magic and specialisation bonuses are not doubled.

**Closing** is the careful advance into melee: up to the full movement rate, but no attack that round except a thrown weapon. In exchange, the foe being closed on cannot attack the closer that round either, even if it hasn't acted. Entering an enemy's 10-ft melee range ends the mover's movement.

**Setting a weapon against a charge.** Spears, javelins, lances on foot, pole arms and tridents can be braced against the ground. The weapon is set in segment 1 of the round it is declared. Its holder does nothing unless someone charges; then it strikes before the charger, and a hit does double damage. Any move-and-attack counts as a charge, including the leaps of pouncing monsters.
