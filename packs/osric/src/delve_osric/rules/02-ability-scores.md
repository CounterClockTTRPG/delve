---
title: Ability scores
source: pg
section: "1.1.1"
---
Every character has six abilities: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma, each from 3 to 18 at creation before ancestry and age change them. The group, not each player, picks one method for everyone:

- **Hardest:** 3d6 per ability, assigned in order.
- **Difficult:** 3d6 six times, the player arranges the results.
- **Normal:** 4d6 per ability, drop the lowest die, in order.
- **Flexible:** 4d6 drop the lowest, arranged as the player likes.

What the scores do (the numbers are in `ability_lookup`):

- **Strength** changes melee and thrown to-hit and damage (not launched missiles), sets the weight carried before encumbrance, and gives the chance for minor feats (forcing a stuck door, on a d6) and major ones (bending bars, on d100). A fighter, paladin or ranger with 18 rolls d100 for exceptional Strength; 00 makes it 19.
- **Dexterity** shifts surprise, missile to-hit, missile initiative, Armour Class, and saves against aimed magic items and area spells such as fireball (and others where agility clearly matters, as the DM decides).
- **Constitution** adjusts hit points per die, and gives the chance to survive system shock and being raised. Its starting score is how many times the character can ever be brought back; each return costs a point.
- **Intelligence** governs languages and a magic-user's spell learning. 19 or more makes the character immune to first-level illusions. It never solves a puzzle for the player.
- **Wisdom** gives a bonus or penalty on saves against charm, fear and similar mind magic.
- **Charisma** sets the henchman limit, followers' loyalty, and the reaction adjustment.
