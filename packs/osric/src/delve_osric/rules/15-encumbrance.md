---
title: Encumbrance
source: pg
section: "1.5.3.3"
---
Encumbrance is the total weight a character carries, in pounds; coins weigh a tenth of a pound each. Strength gives an allowance (`ability_lookup`) that can be carried freely. Weight beyond it slows the character and spoils its chance to catch others unawares:

| Weight over the allowance | Top speed | Surprise |
|---|---|---|
| none | full | +1, but only in armour lighter than chain |
| up to 40 lb | three-quarters | usual bonuses |
| 41–80 lb | half | bonuses lost, penalties kept |
| 81–120 lb | quarter | bonuses lost, penalties kept, a further −1 |
| over 120 lb | cannot move | — |

Armour adds its weight and also sets a hard **movement cap** of its own, whatever the load: plate, scale and splint 60 ft; chain, banded, ring, padded and studded leather 90 ft; leather and elfin mail 120 ft. Worn magic armour weighs nothing for this purpose and its cap is 30 ft higher (never over 120 ft).

The character moves at the lower of the encumbrance speed and the armour cap. A character at the heaviest category cannot charge unless mounted on an animal that is itself below that category.

Treasure is heavy: remind the party to leave room for what they hope to carry out, and share the counting with the players rather than doing it all as DM.
