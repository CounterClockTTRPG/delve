---
title: Reading a spell entry
source: pg
section: "1.8.1.5"
---
`spell_lookup` gives each spell's entry. The parts mean:

- **Kind and school.** The first word says who casts it: arcane (magic-users), divine (clerics), druidic (druids) or phantasmal (illusionists). Same-named spells of different kinds can differ, so look up the right one. The school (abjuration, conjuration/summoning, divination, enchantment, evocation, illusion, necromancy, transmutation) only describes the effect; it carries no rules.
- **Range** is the furthest the centre of the effect can be put. "Caster" means it starts on and moves with the caster; "Nil" means it starts where the caster stands and stays there; "Touch" means the caster must touch the subject. An unwilling subject can dodge: the caster then rolls to hit as with a weapon, and may keep trying in later rounds as long as no other spell is cast. The slot is spent either way.
- **Duration.** "Permanent" lasts until dispelled; "instantaneous" happens once and leaves nothing to dispel (fireball damage, cure light wounds healing). "Per level" means the caster's level in that casting class.
- **Area of effect** may be fixed in place or move with a creature or object. Where the entry offers several, the caster chooses at casting. On the grid, any square the area touches is affected.
- **Components.** V: spoken aloud, impossible when silenced or gagged. S: gestures needing at least one free hand. M: a material object, chosen by the DM unless a cost is given; it may be consumed.
- **Casting time** and how it resolves: see casting time.
- **Saving throw:** whether one applies and what success does.
