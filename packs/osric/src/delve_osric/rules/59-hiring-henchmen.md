---
title: The henchman's offer and associates
source: gmg
section: "2.2.3"
---
**Making the offer.** Once a candidate is chosen, the character makes an offer and the DM adds up the chance of acceptance:

- the minimum is 100 gp per level of the henchman, which gives 30%;
- +1% for every 10 gp more, up to 60% from money;
- +15% for useful magic items offered (magic ammunition counts only in sixes);
- −25% if free room and board is not part of it;
- plus the character's Charisma adjustment.

Roll d100; at or under the chance, the NPC signs on. Henchmen turn up with nothing but their clothes, so the offer must include full equipment for their class. They expect a half share of each expedition's treasure, a fair word on magic items, and healing and restoration when needed. The character should explain the duties plainly.

**Exceptional henchmen.** Sometimes a character holds sway over a higher-level NPC. If the NPC is more than two levels above the character, it will serve only as an associate for one or two adventures, never longer. If the character is more than two levels above it, an offer is made in the normal way. In between, the NPC agrees to be an associate for 1d4 weeks or adventures. Service agreed under threat makes for very doubtful loyalty.

**Associates** see themselves as the party's equals or betters, whether coerced, hired or come for their own reasons. They expect to share the risks fairly and take a full share of treasure and magic, and rarely stay more than an adventure or two.

Once hired, loyalty governs how a henchman behaves under strain (see loyalty).
