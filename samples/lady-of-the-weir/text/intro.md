# The Lady of the Weir

*A notice nailed to the post by the door of the Drowned Toe, in Wetside, Hask, early in Isaine, 1188 RY:*

> **WANTED.** Stout folk to set right a TROUBLE at Ambresleigh on the Ambre, a day's walk upriver. Three of ours lost
> on the river path. The eels dying. The Lady's pool gone black. **Fifty in gold** to them as ends it, and bed and
> board while they try. Ask for the Reeve, Haldan, at the hall with the tally-sticks. *Come before the moon is dark.*

Ambresleigh is an eel-fishing village under a small god, the Lady of the Weir, who is said to keep its river kind in
return for a dish of milk and a silver penny at every new moon. Nobody in the Drowned Toe has been there. Everybody
has an opinion: that the Tesks are behind it, that the Ferrymen's Guild is, that small gods are demons and should be
burned. The road is good, the weather is turning to spring, and the Reeve is paying.
