# Weirholt

[[npc:odo-tesk]] lives here with his two men-at-arms, [[npc:garm-and-totty]], and a cook-housekeeper, old Brannagh,
who sees everything and says nothing unless asked about her rheumatism. By day the men are at the pumps by the weir
and Odo is in his study; after dark everyone is inside with the gate barred.

**Supper.** Odo invites the party to dine the first evening they are in the valley (a man brings the message to the
Reeve's door, which Haldan hates). Odo is charming, curious and greedy, and he wants to hire them: he will pay 100 gp
to whoever clears "whatever is squatting in my sluice-house" and lets him dig there in peace. He denies poisoning
anything (true), admits pumping the pool (true), and says his men "found nothing but old stones" in the Sluice-House
(a lie: they pried a sword out of the floor and ran, see [[secret:odo-pried-the-blade]]). A party that keeps him
talking over the second bottle can learn it with a good Charisma check; a party that snoops while he talks can reach
the study.

**Odo and the seal.** The moment he hears the Sluice-House is clear, or sees the party come out of it alive, Odo goes
down with Garm, Totty and crowbars to pry up the bronze disc in the seal chamber ([S5](text:sluice-key#s5)): it is the
biggest piece of Thaumarch bronze in the valley. Prying it up while Ostric still stands frees him for good; doing it
after the Lady is restored offends her. Stopping him without killing him is [[quest:odo-in-check]].

## W1. The Gate {#w1}

> A squat gate-tower in the wall, with a barred oak gate, a brazier, and a murder-hole in the vault overhead that
> nobody has used in a century.

By day the gate stands open and whoever is in the yard sees who comes. By night it is barred, and Garm or Totty
answers a knock through the murder-hole with a spear ready. They open to the Reeve, to anyone Odo has invited, and
to nobody who won't show their face in the brazier-light. The bar is a beam a strong man can lift alone; the lock on
the wicket is old and easy for a thief.

## W2. The Yard {#w2}

> Beaten earth, a well, a cart with a broken axle and a stack of pump-timbers under a tarpaulin.

The yard is overlooked by every window of the house: anyone crossing it unseen at night needs to move silently or
hide in shadows. The pump-timbers are the same timbers that shore Odo's tunnel in the cellar ([W7](text:weirholt-key#w7)).

## W3. The Hall {#w3}

> A long room with a fire at one end and a good table down the middle, laid for more guests than Weirholt ever gets.
> A painted map of the Ambre from Hask to the weir hangs over the fire.

Where Odo dines the party. The painted map is old and good: it shows the Sluice-House as a long building on the bank,
"the Warden's House", and the old name of the weir, "Ostric's Dam". The stair goes up to Odo's bedchamber and the
guest rooms (empty, dusty, nothing of note).

## W4. The Study {#w4}

> Odo's room: a writing desk, a strongbox bolted to the floor, and shelves of account books and curiosities.

The door is locked when Odo isn't in.

**The desk** (locked; a thief opens it, or a knife and a turn of noisy work) holds Odo's letters to his mother at
House Tesk, asking for money and promising "Thaumarch bronze by the cartload by midsummer"; the lease of the weir,
which gives him the right to "the water, the fish and the works of the old Warden"; and a sketch of the Sluice-House
rooms, roughly right as far as the valve gallery ([S2](text:sluice-key#s2)), with "do NOT turn the big wheel" written
across it.

**The strongbox** is locked and trapped: a needle in the lock pricks anyone who works it without finding the trap
first (a thief's find-traps roll); save versus poison or sleep for 2d4 turns. Inside:

- 120 gp and 300 sp in canvas bags;
- a scroll of *sleep*, which a magic-user can cast once or copy into a spellbook;
- **Sorrel's ring**, a plain silver band with a heron cut on it, wrapped in a rag with "found in the bank, S-House,
  2nd day" written on the rag ([[quest:sorrel-at-rest]]);
- a silver seal-ring of House Tesk (Odo won't forgive its loss).

## W5. The Kitchen {#w5}

> A big hearth, hams in the smoke, a scrubbed table and a trapdoor to the cellar by the far wall.

Brannagh's domain. She will tell a polite visitor that the men came back from the Sluice-House a week ago white and
shaking, and that Totty has been sick every night since; and that Master Odo keeps "a dead man's ring" in his box.

## W6. The Guard Room {#w6}

> Two cots, a dice-cup, a rack of spears and the smell of men who sleep in their boots.

Garm and Totty sleep here. Totty, sick and frightened, will tell anyone who promises to take him to Hask what they
found in the Sluice-House: "a sword in the floor, in a ring of writing, and the master said pull it out. And the floor
breathed." Garm tells him to shut up.

## W7. The Cellar {#w7}

> Barrels, a smell of earth, and a black hole in the east wall where a tunnel has been started.

Odo's tunnel runs thirty feet towards the weir and stops in a fall of clay. He started it to reach the Sluice-House
dry before his men found the arch; it goes nowhere now, but anyone who looks can tell which way it was heading. The
barrels are small beer and salt eels.
