Old Tallow is the small god of one street: [[Tallow Lane]] in [[Hask]], the candle-makers' street. He is fat, greedy
and kindly, the god of small light: the candle in a window, the rush-light in a sickroom, the lamp left burning for
someone late home. His power ends at both ends of the lane, and he seems quite content with that.

## What he is like

People on the lane talk of him as a neighbour, not a god. Old Tallow likes his fat; Old Tallow is in a sulk today;
Old Tallow ate the Wick-Wife's best beeswax again. He has never been seen, but on cold nights the candle-flames of
the whole lane lean the same way at once, as if something large had sighed.

His shrine is at the top of the lane, a heap of melted wax as tall as a man, four hundred years of offerings melted
together, with a wick in the top that has never been let go out.

## What he wants

Fat. Wax. Tallow, especially good mutton tallow, and the ends of candles. Every house on the lane keeps a stub burning
in a niche by its door for him. In return:

- No house on Tallow Lane burns. Fires go out on their own.
- A candle lit on the lane burns longer than it should.
- Anyone who lights a stub at a door niche and asks for shelter will be taken in for a night.

## What he can't abide

Darkness on purpose: a candle blown out, a shutter closed on a lit window to hide something. People who do that on
the lane find their own candles won't light for a week. And thieves who rob a house that sheltered them.

::: dm
Old Tallow knows everything said in every candlelit room on the lane, which is every room. He trades this for fat,
to anyone who brings enough; the Wick-Wife, Ada Fenn, the Chandlers' guildmaster, sells it on to the [[Quiet Hand]].

He is greedy enough to be dangerous. Feed him something better than tallow, and he can be talked into almost
anything, including letting a fire burn. So has the [[Open Mouth]].

**Hooks.**
- Old Tallow is sick: candles on the lane gutter blue, and last week a house burned. Someone has been leaving him
  fat rendered from something that wasn't an animal.
- A crew needs to know what a guildmaster said in a lodging on the lane on a certain night. Old Tallow will tell them
  for a cartload of tallow, or for one stick of the pale Thaumarch wax that crews sometimes find in the
  [[Undercroft]].
:::
