On the downs of the [[Kingdom of Calder]], half a day's walk from the nearest village, there is a long grassy barrow
that sheep will not graze. It is one of the [[Small Gods]], and it answers one question a year. The price is a secret:
one the asker has never told anyone, and must never tell again.

Nobody knows who lies in the barrow. The local shepherds say that does not matter; what matters is that the hill
listens, and that it is never wrong.

## The Asking

Once a year, at the first full moon after [[Harvest Home]], people come to the hill. They lie face down on the turf,
mouth to the grass, and whisper their secret. Then they ask. Most hear nothing and go home. One, chosen by the hill,
hears the answer that night in a voice from under the ground, or finds it the next morning written in chalk on the
turf. The answer is true and short, usually one or two words.

Smaller things the hill does all year, for smaller prices:
- It keeps what you tell it. A shepherd who whispers a worry to the turf feels lighter, and the worry does not spread.
- It hears everything said on its slopes, and sometimes repeats it to someone else at the next Asking.

## Customs and taboos

- No digging on the hill, ever. A baron's men tried in 912; their names are still repeated at the Asking, which is
  worse than being cursed.
- No lies spoken on its slopes. The shepherds talk very little there.
- The local [[Gnomes]] keep the path to it, cut the chalk ear on its side fresh each spring, and act as its unofficial
  priests. They are paid in gossip.
- Saying: "Tell it to the hill," meaning "keep it to yourself."

## Its priests

The hill has no clerics in the usual sense. The gnome keepers of the path have small gifts while on the downs: they can
hear a whisper across a valley and know a lie when it is spoken. A day's walk away, the gifts fade.

::: dm
The secrets given to the hill are not gone. The hill hoards them, and anyone who could make it talk would learn the
private shames of half the barons of Calder, and of two kings. Barons who wait for [[Bertil III]] to die have come to
the Asking every year for a decade.

The [[Sunward Church]] has declared the hill a demon and means to burn its grass and dig it open. If they do, they will
find a Thaumarch vault at its heart, and nothing that listens.

Hooks:
- A baron asks the party to carry his secret to the hill and his question with it, because he cannot be seen there.
  To do it, they must learn the secret.
- A gnome keeper comes to Hask in a panic: the hill gave this year's answer to the wrong person, a Sunward preacher.
:::
