For fifteen hundred years before the [[Unmaking]], every human land of the Reaches was ruled by the **Thaumarchy**,
an empire of sorcerer-princes. Its rulers, the **Thaumarchs**, belonged to **Seven Houses** and governed from
[[Ishavel]], the Lantern of the World, in the east-centre of the Reaches, where the [[Glass Waste]] lies now.

## What it was like

Magic was ordinary then. Streets were lit without oil. Towers rose past the clouds. Roads were laid that did not wear,
and some of them still run arrow-straight across the Reaches, cracked and weed-split but harder than any stone a
mason can cut today. Brass servants (see [[Brass Servants]]) carried water, fought wars and swept floors. A Thaumarch
could cross the empire in a day and live three hundred years.

Ordinary people were subjects, not citizens. They farmed, paid, and kept out of the way. The Thaumarchs were not cruel
by habit, but they did not much notice anyone who could not work a spell. Old songs in [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]]
still call them "the ones who looked over our heads".

## Who kept apart

- [[Elves]] refused the empire and kept to their forests. They remember it, and do not explain why they refused.
- [[Dwarves]] traded with it, sold it iron and stone, and despised it. Dwarven grudges against particular Houses are
  still recited at [[Deephorn]].

## The Seven Houses

Each House held a province and a gift of magic, and they competed ceaselessly for the favour of the throne. Their
descendants, the Thaumarch kin, survived the fall in the south and became the [[Lantern Principalities]], whose
princes still trace their lines to the Houses: [[Corvanne]], [[Tessarine]] and [[Amaranthe]] are the strongest of the
seven today.

## What is left

Towers that fell all at once; the [[Pale Tower]] that did not; the roads; buried vaults; script that the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] pays for by the page; the [[Seven Lanterns]] somewhere; and, in the Waste, the glass that was Ishavel.

::: dm
The Thaumarchy's magic drew on the gods more than its people knew. In its last centuries the Thaumarchs had begun to
treat prayer as a cost to be cut, which led straight to the [[Great Binding]].

- The old roads still answer to Thaumarch blood: a Lanterner who walks one at night may see the lamps that once lined
  it, and things walking that should not be.
- A Thaumarch vault opens to a spoken House name. Lanterner nobles know them; they do not share.
- At least one Thaumarch did not die at the Unmaking but stepped somewhere else. Their tower may still have a door.
:::
