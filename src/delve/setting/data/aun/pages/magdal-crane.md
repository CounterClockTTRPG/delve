Magdal Crane is the inquisitor of the [[Sunward Church]] in [[Hask]], and head of the House of the Scale, the Sunward
mission beside [[Aurel]]'s great temple in [[Templegate]]. She has been in Hask for six years. In that time she has
burned no one, arrested almost no one and raised her voice to no one, and the whole city is afraid of her.

## The woman

Crane is a tall, spare woman in her fifties, born in Calder and schooled in [[Candor]]. She wears the plain white of
her order with a small golden scale at the throat, and grey gloves she never takes off. She is patient, polite and
thorough. She listens more than she talks. She remembers everything, and writes most of it down.

She walks the [[Lamp Quarter]] every morning, buying nothing. She attends every Council session, sitting in the
gallery. She visits the sick in Wetside, and pays for their physicians, and asks them, kindly, which gods they pray
to.

## What she wants

Crane wants Hask's small gods gone: the [[Ferryman]]'s niche, [[Old Tallow]]'s shrine, [[Mother Gutter]]'s gratings,
all of it, burned as the Church burns demons in the [[Sunward Theocracy]]. She wants the [[Collegium of the Lamp]]
closed and its wizards tried. She wants the [[Open Mouth]] dug out. In that order, the city says, though she says the
opposite.

She knows she can't have any of it yet. Hask's law protects its shrines, the Council protects the Collegium, and the
city would riot if she touched the Ferryman. So she waits, and keeps lists.

## Dealing with her

Crane hires crews: to find cultists, to report on wizards, to recover "heretical" finds from the [[Undercroft]] before
the Collegium buys them. She pays in gold from Candor and in the Church's goodwill, which can be worth more. She never
threatens. She only explains, gently, what she knows.

::: dm
Crane's lists include three guildmasters seen praying at the Ferryman's niche on Aurel's holy days, the names of two
unlicensed hedge-wizards in Wetside, and a guess (close to right) about who the [[Widow]] is. The Hierarch,
[[Anselm IX]], has written to her that he wants Hask "made clean" before he goes to war; she has a candidate for
Lord Warden, the Draper guildmaster, and gold to spend on Ferry Night.

She genuinely hates the Open Mouth, and will work with anyone, even a wizard or a beggar of Mother Gutter, to destroy
it. That is the one door into her trust.

**Hooks.**
- Crane asks the crew to deliver a sealed letter to the Ferrymaster. It offers the guild Church protection if it will
  stop paying the tithe. If the crew reads it, she will know.
- She offers a large reward for the Tongue of the Open Mouth, alive. She means it, and she'll ask the crew how they
  found him.
