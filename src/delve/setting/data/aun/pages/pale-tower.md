A long day's walk up the towpath from [[Wetside]], past the [[Lady of the Weir]]'s water, the Pale Tower stands on a
bluff above the west bank of the [[Ambre]]. It is a Thaumarch tower, a single smooth shaft without a seam or a window
for its first hundred feet, whiter than bone and leaning toward the river at an angle that makes visitors' stomachs
turn. It was taller once: the top third lies in the river below, a white island the ferrymen call the Knuckle. What
remains is still the tallest thing for fifty miles.

Nobody has ever got in through the walls. The way in is down: under the tower lies the [[Undercroft]], the great
dungeon of Hask, and the tower is the lid on it.

## Leantown

At the tower's foot, where the bluff falls to the river, a shanty town has grown in its shadow: Leantown, a few
hundred souls who live off the people who go down. It has:

- **The Lean Gate**, a Thaumarch archway half sunk in the bluff, where the stair into the Undercroft begins. The
  Lord Warden keeps a tollkeeper here and a dozen bored Watchmen. Going down is free. Coming up costs a tenth of what
  you carry, in coin or in kind, assessed by the tollkeeper's eye, which is generous to friends.
- **Rope-sellers, lamp-sellers, chalk-sellers**, a forge that will mend a sword overnight, a priest of [[Velis]] who
  buries what comes up dead, and a woman who will sell you a map of the first level that is mostly right.
- **The Leaning Post**, a beer-tent on a barge, where crews that came up alive drink until they can sleep.

## Things people say about the tower

- It leans a finger's width further every hundred years. When it falls, it will fall on Hask, a day downriver. (It
  can't. People say it anyway.)
- On clear nights a light shows at the broken top, though there is no way up.
- Birds don't nest on it. Rain doesn't wet it.
- It was built by the Thaumarch House that kept the river, and it was the last of the Seven Houses' towers to
  stand. The Collegium will tell you this is not proven.

::: dm
The light at the top is real: it is the same light as the Last Lamp in the [[Lamp Quarter]], drawn up from the
deepest levels, where one of the [[Seven Lanterns]] lies in the vault it was built for. The tower is a chimney for its
glow, and the Undercroft is the binding-works of one House of the [[Thaumarchy]], abandoned on the night of the
[[Unmaking]].

The tollkeeper, Wendel Pask, takes his tenth honestly when watched and takes a second tenth for House Orlemont when
not. He also tells the [[Ember Court]]'s Leantown man which crews came up heavy.

**Hooks.**
- The Knuckle, the fallen top in the river, has a door in it that shows only at the lowest water of late summer. A
  Riverfolk fisher has seen it open.
- A crew has come up with a [[Brass Servants|brass servant]]'s head, and the head is talking. The tollkeeper has
  claimed it as the city's tenth; three buyers are already on the towpath.
:::
