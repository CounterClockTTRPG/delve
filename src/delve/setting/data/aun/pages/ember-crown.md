The **Ember Crown** is the relic of the Undying Margrave, [[Teodric Vael]]. In 887 RY Vael, then a march-lord of the
[[Kingdom of Calder]], took it from a Thaumarch tomb on the edge of the [[Glass Waste]]. He put it on. A year later he
broke from Calder and founded the [[Margravate]]. He has not aged a day since, and he is now about three hundred and
thirty years old.

## What it looks like

A narrow circlet of dark red-gold, unadorned except for a single stone at the brow that glows like a coal in a dark
room. Those who have stood near the Margrave say the air around him is warm, and that he smells faintly of a forge.
Portraits of him from 900 RY and from 1180 RY show the same face.

## What people say

- That it keeps him alive. This much is plainly true.
- That he cannot take it off. He has never been seen without it, sleeping, bathing or at table.
- That it was made by the Thaumarchs for a queen of the House that lies in the tomb, and that she died anyway.
- That its stone is a drop of the fire that burned [[Ishavel]].
- In the [[Sunward Theocracy]], that it is a demon's eye, and that the Margrave is long dead and the crown wears him.

## At the table

The Crown is the reason the Margravate exists and the reason it is feared. Anyone who brings news of it, a drawing of
it, or a fragment of the tomb it came from will find the [[Ember Court]] interested, and the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] more so.

::: dm
The Ember Crown holds the trapped spark of a lesser power, caught with [[Great Binding]] craft in the last years of the
[[Thaumarchy]]. It keeps its wearer alive by burning that spark, and it is burning out. It is also eating the Margrave
from within: under his clothes his skin is crazed like old glaze, and he feels very little any more.

- Vael knows he has perhaps a few years. The diggers in the Glass Waste are looking for a fresh spark, or a
  [[Seven Lanterns|Lantern]] to feed the Crown with a god.
- The power in the Crown is aware. If it is freed it will remember who held it; if it is freed in Hask, it will want
  a place of its own, and may make itself a [[Small Gods|small god]] there.
- The tomb Vael robbed is still on the edge of the Waste. It had two niches. Only one was empty when he found it.
:::
