Mother Gutter is the small god of [[Hask]]'s sewers and gutters: the drains, the culverts, the gratings, and the old
Thaumarch tunnels below them, which are far bigger than a city like Hask needs. She is the god of everyone who has
fallen through the bottom of the city: beggars, runaway apprentices, rat-catchers, lost children, the drunk asleep
in the gutter.

## Her people

Her worshippers are Hask's beggars, who call themselves **the Gutter's Children** whatever their age. They hold their
pitches by rights older than the guilds, sit on the gratings and keep a watch nobody else in the city notices. If you
want to know who went down which alley at midnight, ask a beggar and put a toe in the cup.

Rat-catchers honour her too: she gives them the rats, and they give her the first of every catch. In Wetside every
grating has a few crumbs on it, left for her, and stealing them is the one theft the [[Quiet Hand]] punishes for
free.

## What she does

- **Finds the lost.** A child lost in Hask is often found sitting on a grating, dry and fed, with no idea how they got
  there. Mothers in Wetside leave a crust at the nearest drain and pray.
- **Shelters the poor.** In the worst winters the beggars go below, to dry Thaumarch galleries under the city that
  nobody else can find.
- **Keeps the dark.** She hates the [[Open Mouth]], the cult of [[Nhul]] that lurks in the city's underside, and her
  Children hunt it. A beggar who gives you a warning about the sewers means it.

Nobody sees her. Some beggars say she is a great grey woman with rats in her hair; some say she is the drains
themselves, and the sound of running water at night is her talking.

## Bargaining with her

Mother Gutter will help anyone who helps her Children. Bread for a beggar, a lost child carried home, a rat-catcher
paid fairly: she remembers. She pays in secrets, guidance through the drains, and the occasional thing the city has
dropped.

::: dm
Mother Gutter knows every pipe and tunnel under Hask, including the old drains that join the Old Cut to the upper
levels of the [[Undercroft]]. She is losing a war: the Open Mouth has been taking her Children, a few a month, for
two years, and she is weakening as they go. The beggars' queen, **Nan Ditch**, a one-eyed old woman who sits on the
grating outside the [[Drowned Toe]], is looking for a crew to go down and stop it.

**Hooks.**
- Nan Ditch offers the crew a way into the Undercroft that avoids the Lean Gate and the tollkeeper, if they will
  first clear a nest of something in the drains below [[Wetside]].
- A beggar boy who was taken by the Open Mouth has come back, and he won't stop smiling.
:::
