The **Sun's Judgement** falls on 15 Aurelmas, midsummer, the longest day of the year. It is [[Aurel]]'s great day,
and the [[Sunward Church]] marks it with courts.

## The courts

At dawn every Sunward temple opens a court in its square. A priest sits under a golden scale and hears cases until
sunset: disputes of debt, broken oaths, quarrels over land, and accusations of heresy. A verdict given on the day is
held to be Aurel's own and cannot be appealed. People save up their lawsuits for months to bring them on the Sun's
Judgement, so courts are crowded and a case may get a few minutes.

At sunset, the year's heretics are sentenced. In the [[Sunward Theocracy]] sentences are carried out on the same
evening, by fire. Elsewhere the Church can only pronounce them, which it does at length and in public.

## Around the Reaches

- In [[Candor]] the Hierarch himself sits in judgement, and the crowd in the great square is ten thousand strong.
- In [[Hask]] the House of the Scale holds its court in [[Templegate]]. Its verdicts bind nobody under Hask law, but
  merchants who trade south take them seriously. The Ford Council sends an observer and a polite note.
- In [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]] most lords hold their own manor courts on the same day, out of rivalry.
- Ordinary folk light bonfires on the hills at sunset and jump them for luck; the Church disapproves and does it
  anyway.

## At the table

Midsummer is the time to sue someone, to swear an oath that must hold, or to keep away from the inquisitors. An oath
sworn before a Sunward priest on the Sun's Judgement is considered unbreakable, and breaking it makes the oath-breaker
fair game in any Sunward land.

shrines burned across the Reaches. In Hask, [[Magdal Crane]] will read out a list.

- The list names the [[Ferryman]]. If the Church moves against him, the ferries stop and the city chokes.
- A PC accused before a Sunward court on the day can be defended by an advocate; good ones are in short supply and
  very expensive.
- Someone the PCs care about is among the heretics to be sentenced in Candor; they have three weeks to get there.
