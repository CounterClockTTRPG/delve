Tessarine stands in dry, terraced hills at the eastern end of the [[Lantern Principalities]], three days' ride from the
first glass dunes of the [[Glass Waste]]. It is a city of white stone, cypress and slow fountains, and it smells of
jasmine, cedar oil and, under them, something bitter. Tessarine's two trades are perfume and glass: the first made in
its stillrooms, the second carried in from the Waste by the [[Glass-Pickers]] and cut, sorted and sold in the Shard
Market.

## The Host's Law

In Tessarine the host must taste every dish and every cup before a guest does. A host who forgets has insulted the
guest; a guest who eats before the host has called him a poisoner. The great families keep children as "first
mouths", usually nephews and wards, who taste on the host's behalf, and a family with many young cousins is reckoned
very safe and very wealthy. Poison is still the city's favourite way to settle a quarrel. It is simply slower now.

## The city

- **The Shard Market.** Glass from the Waste sold by weight and by colour: green for windows, black for knives, the
  rare clear "lamp-glass" for lenses and charms. Pickers sell here and drink their money in the same street.
- **The Stillrooms.** Perfumers' workshops in the old quarter. A Tessarine perfumer can make a scent that makes you
  trust its wearer, or so the perfumers say, and they charge as if it were true.
- **The Shut Room.** At the heart of the princess's palace is a chamber sealed with lead for two hundred years. The
  House says its Lantern is inside. The door is shown to guests. The door is very fine.

## The princess

Princess Ilaria is thirty, widowed twice, both times quietly. She is cleverer than Prince Severin of [[Corvanne]] and
poorer than old Loriot of [[Amaranthe]], and she sends more glass-pickers into the Waste each year than ever before.
Since the [[Margravate]]'s diggers began their great work in 1187, she pays double for any news of it.

::: dm
**The Shut Room** is one of the two princely Lanterns never opened (the other is House Dulcamare's, kept in a lead
box). The House does not know what is inside; the room hums faintly on hot nights, and the last prince to open the
door, in 987, died before he could close it. If the campaign wants a second true Lantern above ground, it is here.

**Ilaria's game.** She has an agent among the Margrave's diggers, a glass-picker named Benna Coss, and knows more about
what they found than anyone south of the Waste. She is weighing whether to sell that knowledge to the Margrave's
enemies or to the Margrave.

**Hooks.**
- A guest at a Tessarine supper collapses. The host tasted the dish; the "first mouth" child is missing. The party
  were the only foreigners at the table.
- The princess hires a crew to carry a sealed letter to Benna Coss at the dig, and to bring back whatever Benna
  gives them, without opening it.
- A Stillroom perfumer wants a pint of water from the Glass Saint's mirage pool. She swears it exists.
:::
