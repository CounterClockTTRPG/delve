The Open Mouth is the hidden cult of [[Nhul]], the Mouth Beneath, the one great god whose name is not spoken in a
temple. Its creed is short: everything is food, and the hungry will be fed. It feeds its god with the living. It has
cells in the underside of every city in the Reaches, and every church, guild and thieves' society hunts it. It
survives anyway, because hunger always does.

## What people know

- Its sign is an open mouth, drawn as a circle with a gap. Chalked on a wall, it means a cell is near. Haskers
  scrub it off and spit.
- It takes people nobody will miss: beggars, drunks, foreigners, children of the gutter.
- Its members look like anyone. They are said to keep their mouths covered when they eat, and to eat a great deal.
- It is strongest in the [[Hollow Days]], when doors are shut and nobody walks the streets.
- Being found a member of it means death in every city of the Reaches, and in Hask, unusually, no fine will do.

## In Hask

Hask's cell lives in the sewers, below the reach of the Watch, and has done for at least twenty years. Its leader is
called **the Tongue**. [[Mother Gutter]]'s beggars hunt it, and in the drains under [[Wetside]] there is a quiet war
that the rest of the city barely notices: a missing beggar here, a dead cultist floating out of a culvert there.

Everyone in Hask knows a Wetside story about it: the rat-catcher who went down and came up thin, the tanner's boy who
was heard singing in the drains three days after he drowned. Most of the stories are false.

::: dm
The Hask cell is larger than anyone above ground believes: forty members, three of them in guild households on
[[Tollside]] and one a Watch sergeant. They have found a way from the sewers into the Wet Halls of the [[Undercroft]],
and they use the drowned library as their temple, where something in the water takes what they bring it. The Tongue
is a well-liked cook at the Gilded Eel.

They are not only feeding Nhul: they are feeding on Hask's small gods. They have been leaving rendered fat for
[[Old Tallow]] and taking Mother Gutter's Children, and both gods are weakening. A small god eaten from within
becomes a door the Mouth can open.

**Hooks.**
- A chalk mouth appears on the door of the crew's lodging. Someone has marked them as food, or as recruits.
- A Watch sergeant offers a bounty for the Tongue, and quietly steers the crew toward the wrong man.
- In the Wet Halls, a crew finds Mother Gutter's beggars and the cult fighting in the dark, and has to choose a side
  fast.
:::
