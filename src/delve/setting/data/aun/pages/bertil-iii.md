Bertil III is the King of [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]], twenty-third of Aldric's line, seventy-one years old and
without an heir. He has reigned for forty-four years, from [[Aldmoor]], and has not won a war, lost a war, or done
anything else that bards remember. People in Calder call him "the Old King" with more fondness than respect.

## The man

Big once, now stooped; white-bearded, slow-spoken, with a shepherd's hands he is proud of. He likes sheep, hunting,
old songs and plain food, and dislikes ambassadors, bankers and the Crown Council, which is to say his job. He is
shrewder than he lets on, and has kept his barons from civil war for forty years by setting them against each other.

## Family

His queen died in 1165. Their two sons are dead: Prince Edric of a fever in 1179, Prince Hal in a hunting fall in 1183.
His only daughter married a Lantern prince and died in [[Tessarine]] childless. The nearest kin are cousins, and the
cousins are barons.

## What he does

- Keeps the Crown Council arguing instead of fighting.
- Borrows from House Orlemont in [[Hask]] to pay the march garrisons.
- Has kept a careful peace with the [[Sunward Theocracy]], letting the [[Sunward Church]] build temples in exchange for
  troops it has never yet had to send.
- Has refused, three times, to name an heir.

## Reputation

His people think him a decent old man. His barons think him an obstacle. Foreign courts think him finished.

## At the table

The king is rarely met in person; what adventurers meet is his household, his barons and the rumours of his health.
If the party does reach him, he asks them blunt questions, remembers the answers and may well give them a job no one
else is to know about.

::: dm
**He is dying,** and has been in bed since the [[Hollow Days]]. He has not named an heir because he has one: a
grandson by his dead son Hal and a shepherd's daughter of the downs, a boy of seventeen called **Wat**, who does not
know who his father was. Bertil has told only one person: the [[Hill That Listens]], last year, in exchange for an
answer. He asked it whether the boy would live to be king. He has not told anyone what it said.

**Hooks.**
- The king summons the party at night, secretly, and asks them to bring the boy to Aldmoor alive.
- A baron learns that the king paid the Hill a secret, and hires the party to go and buy it back.
- The king dies mid-campaign. Each baron asks the party to carry the news to his allies first.
:::
