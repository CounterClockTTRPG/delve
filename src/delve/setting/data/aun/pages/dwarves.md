Dwarves are dour, proud and long-lived, and their memories are long and their grudges longer. Their holds are in the
[[Hornwall]], the mountains between the [[Northern Holds]] and the south; the greatest is [[Deephorn]], under King
[[Barrek Stonesong]]. They are crafters before they are anything else, and they judge every people, god and stranger by
the quality of their work.

They traded with the [[Thaumarchy]] for fifteen centuries and despised it the whole time. Ask a dwarf about the old
empire and you will get a list of every contract it broke, with dates.

## What they are like

- **Makers.** Dwarven steel, stonework and gemwork are the best in the Reaches and priced like it. Every piece carries
  its maker's mark and its maker's family's mark, and a flaw shames both.
- **Grudges.** Dwarves keep grudge-books, written records of every wrong done to the family, the hold, the people. A
  grudge is settled by payment, apology or blood, and nobody is allowed to forget one that isn't. Barrek Stonesong's
  grudge against the Margrave over the eastern mines fills three pages.
- **Faith.** They honour [[Asheth]] above all the [[Great Gods]] and [[Vorm]] after her. They are polite to small gods
  of stone and water, and pay them in work: a mended wall, a cleared spring.
- **Dour.** A dwarf who laughs out loud has either drunk a great deal or seen an enemy fall. Their humour is dry
  enough to start fires.

## Customs

- A dwarf never sells the first thing they make in a new forge; it goes to the temple of Asheth.
- Beards are not cut except in mourning. A beardless dwarf is grieving or disgraced, and asking which is rude.
- Debts are paid in full, early. A dwarf who owes you will find you.
- Sayings: "Written down." (Said of an insult, and it means it is.) "Stone remembers."

## At the table

Dwarves travel south to trade, to work as smiths and masons in [[Hask]], and to settle grudges. A dwarf adventurer may
have a grudge to settle, or be one: some holds send their disgraced out to earn back their names. They are distrusted
less than elves, since everyone wants their work, and they see ruins as builders do: what was made well and what was
skimped.

::: dm
The eastern mines Barrek's grudge is about, [[Deephorn]]'s Garrowdeep, were taken by the Margrave's orc tribes in 1163. What the dwarves do not say
is why they were dug: the deepest shaft broke into a Thaumarch vault, and something brass walked out of it. The hold
sealed the shaft, and the Margrave's diggers have since unsealed it.

Hooks:
- A dwarf in Hask hires the party to collect on a grudge two hundred years old, from the great-grandson of the man who
  owed it. The grandson is a Watch captain.
- A grudge-book has been stolen from a Deephorn trading house in Hask. Every family named in it wants it back, or burned.
:::
