The **Seven Lanterns** were the binding-lamps of the [[Great Binding]], forged by the last Thaumarch-Queen, Ysarre the
Bright, and the Seven Houses of the [[Thaumarchy]], one Lantern for each House. Lit together in [[Ishavel]], they were
meant to draw the [[Great Gods]] into service. They were lit, and the [[Unmaking]] followed. Afterwards they were
scattered, and nobody agrees how or where.

## What they look like

Descriptions differ, which tells you how many people have actually seen one. The commonest account, from Thaumarch
carvings: a lamp the height of a forearm, a cage of pale metal around a heart of clear stone, with the sigil of its
House on the handle. Unlit, it is cold to the touch even in summer. Lit, it is said to burn without oil and cast no
shadow behind the one who holds it.

## Who claims one

- **Every Lantern prince.** Each of the seven princes of the [[Lantern Principalities]] claims to hold their House's
  Lantern, and shows it on high feast days behind a screen of silk. The Principalities take their name from the
  claim.
- **The [[Sunward Church]]** says one was given into [[Aurel]]'s keeping at the Unmaking and lies in [[Candor]]. It
  has never been shown.
- **Rumour in [[Hask]]** says one is under the [[Pale Tower]], deep in the [[Undercroft]]. Every crew at the
  [[Drowned Toe]] has heard a version. Most have a map.

## At the table

A Lantern is the greatest prize in the Reaches. Anyone with a credible lead on one becomes, overnight, the most
interesting person in any city: the [[Collegium of the Lamp]], the [[Ember Court]], the Sunward inquisitors, the
Lantern princes and the [[Open Mouth]] will all want a word. Fake Lanterns are a trade in themselves; a good one sells
for more than a manor.

::: dm
At most two Lanterns are real and survive. One lies under the Pale Tower, at the bottom of the Undercroft, still
partly lit; the tower leans because of it. The other is held by one of the three strong princes, though not the one
who shows it most. The rest are lost, broken or melted into the glass of the Waste.

- A real Lantern still holds a share of what the Binding caught. Lighting one draws a god's attention to the bearer,
  fully and at once. It may also loosen something the god has kept shut for twelve centuries.
- The [[Margravate]]'s diggers in the [[Glass Waste]] are looking for a Lantern. The giant brass hand they found in
  1187, open and palm up, may be the cradle where one was kept.
- Clue trail for the Pale Tower Lantern: each level of the Undercroft has a brass plate with one of the Seven House
  sigils, and the last plate tells which House's Lantern lies below.
:::
