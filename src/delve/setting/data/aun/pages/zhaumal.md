Zhaumal is the ruined city at the heart of the [[Greenmaw]], a hundred-odd miles up the brown rivers from the coast.
It is older than the [[Thaumarchy]], older than any human kingdom, perhaps older than humans: a city of stepped stone
temples, causeways and drowned plazas, every surface carved with serpents. The jungle has taken most of it. The
tribes keep the rest.

## The city

Zhaumal covers some six miles of ground, most of it under roots and green water. What still stands:

- **The Causeway of Heads.** The way in from the river: a raised road a mile long, lined both sides with stone serpents'
  heads, each the size of a cart, each with its mouth open. Pilgrims put a gift in every twentieth mouth.
- **The Stepped Temples.** A dozen great stairs of stone rising out of the trees, flat-topped, each with a shrine at
  its summit. The tribes climb them at dawn to sing.
- **The Drowned Plaza.** A great square flooded knee-deep with warm, clear water, so still that the temples stand in it
  upside down. Something large moves through the plaza at night.
- **The Oldest Temple.** At the city's centre, a low, plain building of black stone with one door and no carving at
  all. Inside it, in the dark, the [[Serpent Oracle]] speaks.

## Coming to Zhaumal

The tribes allow outsiders into the city only as petitioners of the Oracle. A petitioner comes up the river in a tribal
canoe, unarmed, walks the Causeway barefoot, and waits on the temple steps until called, which may be an hour or a
month. Armed strangers are turned back once. They are not turned back twice.

The price of a question is a name. A petitioner gives up their own name at the door, and leaves Zhaumal with a new
one, given by the Oracle. The tribes will use no other, and many who have come back from Zhaumal find their old name
will not quite stay in their mouth.

## What is said of it

- The serpents on the walls change position over the years.
- The city's builders were not human, and are still there, very small, in the walls.
- The Thaumarchs sent an army to Zhaumal once, in the days of their power. It went up the river and did not come down.

::: dm
**The army** is in the Drowned Plaza: three thousand Thaumarch soldiers, still in their armour, standing under the
water, all facing the Oldest Temple. Their brass officers stand with them, and one carries the standard of a House.

**The new name** is real magic. A character who takes it is hard for small gods and curses to find under the old one
(+2 on saves against magic that names them) but cannot be raised from death under the old name either.

**Hooks.**
- The [[Ember Court]] has sent a petitioner to ask the Oracle where the [[Seven Lanterns]] lie. The party is asked by the
  tribes to keep him from reaching the door, without killing him on the Causeway.
- A Lantern prince wants the House standard from the Drowned Plaza.
- A party member's new name, given by the Oracle, is a name already carved on a wall in the Oldest Temple, next to a
  date three years from now.
:::
