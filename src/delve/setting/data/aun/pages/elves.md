Elves are few, old and strange. They were in the Reaches before human memory, and many now living remember the
[[Thaumarchy]] because they saw it. They refused it. They will not say why, and they do not explain themselves, about
that or anything else. Most humans have never met one; most who have wish they had asked better questions.

Their great forest is the [[Thornwold]], between Calder and [[Hask]], ruled by the Lady [[Ysmeine]]. There are others
farther off, and scattered elves live alone in odd places: a wood on a hill, an island in a lake, the attic of a
bookshop in the [[Lamp Quarter]].

## What they are like

- **Slow.** An elf may live a thousand years and acts like it. They let a quarrel sit for a century to see if it
  matters. Humans find this infuriating; elves find humans exhausting.
- **Exact.** An elf says exactly what they mean, and no more. A promise from an elf is kept to the letter, which is not
  always the same as being kept.
- **Apart.** They do not trade much, marry out rarely, and keep no embassies. Half-elves exist, a few in a generation,
  and belong fully to neither side.
- **Memory.** They remember the names of the Thaumarchs, the old roads, where the great works stood. Ask the right elf
  the right question and you will learn where something is buried. Ask the wrong one and you will be told a riddle.

## Customs

- An elf who gives you their name has done you an honour; using it in front of other humans is an insult.
- Cutting a living tree in the Thornwold without leave is answered by an arrow, then a question.
- Elves do not eat in front of strangers. Offering one food is polite; watching them eat it is not.
- They bury their dead nowhere anyone has found. [[Velis]]'s priests have asked; the elves said, "They are kept."

## At the table

An elf character is a stranger everywhere outside the forest: stared at in villages, followed in Hask, distrusted by
the [[Sunward Church]], which suspects anything that remembers the old empire. They have a long view, short patience
for human fuss, and a memory the DM can use: once a session, an elf may remember something about a ruin that no living
human knows. It need not be useful.

::: dm
The elves refused the Thaumarchy because they had seen something like it before, long before humans, and remember how
it ended. Their oldest songs speak of an earlier binding of gods, by elves, that failed. They will not let it be tried
again, and some of them watch the hunt for the [[Seven Lanterns]] very closely.

Hooks:
- An elf asks the party to bring back a brass key from the [[Undercroft]] and to not look at the door it opens. The elf
  will know if they did.
- A half-elf in [[Wetside]] is being hunted by elves from the Thornwold. He says it is for being born. It is for
  something he took.
:::
