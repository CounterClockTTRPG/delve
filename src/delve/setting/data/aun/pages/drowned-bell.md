Off the [[Drowned Coast]], in water too deep for divers and too shallow for comfort, there is a temple bell. It hung
once in a Thaumarch town that the sea took, and it rings still, though it has no clapper and lies on its side in the
weed. When it rings, a ship is going to be wrecked. It is one of the [[Small Gods]], and the pirates of [[Gullhaven]]
pray to it.

## What it does

- **It warns.** Before a wreck, sailors within a few miles hear a single deep note under the hull. A captain who turns
  back at the sound is usually glad. A captain who doesn't is usually not heard from.
- **It chooses.** Pirates say the bell rings for the ship that will be wrecked, so a pirate who hears it looks for the
  nearest merchant and follows. Wreckers on the shore light false lights on nights the bell has been heard.
- **It keeps.** What a wreck spills near the bell belongs to it. Divers who take from its own waters find it ringing in
  their ears for the rest of their lives.

## Customs and taboos

- **The bell-coin.** A pirate ship sailing out of Gullhaven drops a piece of plunder over the bell's water, usually
  something that rings: a cup, a buckle, a spoon. The best offerings are bells stolen off other ships.
- **Silence.** No bells on board a ship that prays to it. Captured ship's bells are thrown to it, never kept.
- **Taboo.** Ringing a bell at sea in mockery. Sailors who have done it say it rang back.
- Saying: "If you hear it, you're not the one" is what pirates say, hoping it is true.

## Its priests

The Drowned Bell's priests are bell-wives: old women of Gullhaven who were once divers. On the Drowned Coast they can
hear a ship's bell a day's sail off and know a wreck before it happens. Beyond the coast they are only old women. They
are in steady feud with [[Marrah]]'s hook-priests, who call the bell a cheat that steals the goddess's drowned.

::: dm
The bell rings before wrecks because it causes them. It is hungry for the sound of ships breaking, and its strength
has grown with every wreck since [[Salt Annet]]'s captains began feeding it plunder. Lately it rings more often.

Under the bell, in the drowned Thaumarch town, the temple it hung in is nearly whole, and dry inside, in a bubble of
old air.

Hooks:
- A merchant house hires the party to escort a ship past the Drowned Coast. Halfway, the bell rings.
- A bell-wife wants a particular bell stolen, the great bell of a Sunward temple in [[Candor]], and promises the
  Drowned Bell's favour for a year in return.
:::
