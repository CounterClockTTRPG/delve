Teodric Vael is the ruler of the [[Margravate]], and has been for three hundred years. He was born about 858 RY, the
younger son of a Calderish march-lord on the edge of the [[Glass Waste]]. In 887, as lord of the march himself, he led
a small party into a Thaumarch tomb that his diggers had found in the glass, and came out alone, wearing the
[[Ember Crown]]. The next year he broke from [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]]. He has looked about fifty ever since.

## The man

Tall, lean, grey at the temples, with a soldier's short beard and a clerk's ink-stained fingers. He dresses plainly in
black and dark red. The Crown is a thin band of reddish metal, worn under his hair, and gives off a faint heat that
people who stand close to him notice. He speaks quietly and seldom repeats himself.

He is not a raving tyrant. He is patient, practical and very good at governing; he reads every report, remembers every
name and hangs people for reasons he can explain. He has outlived every enemy he ever had and most of their
grandchildren, and he treats the rulers of the Reaches the way a schoolmaster treats children who will soon go home.

## What he does

- Rules from [[Vaelholt]] through reeves he chooses and dismisses.
- Commands the [[Grey Legions]] and the [[Ember Court]], and pays both well.
- Fought Calder and the [[Sunward Theocracy]] to a standstill in the [[Seven Years' War]].
- Keeps [[Hask]] free, because a free Hask sells him everything.
- Since 1187 has sent diggers deep into the Glass Waste.

## Reputation

In Calder he is a traitor and a monster; mothers threaten children with him. In the Margravate he is the law, and
the law is safe if you keep it. In Hask he is a customer. Scholars of the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] would give
anything to examine the Crown, and one or two have tried.

## At the table

The Margrave is a patron as much as a villain. He hires adventurers often, pays exactly what he promised, and asks
for exactly what he wants. He is interested in anything Thaumarch, and in anyone who has been under the
[[Pale Tower]].

::: dm
**The Crown is eating him.** The Ember Crown holds the trapped spark of a lesser power and keeps him alive by burning
it, and him, from within; it is going out. When he takes it off he ages a day an hour. His body is hollow and hot
inside, and on bad nights he coughs ash. He has perhaps five years. He is searching for a new fire: the dig in the Waste,
the [[Seven Lanterns]], anything bound that could be bound again.

**Hooks.**
- The Margrave hires the party to recover a Thaumarch text from the [[Undercroft]] on binding sparks. He wants it
  whole; he will know if a page is missing.
- A Collegium wizard claims she can make a second Crown. The Margrave wants her brought to Vaelholt, alive and
  willing, and the [[Sunward Church]] wants her burned first.
- The trapped spark is aware, and dreams. Someone who touches the Crown hears it ask for help.
:::
