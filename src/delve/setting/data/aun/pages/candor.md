Candor is the holy city of [[Aurel]] and the capital of the [[Sunward Theocracy]], a white city on a bay of the Sunder
Sea. Seen from a ship at noon it is too bright to look at, which is the point. Every wall is limed, every roof is pale
tile, and the streets are laid out in straight lines from the great temple at the centre, so that at noon no street
has a shadow in it.

## The city

- **The Dial.** The central square, paved in white and grey stone as a sundial a quarter-mile across. The shadow of
  the temple spire is its pointer. Courts sit on the hour-lines; a case is heard on the line where the shadow falls when
  it begins, and must end before the shadow leaves it.
- **The Temple of the Scale.** Aurel's great temple, with a gilded spire and a golden scale over the altar large
  enough to weigh an ox. On the [[Sun's Judgement]] the Hierarch sits beneath it and the year's heretics are sentenced.
- **The Gnomon.** The Hierarch's palace, a plain tall house beside the temple. [[Anselm IX]] lives in four rooms of it
  and has given the rest to the inquisitors.
- **The Ash Yard.** A walled field outside the north gate where small-god shrines, forbidden books and, on bad years,
  heretics are burned. Velis's priests keep a hut by the gate.
- **The harbour.** The one untidy part of the city: foreign ships, sailors, Southron traders and a Marrah shrine the
  Church has not quite dared to close.

## Custom

- No one walks in the Dial at noon except the court. Crossing it then is contempt, and costs a day in the stocks.
- Inns lock their doors at sunset. Nothing done in Candor after dark is lawful, and strangers abroad at night are
  asked their business by the watch, which carries lanterns with gold-tinted glass.
- Beggars are licensed and wear a white cord. Unlicensed beggars are put to work on the walls.
- A visitor's first purchase is lime: lodgings must be whitewashed by the guest before he leaves.

## At the table

Candor is beautiful, clean, safe and terrifying. Adventurers come here on Church business, to plead a case on the
Dial, to rescue someone from the Ash Yard, or to steal from the richest temple in the west.

::: dm
**Under the white.** Candor's harbour quarter hides the last small god in the city: **Salt Wife**, the spirit of a
tide-pool under the fish market, kept alive by fishwives who drop a scale from every catch. Anselm IX knows it is
there, and is waiting to find it himself, before the Sun's Judgement, as a sign.

**Hooks.**
- A merchant's case on the Dial has been placed on the shortest hour-line. He hires the party to delay the shadow:
  cloud, smoke, anything, for an hour.
- An inquisitor's confession records go missing from the Gnomon; the party is suspected, and must find the real thief
  before noon tomorrow.
- A Lantern prince's envoy pays the party to get a sealed letter to the Hierarch's own hand, past his inquisitors.
:::
