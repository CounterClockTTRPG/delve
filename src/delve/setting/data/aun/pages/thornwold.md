The Thornwold is the elves' forest, a hundred and fifty miles of old oak, holly and blackthorn between
[[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]] and [[Hask]]. No Calderish king has ever ruled it, and none has tried twice. It is dim,
quiet and very old, and the thorns on its blackthorns are as long as a finger. Its lady is [[Ysmeine]].

## The road through

The old Thaumarch road from [[Aldmoor]] to Hask runs straight through the forest: the **Thornway**, a hundred and
sixty miles of pale stone that has not cracked or grown over in twelve hundred years. It is the quickest way between
the two cities, and the elves allow it, on terms.

- **By day only.** Travellers move between dawn and dusk. Along the road are five clearings, the **Stopping Places**,
  a long day's ride apart, and a traveller must be inside one by nightfall. Those who camp anywhere else are not seen
  again, or are seen again much later. Walkers who cannot make a Stopping Place in a day should not start.
- **No fire and no axe.** No fire may be lit except in the Stopping Places, and no living wood cut anywhere. A broken
  branch is forgiven; a felled tree is not.
- **The toll.** At the forest's edge stands a grey stone, and someone sits by it. The toll is a true story, told aloud,
  that the teller has never told before. The elves can tell a lie, and they keep the stories.

The long road round the south of the forest takes four days more.

## The forest

Off the road the Thornwold is a maze. Paths that lead in do not lead out. There are pools that show the sky of another
season, rings of standing thorns, Thaumarch ruins swallowed by roots, and trees that the woodcutters of the edge swear
were not there last year. The [[Elves]] themselves are seldom seen: a voice, a laugh, an arrow in a tree beside your
head as a warning.

## At the table

The Thornwold is wonder and danger in equal parts, and a lesson in keeping odd rules exactly. Adventurers cross it,
are sent into it to find someone who left the road, or carry a message to the Lady, who may answer and may not.

::: dm
**What the elves guard.** At the heart of the forest is a hollow where Ysmeine's people buried something from
[[Ishavel]] at the [[Unmaking]], something they carried out of the burning city and will not let humans have again. It
is not a [[Seven Lanterns|Lantern]], but it was made to light one.

**Hooks.**
- A Calderish baron's son left the Thornway on a dare a year ago. His father pays the party to bring him back; when
  they find him, a year has been three days for him, and he does not want to leave.
- The toll-keeper's stories are being stolen; someone is selling them in Hask's [[Lamp Quarter]], where scholars pay
  for the true memories of strangers.
- Woodcutters from the Calder side have begun felling at the forest's edge, paid by a baron who wants a war he can win.
:::
