Tallow Lane is a single street in [[Hask]], and it is a ward of its own. It climbs from the Hards in [[Wetside]] up to
the [[Lamp Quarter]] in a long crooked S, so steep in places that it turns to steps, and every house on it is a
candle-maker's. The air is thick and warm and smells of rendered fat, beeswax and smoke. The cobbles are greasy year
round. People slip here a great deal, and they are always cheerful about it, because the lane is lucky.

## Old Tallow's street

Tallow Lane belongs to [[Old Tallow]], the small god of the street: a fat, greedy, kindly god of small light. Every
house has a niche by the door where a stub of candle burns for him, and the stubs are never allowed to go out.
At the top of the lane, where it meets the Lamp Quarter, is his shrine: a heap of melted wax as tall as a man,
built up over four hundred years from offerings, with a wick in the top that burns night and day.

On Tallow Lane, nobody's house burns. Fires that start there go out on their own. In a city of timber and lamp-oil,
that is worth a great deal, and lodgings on the lane are dear for it.

## The Chandlers

The Chandlers are one of the nine great guilds of the Ford Council, and Tallow Lane is their seat. They make every
candle in Hask, from rush-lights for the poor to the great tapers in [[Templegate]], and they buy fat from the
tanneries and the butchers at prices fixed every [[Ferry Night]]. The guild's master keeps the shrine; she is called
the **Wick-Wife**, and she gives the god his first candle every morning.

## Customs

- Light a candle-stub at a door niche and the house will take you in for a night, no questions. Stealing from a house
  that took you in this way brings bad luck, and the street makes sure it does.
- Never blow out a candle on Tallow Lane. Pinch it, or let it burn.
- Children of the lane are given a stub of the god's candle at birth and keep it all their lives.

::: dm
Old Tallow can be bargained with by anyone who brings him fat. He knows every room a candle has ever burned in on the
lane, and what was said there. The Wick-Wife, Ada Fenn, sells this knowledge, carefully, to the [[Quiet Hand]].

**Hooks.**
- A house on the lane caught fire last week and burned. It shouldn't have. Old Tallow is sick, and the lane is
  frightened. Someone has been leaving him fat that did not come from any animal.
- The [[Sunward Church]] wants the shrine at the top of the lane gone; Magdal Crane has asked to buy the house
  beside it.
