Barrek Stonesong is the King under the Hornwall, lord of [[Deephorn]] and first among the [[Dwarves]] of the
mountains. He is about two hundred and sixty years old, which is late middle age for a dwarf, and has been king for
ninety of them. His name comes from his family's trade: they were the hold's tunnel-singers, who listen to rock and
tell the miners where it will break.

## The king

Short even for a dwarf, broad as a door, with a grey beard braided in nine plaits (one for each of his dead sons and
daughters, and three more for the dead of Garrowdeep). His voice is very deep and very soft. He wears a plain iron
crown and carries a miner's hammer, not a sword. He sits in the Hall of [[Asheth]] in the evenings and listens to the
smiths.

He is courteous, patient and does not forget anything. He reads the Long Book every morning, beginning with the red
pages.

## What he does

- Rules Deephorn with a council of the forge-masters and the elders of the great families.
- Holds the Ambre Gate in the [[Hornwall]] and takes its toll.
- Sells dwarven steel to [[Hask]], the [[Northern Holds]] and [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]], and never to the
  [[Margravate]].
- Nurses a grudge against [[Teodric Vael]], whose orc tribes took the eastern mines of Garrowdeep in 1163. Barrek led
  the defence himself, and lost his eldest son there.

## Reputation

To dwarves he is a good king: careful, just, stubborn. To humans he is a hard bargainer and an honest one. To the
Margrave's officers he is the only neighbour worth worrying about.

## At the table

Barrek hires humans for work dwarves will not or should not do: scouting the Margrave's mines, carrying messages
through orc country, buying back things stolen from the hold. He pays in steel and introductions, and enters every
debt in the Book, both ways.

::: dm
**The war he is planning.** Barrek means to take Garrowdeep back before he dies. He is waiting for the Margrave to
commit the [[Grey Legions]] west against Calder, and is quietly hiring outsiders to map the way in. He has also
learned that the Margrave is weaker than he looks (a smith of Deephorn was once asked to repair the Ember Crown's
band, and saw what was inside it).

**Hooks.**
- Barrek hires the party to map Garrowdeep's upper workings and bring back a lump of the iron the Margrave is mining
  there. It is warm to the touch.
- One of the king's surviving daughters wants to marry a Hold-folk jarl. The king wants the party to find out what the
  jarl is really after (the Ambre Gate).
- A human has been entered in the red pages under the party's patron's family name. The king expects the debt paid.
:::
