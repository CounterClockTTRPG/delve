The Drowned Toe is the adventurers' tavern of [[Hask]], on the corner of the Hards and [[Tallow Lane]] in [[Wetside]].
It is low, long, smoky and loud, built out of three warehouses knocked into one, with a river-stair at the back for
customers who need to leave by water. Crews meet here, hire here, sell maps here and drink to their dead here. If you
want work in Hask that involves a sword, start at the Toe.

## The house

The landlady is **Hessa Brannock**, a broad half-orc woman with a voice that carries across the ferry-queue and a
cudgel called Reasonable. She doesn't care what you've done; she cares whether you pay. She keeps a back room for
crews to plan in, at an oar a night, and a strong-box for what crews leave behind.

- **Ale** is two toes, the black river beer one, and nobody drinks the wine twice.
- **A bowl of eel and onions** is three toes. The eels are from the Ambre and are best not thought about.
- **A bed upstairs** is an oar a night, two to a bed, swords under the pillow.

## The walls

The Toe's walls are its notice-board. Look for:

- **The Map Wall**: maps of the [[Undercroft]], all sold as true, a few of them nearly so. A good one costs ten oars; a
  great one has a name on it, and that crew is usually dead.
- **The Work Wall**: jobs nailed up by merchants, guilds and people who'd rather not be named. Caravan guards, bounty
  notices from Gallows Hill, "a quiet errand, pays well".
- **The Toe Wall**: one copper toe nailed up for every adventurer who went into the [[Pale Tower]] from here and
  didn't come back. There are thousands. On the first night of each month the house drinks to the newest.

## Customs

- A new crew buys a round for the room. A crew back from the Undercroft buys two.
- Anyone may stand on a table and ask for hands for a job. Nobody may interrupt.
- No fighting inside. Outside, on the Hards, is your own business.
- A crew that comes back short of a member nails a toe to the wall before it drinks.

::: dm
Hessa keeps the strong-box honestly, which is rarer than it sounds, and the [[Quiet Hand]] protects the Toe for it:
the Widow likes to know what comes out of the Undercroft before the Collegium does. One of the oldest maps on the Map
Wall, half burned and nobody's favourite, is a true map of the third level, drawn by a crew that found the Lantern's
vault and didn't live to come back for it.

**Hooks.**
- A drunk old delver offers the crew his place on a dead crew's map for a round of drinks. He is the last of that
  crew, and he isn't sure why he lived.
- A toe on the Toe Wall has started to turn by itself on its nail, each night, to point upriver. Its owner's sister
  wants to know if he's still alive.
:::
