Vorm is winter and the mountains and the hard business of lasting through both. He is the high god of the
[[Northern Holds]], where winter is half the year, and the dwarves of the [[Hornwall]] honour him second only to
[[Asheth]]. He does not reward. He tests, and those who pass the test are still alive, which in the north is reward
enough.

In the south he is barely worshipped, except by old soldiers who served a winter on the Calder march and came back.

## What his people do

- **The Crowning.** On 1 Vormas, the first day after the [[Hollow Days]], the eldest of each household goes out at dawn
  and sets a ring of snow or ice on the household cairn: the white crown. If it lasts till noon, the household will
  last the year.
- **No complaints.** Complaining of cold, hunger or hardship in front of a shrine of Vorm is an offence. Hold-folk
  children learn early to say "It is a fine day" through chattering teeth.
- **The long fast.** In bad winters a household fasts one day in seven and gives that food to the household with the
  least. Refusing is not a crime in the Holds. It is remembered.
- **Taboos.** Abandoning a companion in the snow; begging; dying indoors when you could crawl outside. Warriors who
  grow old are expected to walk into the winter at the end, and many do.
- **Sayings.** "Vorm hears no complaints." "It is a fine day." "Last." (That one word, said at a parting, is a blessing
  in [[Skarnholm]].)

## His clerics

A cleric of Vorm is hard, quiet and very hard to kill. They wear white, often go bareheaded in snow, and heal by
closing wounds with cold. They are expected to go last through any danger and first into any winter. Among the
Hold-folk they speak at the [[Hold Moot]] and judge feuds alongside the skalds. On the road they are calm in disasters
and impatient with soft companions. They respect [[Small Gods]] of the mountains and speak of [[Grandmother Frost]] as
Vorm's own daughter, which she has not confirmed.

::: dm
Vorm's priests in the Holds have begun to say that this coming winter will be the longest in living memory. They are
not prophesying; they have counted the ice on Grandmother Frost and it is growing. The jarls hear "a bad winter" and
think of raiding south while they still can, which suits [[Sigrun Halfhand]].

Hooks:
- A Vormite asks the party to carry an old warrior up into the mountains to die properly. His sons want him to die
  indoors, warm, and are willing to fight about it.
- A white crown in a Hold village has not melted in three months, through summer. The village is delighted. The priest
  is frightened.
