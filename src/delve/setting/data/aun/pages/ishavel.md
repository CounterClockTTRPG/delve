Ishavel was the capital of the [[Thaumarchy]], the seat of the Seven Houses, and in the old songs the Lantern of the
World: a city whose streets were lit without fire, whose towers stood above the clouds, and whose people never prayed.
On the night of the [[Unmaking]] it burned to glass. It lies now at the centre of the [[Glass Waste]], twelve days'
hard walk from the nearest water anyone will admit to, and it is the most dangerous ruin in the Reaches.

## What is left

From a distance Ishavel looks like a frozen sea breaking on a reef. Close to, the waves are streets. The fire melted
the city where it stood and left its shape: avenues running like glass rivers, houses slumped like wax candles,
domes collapsed into bowls. Walls of clear glass still hold whatever was inside them when they set: furniture, lamps,
and people.

- **The Seven Stumps.** The palaces of the Seven Houses stood in a ring around the centre. Each is now a broken
  tower of glass, fused and leaning, between fifty and two hundred feet high. Each House's colour still shows faintly
  in its glass.
- **The Bowl.** At the city's heart, where Ysarre the Bright's palace stood, is a smooth crater of black glass half a
  mile across. Nothing stands in it. Sand never settles on it.
- **The Undercity.** The fire did not reach everything below ground. Cellars, vaults, cisterns and the Thaumarchs'
  sealed workrooms lie under the glass, and the pickers' tunnels reach a few of them.

## What people say

- At night the glass glows faintly, as if remembering its lamps.
- The dead of Ishavel walk the glass streets at dusk, going about the last errands of their last evening.
- Anyone who sleeps inside the ring of the Stumps dreams of the [[Great Binding|Binding]], and wakes knowing a word in Thaumarch.
- The [[Glass Saint]] was once a woman of Ishavel who led children out of the fire.

## Visiting

Only a fool goes without a [[Glass-Pickers|glass-picker]] guide, and only the bravest pickers go past the Stumps.
[[Old Mazzar]] claims to have slept in the Bowl. Expect thirst, glass-wind, ghosts, cuts that will not close, and at
least one other crew that would prefer you were dead.

::: dm
**The Bowl** is where the gods answered. Anyone who stands at its centre feels watched by something very large and
very far away; a cleric of any great god there gets their prayer answered fully, once, and is never again answered
anywhere else.

**Ysarre's shadow** is burned into the inner wall of the Stump of her own House: a woman with her arms raised, holding
something. The thing in her hands is not there in the shadow, as if it had been taken away before the fire.

**Hooks.**
- A sealed workroom under the third Stump has been opened by someone else in the last month. The tracks lead
  north-west, toward the Margrave's dig.
- A ghost-family of Ishavel lays its table every dusk. They invite the party to sit. Those who eat the glass bread
  remember the city as it was, and speak a little Thaumarch ever after; one in six doesn't stop remembering.
- A Lantern prince will pay a fortune for a single piece of his House's colour from its Stump, to set in a crown.
:::
