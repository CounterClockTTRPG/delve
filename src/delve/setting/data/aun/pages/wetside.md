Wetside is the west bank of [[Hask]] and most of its people: dockers, tanners, rope-makers, washerwomen, half-orc
porters, Riverfolk ferry crews, deserters from the [[Free Companies]], refugees from wherever the latest war is, and
every adventurer who can't afford [[Tollside]]. It floods every spring. It smells of lime and hides and river. It is
the part of the city where things happen.

## What is here

- **The Wet Stair**, where the ferries land, and the queue that stretches back from it for half a mile on a busy
  day.
- **The Hards**, Wetside's docks: a mile of rotten jetties and stone quays where barges from [[Deephorn]] and the
  [[Lantern Principalities]] unload, and where nobody asks what's in a crate unless it moves.
- **The tanneries** along the southern bank, whose lime pits steam at dawn. The Tanners' guildmaster is the only
  guildmaster who lives in Wetside, and he never lets anyone forget it.
- **The rope-walks**, long sheds where rope is twisted by hand. Ropers' apprentices make the best runners and the
  worst lookouts in the city.
- **The [[Drowned Toe]]**, the adventurers' tavern, on the corner of the Hards and Tallow Lane.
- **The towpath**, which runs north from the Hards along the river to the [[Pale Tower]], a long day's walk past
  the [[Lady of the Weir]].

[[Tallow Lane]] climbs north out of Wetside into the [[Lamp Quarter]]; the [[Stews]] are its southern
end, where the lanes get narrower and the lamps fewer.

## Living in Wetside

A room above a rope-walk costs two oars a month; a bed in a dosshouse costs a toe a night and comes with company. The
Watch comes to Wetside in force or not at all. Disputes are settled by the guild, the [[Quiet Hand]] or a fist,
roughly in that order. Every street has a grating with a few crumbs left on it for [[Mother Gutter]], and nobody
steals them.

::: dm
The spring floods this year have opened a hole under the Hards where a warehouse floor fell in: a Thaumarch drain,
dry beyond the first bend, running west under the city. Three dockers went in to see. One came out, and won't say
what he saw except that it was "eating the walls".

**Hooks.**
- The flooded warehouse belongs to House Tesk, who want the hole bricked up and the story stopped. The [[Open Mouth]]
  wants it left open.
- A half-orc docker, Gorrick, is organising the Hards' porters to refuse Tesk barges until wages go up. Tesk wants him
  frightened; the Quiet Hand wants him alive and owing them; the porters want a crew who'll stand at the door.
:::
