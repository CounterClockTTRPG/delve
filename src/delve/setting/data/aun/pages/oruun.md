Oruun is the moon, and the moon dreams. Night, sleep, madness and foresight are all his; he gives seers their visions
and madmen their voices, and nobody is sure he knows the difference. Dreams from Oruun are rarely kind, and his
prophecies are true in the worst way available. Wise people do not ask him things.

He is worshipped by watchmen, night-soil carters, sleepwalkers, the mad and those who look after them, and by people
desperate enough to want to know the future. His month, Oruun, comes in autumn; in [[Hask]] it opens with
[[Ferry Night]], when the guilds pay the [[Ferryman]] by lantern-light, under his eye.

## What his people do

- **The dream bowl.** A bowl of water is left by the bed to catch dreams. If you dream something terrible, you pour the
  bowl out of the window before speaking to anyone.
- **Dream-telling.** Dreams are told only to a priest of Oruun or to no one. Telling a bad dream at breakfast is said
  to make it come true.
- **The dark of the moon.** On the moonless night in his month, his temples open to the mad, who are fed, washed and
  listened to. Some come to the temple to hear them, writing down every word.
- **Taboos.** Waking a sleepwalker; mocking the mad; sleeping with your face to the moon without a cloth over your eyes.
- **Sayings.** "Ask the moon and he'll answer." (A warning, not advice.) "Pour it out." People who are behaving oddly
  are "moon-touched", which is not entirely an insult.

## His clerics

A cleric of Oruun is strange. They sleep in the day, walk at night, and wake some mornings knowing things. Most are a
little mad and some are very mad; their church cares for its own. They are often kind, in an absent way, to the
broken and the lost. In a party they make excellent night watch and unsettling company, and once in a while they say
something at breakfast that turns out, a week later, to have been a warning.

::: dm
Since the Margrave's diggers found something in the [[Glass Waste]] in 1187, Oruun's seers across the Reaches have
been dreaming of a lantern that burns black. They draw it the same way, in different cities, without having met. The
temple in [[Templegate]] keeps the drawings in a chest and hopes nobody asks.

Hooks:
- A sleepwalking child in [[Tallow Lane]] walks to the same sealed cellar door every night. The priest of Oruun wants
  someone to follow her inside, quietly, without waking her.
- A seer says one of the party will "open the door at the bottom." It is true. It is not what anyone thinks.
:::
