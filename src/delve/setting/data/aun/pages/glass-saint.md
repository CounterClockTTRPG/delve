In the [[Glass Waste]], where the dunes are glass and the heat comes up off them like a forge, travellers who are lost
sometimes see a figure walking ahead of them: tall, thin, too bright to look at, always a little too far to call to.
The [[Glass-Pickers]] call it the Glass Saint. It is a mirage that walks. It leads the lost to water, or to their
deaths, and nobody has found a way to tell which before the end.

It is the strangest of the [[Small Gods]]: its home is a place no one lives, and it has no shrine except the ones the
pickers make.

## Customs

- **The shard.** Every glass-picker carries a shard of clear glass with a drop of water sealed in it, or at least the
  hope of one. Shown to the sun when lost, it calls the Saint.
- **The cairns.** Pickers who are saved build a small cairn of glass where they found water, with a cup at its foot.
  A cairn without a cup marks where someone followed the Saint and died. They look the same from a distance.
- **The share.** The last mouthful of any waterskin is poured on the glass "for the Saint." Pickers who forget say they
  saw it walking the other way.
- **Taboos.** Following the Saint when you are not lost; speaking to it; looking back after it has gone.
- Sayings: "Lost enough?" (asked of a companion who wants to follow it). "The Saint takes the thirsty." [[Old Mazzar]]
  claims to have followed it seven times, which is either a lie or a miracle.

## Its priests

The Saint has no priests, only a few pickers whom it seems to favour. In the Waste they can find water by the shine of
the glass and walk all day without thirst. Off the glass, their gift is gone, and many of them are miserable away from
it.

::: dm
The Saint is what is left of a Thaumarch who walked out of [[Ishavel]] as it burned. It leads the lost to water when
it remembers being kind, and to its own death-place when it remembers the fire. Those it leads to its death-place find
the glass there shaped like a staircase going down.

Hooks:
- A Margravate digging party has gone missing; one survivor says the Saint led the rest "home." The Margrave wants them,
  or what they dug up, back.
- A glass-picker the Saint favours has lost her gift for the first time. Something else in the Waste is calling the
  lost, and it looks like the Saint.
:::
