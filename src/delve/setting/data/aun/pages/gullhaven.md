Gullhaven is the chief port of the [[Drowned Coast]] and the only town on it that could be called a town. It is built
on and around a Thaumarch harbour mole, a curved wall of fitted stone a mile long that the sea has never broken. Half
the town stands on the old stones; the other half stands on the wrecks of ships, hulls hauled up the shingle, roofed
with planks and turned into houses, taverns and chapels. It smells of tar, fish, smoke and old rope, and on a still
day you can hear a hundred gulls arguing over the gutting tables.

## The town

- **The Mole.** The Thaumarch wall. Its top is a street, wide enough for two carts, lined with captains' houses. Its
  stones are warm to the touch, winter and summer, and nobody knows why.
- **Hulltown.** The wreck-houses on the shingle, built from a hundred ships and still named for them: a family lives
  in *the Constant Wife*, a cooper works in *the Pride of Amaranthe*.
- **The Captains' Table.** A long table of black wood in the hall at the end of the Mole, with a chair for every
  captain who holds a ship. The chairs are carved with the ships' names. Empty chairs are not removed; they are turned
  to face the wall.
- **The Hanged Gull.** The tavern where crews are hired, cargoes sold and quarrels started. Nobody is asked their
  name. The landlady keeps a ledger of debts in a code only she can read.
- **The Bell Chapel.** A driftwood shrine at the tip of the Mole, facing the sea, with a rope that hangs down into the
  water. Sailors pull the rope before a voyage; it moves nothing anyone can see. Offerings to the [[Drowned Bell]] are
  left here: rings, teeth, ships' nails.

## Customs

- A ship entering harbour rings its own bell three times. A ship that enters silent is taken to be a Sunward galley,
  and the Mole's catapults are loosed on it.
- Fighting on the Mole is forbidden. On the shingle, below the tide line, it is not.
- Any captain may call the Table by hanging a lantern from the end of the Mole. Calling it for nothing costs the
  captain a ship.
- Thaumarch salvage from the sunk towns is sold at the Low Market, held on the shingle only at low tide. Haggle fast.

## Visiting

A party in Gullhaven can buy passage anywhere, sell nearly anything, and find a fence for goods too hot for [[Hask]].
It will pay double for everything, and someone will try to rob it within a day. [[Salt Annet]]'s people watch the
harbour; it is wise to call on her, or her bosun, before doing business.

::: dm
**The Mole** is warm because it is still working. It was built to hold back the sea, and part of it still does: a
single Thaumarch keystone halfway along keeps the whole harbour from sinking like Old Calvey did. The Quell brothers
have been offered a fortune by a Sunward agent to dig it out.

**Hooks.**
- The landlady of the Hanged Gull has died, and her coded ledger is missing. Every captain on the coast is in it.
- A chair at the Captains' Table, turned to the wall for forty years, has been turned back. Its ship, *the Grey
  Heron*, is sighted outside the harbour, crewed by something that does not breathe.
- Salt Annet needs a crew of strangers, people no captain knows, for a job in [[Candor]]'s harbour.
:::
