Velis keeps the dead and what they leave behind: names, debts, the shape of a face in someone else's memory. She is
neither cruel nor kind. People fear her the way they fear deep water and respect her the way they respect a good
midwife. Of all the [[Great Gods]] she is the one whose priests turn up in every village, because someone always dies.

Her priests bury everyone: paupers, hanged men, enemies on the field, heretics the [[Sunward Church]] has burned. They
will cross a battle line under a grey veil to do it, and both sides of the [[Seven Years' War]] let them pass. A
general who kills one is cursed in every army in the Reaches.

## What her people do

- **The grey thread.** A thread of undyed wool is tied round the left wrist of the dead. Cutting it is how the living
  let them go; a ghost is often found to have its thread still on.
- **The Naming.** On 1 Velith, the month of the dead, families say aloud the names of everyone they have lost, back
  as far as anyone remembers. A name not spoken for three Namings is said to be gone for good.
- **Taboos.** Leaving a body unburied; speaking ill of the dead in a graveyard; looking at a corpse's open eyes before
  they are closed. Graverobbing is the worst crime her church knows, worse than murder, and adventurers who dig in
  tombs should keep that in mind.
- **Sayings.** "She keeps what we drop." "Grey for all." A mourner tells a grieving friend "She has him," and that is
  thought enough.

## Her clerics

A cleric of Velis wears grey and a veil for rites, carries a spade as often as a mace, and keeps a book of the names of
everyone they have buried. They are the natural enemy of the walking dead, and they treat [[Small Gods]] with weary
tolerance: many small gods keep a few dead of their own, like [[Grandmother Frost]]. In a party, a Velisite will insist
on burying the enemy, slowly, which is both a mercy and a delay. People confess things to them they would tell no one
else.

::: dm
Velis's church keeps the Long Ledger in a cold vault under her temple in [[Templegate]]: the names of the dead of
[[Hask]] since its founding, with notes on what they owed and to whom. The [[Quiet Hand]] would pay a fortune for a
day with it.

No one ever buried the dead of [[Ishavel]]. The church calls this its oldest unfinished duty, and some of her priests
go into the [[Glass Waste]] to do it, one glass-burnt body at a time.

Hooks:
- A Velisite asks the party to recover a crew's bodies from the [[Undercroft]], no treasure, just the dead. Something
  down there is wearing their grey threads.
- A name has been struck out of the Long Ledger with fresh ink. The man is alive and does not know he is officially dead.
:::
