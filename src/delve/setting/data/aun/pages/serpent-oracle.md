In the Oldest Temple of [[Zhaumal]], at the centre of the [[Greenmaw]], there is a voice in the dark. It speaks every
language a petitioner speaks, and some they don't. It is slow, dry and quiet, like a large thing breathing between
words. The tribes call it by many names; outsiders call it the Serpent Oracle. No one has seen it, or no one who saw
it came out to say so.

## What it does

The Oracle answers questions. It answers them truly, as far as anyone has ever been able to check, but it answers the
question asked and not the one meant, and it often answers in the form of a request: go to such a river, carry such a
stone, sit by such a dying man. Those who do as it asks tend to find their answer there. Those who don't tend to
find it anyway, later and worse.

The Greenmaw tribes obey it. When it speaks to a tribe, feuds stop, villages move, and once, two generations ago, a
whole people walked into the sea. The tribes do not think of this as a cruelty. The Oracle sees further than they do.

## Consulting it

- The petitioner must come to Zhaumal unarmed and give up their name at the door (see [[Zhaumal]]).
- One question per visit, asked aloud, in the dark. The Oracle may ask one back. It is wise to answer.
- The Oracle does not like being asked about the future. It prefers the past, and knows a great deal of it: the
  Elder Days, the [[Thaumarchy]], the [[Unmaking]], as if it had watched them.
- It is said never to have lied, and never to have told anyone everything.

## What people think it is

A great serpent older than the world. A small god of the whole temple-city. The ghost of one of the gods from the time
they walked. A Thaumarch working that went on speaking after its makers died. Priests of [[Sere]] want to know very
badly; the [[Sunward Church]] calls it a demon, and has never sent an inquisitor up the river twice.

::: dm
**What it is.** The Oracle is a god from the Elder Days that was never one of the twelve and was never small: a
power of the old earth, which went to ground in Zhaumal when the great gods took the sky and has been dying slowly
ever since. It is strong only inside the city. It was there when the [[Great Binding]] was attempted, felt the great
gods' anger at the [[Unmaking]], and fears nothing in the world except the [[Seven Lanterns]], because a Lantern could
bind it as easily as a great god, and it is much nearer.

So its one rule: it asks every petitioner for news of the Lanterns, and it will send the tribes against anyone who
brings one into the Greenmaw.

**Hooks.**
- The Oracle answers a party's question, then asks them to carry a sealed clay jar to the Glass Saint in the
  [[Glass Waste]]. What is in it is a message from one old power to another.
- It tells a character the true name of a dead Thaumarch prince of their own House. They had no idea they had a House.
:::
