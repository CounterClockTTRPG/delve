Sigrun Halfhand is the jarl of [[Skarnholm]], the greatest hold of the [[Northern Holds]], and the first to speak at
the [[Hold Moot]]. She is about forty, has been jarl for nine years, and in that time has led six raids south, lost
none, and buried two husbands. She wants to do something no jarl has done since the [[Ash Centuries]]: lead every hold
south together, in one host, over the mountains and down the river to the riches of [[Hask]].

## The jarl

Tall even for Hold-folk, red-haired going grey, with a weathered face and a laugh that fills a hall. Her left hand has
only the thumb and first finger; she wears a silver guard over the stumps, and fights with an axe in the right. She is
generous with silver, rough with words and very good at making people want to follow her. She cannot read, and keeps
three skalds to remember everything.

## The hand

Skalds tell three stories of how she lost the fingers, all of which she encourages:
- that she gave them to [[Grandmother Frost]] in exchange for her drowned brother's body;
- that a Calderish knight cut them off and she killed him with the other hand;
- that she bit them off herself to get free of a chain on a Margravate slave-ship.

## What she does

- Rules Skarnholm and its fjord, with its ships, its farms and its feuds.
- Speaks first at the Moot, and is listened to, if not always obeyed.
- Raids [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]]'s north coast in summer and trades with [[Deephorn]] in winter.
- Feuds with Ketil Sornsson, jarl of Kaldvik, who would like to speak first himself.

## Reputation

To her people, a lucky jarl, which is the best kind. To Calder's coast, a name to frighten children with. To Hask, not
yet anything at all, which she means to change.

## At the table

Sigrun hires southerners as guides, translators and spies, and takes on bold adventurers as crew. She asks for
loyalty and pays in silver and stories. She is fond of people who make her laugh, and quick to kill people who lie to
her.

::: dm
**The truth of the hand:** she lost the fingers to frostbite crossing the ice at nineteen, drunk, on a bet. She has
never told anyone, and only one other person, a young skald who carried her home, ever knew.

**The great raid.** Envoys of the [[Ember Court]] have promised her free passage over the [[Hornwall]] by the orc-held
Red Stair, river-boats waiting on the upper [[Ambre]], and that the [[Grey Legions]] will look away while her host
goes down the river. The Margrave wants Hask frightened and
Calder's north burning while his Legions move. Sigrun suspects she is being used, and doesn't much mind.

**Hooks.**
- Sigrun hires the party to scout the Ambre down to Hask, and to find out what the [[Ferryman]] takes from a fleet of raiders.
- Ketil Sornsson pays the party to prove to the Moot that Sigrun has taken Margravate silver.
- That skald, now old, has gone missing. Sigrun wants him found before anyone else finds him.
:::
