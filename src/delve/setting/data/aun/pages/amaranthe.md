Amaranthe is built on the islands and mudbanks where the [[Ambre]] meets the Sunder Sea. It is the largest and
richest city of the [[Lantern Principalities]], a crowd of pink stone palaces, warehouses and canals, sinking slowly
and expensively into the delta. Ships from the Greenmaw coast, the Theocracy's ports and the far south unload here,
and Hask's House Velloure keeps its biggest counting-house on the Spice Quay.

## The Chair Law

In Amaranthe a dead man's debts pass to whoever sits in his chair at the funeral feast. The chair is set at the head of
the table, empty, and the mourners eat standing. Creditors bring their own candidates: drunks, beggars and children
paid a few coppers to sit down. The family brings strong young men to stop them. An Amaranthe funeral is the liveliest
thing in the city, and sensible guests stand well back from the chair.

## The city

- **The Lighthouse of the Lamp.** A Thaumarch tower at the harbour mouth, its top rebuilt three times. The House's
  Lantern burns in its crown on feast nights, a cold white light that can be seen from twenty miles out to sea.
- **The Spice Quay.** Pepper, cloves, silk and Greenmaw gold go out; Hask iron, Calder wool and Tessarine glass come
  in. The quay smells of everything at once.
- **The Drowned Steps.** Old Thaumarch streets under the eastern canals, visible on still days. Boys dive for coins
  thrown by visitors, and sometimes come up with older coins.
- **The Temple of Marrah.** The richest temple in the city; captains pay [[Marrah]] before they sail and curse her
  when they come home.

## The prince

Prince Loriot is seventy-eight and has buried four heirs. He governs through ceremony: his court rises when he
rises, eats when he eats, and has not heard him speak above a whisper in ten years. He loves birds, keeps a
thousand of them, and is said to have poisoned a cousin for laughing at a parrot.

::: dm
**The Lighthouse Lantern** is a genuine Thaumarch working, a lamp that never needs oil, but it is a harbour-light, not
a binding-lamp. Loriot knows. He does not care: a light that works is better politics than a Lantern that might.

**The heir.** Loriot's only surviving grandson, Teo, twelve, has been raised by an [[Ember Court]] tutor named Master
Hollis Brand, who is kind, patient and absolutely loyal to the [[Margravate]]. When Loriot dies, the Margrave will have
a friend at the mouth of the Ambre.

**Hooks.**
- A merchant pays the party to sit in a dead rival's chair, for a share of the debts' discharge. The family has
  hired a Free Company to keep the chair empty.
- A diver boy brings up a brass hand from the Drowned Steps. It grips. Something under the canal is missing it.
- Smugglers from [[Gullhaven]] want a pilot through the delta channels on a moonless night. Their cargo is a Sunward
  inquisitor, alive, who knows something about the prince.
