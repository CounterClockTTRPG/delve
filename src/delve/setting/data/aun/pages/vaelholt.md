Vaelholt is the seat of the [[Margravate]], an iron town in a cleft of black hills, built around the fortress where
[[Teodric Vael]] has lived for three hundred years. The air tastes of coal and hot metal. The rain leaves grey streaks
on everything. The streets are swept twice a day, the hour-bells ring together, and the city is silent at night
except for the forges, which never stop.

## The city

- **The Ember Keep.** The Margrave's fortress on a crag above the town: seven square towers of dark stone, roofed in
  lead. A light burns in the top window of the highest tower every night. People say the Margrave does not sleep.
- **The Ledger House.** The hall of records, where every tally ever issued is written down. Clerks work in shifts, day
  and night. To have your page pulled is the start of a very bad week.
- **The Foundries.** The lower town: forges, smelters and armouries making the swords, helms and arrowheads of the
  [[Grey Legions]]. Workers sleep in long barracks by the foundry they serve.
- **The Gallows Road.** The road in from the west gate, lined with gibbets. Each one has a plaque saying who hangs there
  and why. They are kept up to date.
- **The Legion Field.** Barracks, drill grounds and pens outside the north wall; orc companies from the
  [[Hornwall]] camp at its far end and do not come into town.
- **The Sorrow Market.** The one place in Vaelholt where people talk freely: a covered market of foreign goods, where
  [[Hask]] merchants sell wine, salt and silk to the Margrave's officers. Everyone assumes it is watched, and it is.

## Custom

- Foreigners enter by the west gate only, buy a brass tally and give their business in writing. The clerk reads it,
  and gives back a copy with a stamp.
- The curfew bell rings at the second hour after dusk. After that only tally-holders on night work may walk the streets.
- At the Margrave's birthday each spring the whole city lines the road to the keep, and he rides down it, looking
  exactly as he did on the last birthday, and every one before.

## At the table

Vaelholt is a place to sneak into, to bargain in, or to be summoned to. Its rules are many, plain and enforced, and an
honest traveller who keeps them is safer here than in Hask. A dishonest one should know where every gate is.

::: dm
**The light in the tower** is the Ember Crown itself; the Margrave sits with it at night, because when he takes it
off he begins to age at once, and he has been taking it off for an hour each night to spare it. He is losing. His
Castellan, **Ilva Dorn**, a grey-haired woman who has served forty years, is the only one who knows.

**Hooks.**
- The Ledger House holds the record of a Calderish noble family's lost heir, given a tally sixty years ago. A baron in
  Aldmoor wants that page.
- A foundry master asks the party to find out who is putting cracks in the swords before they leave the forge. It is
  his own daughter, and she is in the pay of [[Deephorn]].
- The Margrave invites the party to dine. He asks a great many questions about the [[Pale Tower]].
