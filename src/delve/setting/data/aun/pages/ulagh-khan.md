Ulagh Khan is the khan of the clans of the [[Horse Steppe]], chosen at the [[Thousand Fires]] eleven autumns running.
He was born to the Kestrel clan, a small one, and became khan by winning: races, wars, arguments and, once, a famous
bet with a well. He is about fifty, which is old on the grass.

## The khan

Short, bow-legged, deep-chested, with a flat broken nose and a long grey moustache. His arms, neck and back are
covered in tattoos; the newest, on his left wrist, is a small horse for his favourite mare, dead last winter. He laughs
easily, drinks fermented mare's milk from a cup made of a Calderish helmet, and never sits on a chair.

He is clever, careful and very hard to surprise. He has kept the clans together for eleven years by giving each chief
something to want and something to fear. He trusts no one who lives in a house.

## What he does

- Judges quarrels between the clans and keeps the peace of the Thousand Fires.
- Takes the yearly payment from [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]] in grain and iron, and divides it, not quite fairly.
- Leads the great hunts, and the war-bands when he must.
- Raids Calder when the payment is late, and sometimes when it is not, to remind the barons why they pay.

## Reputation

To the clans, a good khan: lucky, fair enough and still alive. To Calder's border lords, the devil they know. To the
[[Ember Court]], a man whose price has not yet been found.

## At the table

Ulagh Khan respects courage, plain speech and good horses, and despises flattery. Adventurers who meet him should
expect to be tested: a race, a drinking match, a question with no right answer. He hires outsiders for things his own
people must not be seen to do.

::: dm
**The Margrave's gold.** An Ember Court envoy has offered Ulagh enough gold to buy every chief on the grass if the
clans ride east into Calder when the [[Grey Legions]] march west. Ulagh is tempted: Calder is weak, its king dying,
its payments shrinking. But he knows that a Calder broken by the Margrave means the Margrave on his border next. He
means to take the gold, promise everything, and ride when it suits him.

**Hooks.**
- Ulagh hires the party to find out, in Aldmoor, which baron will be king, so he knows whom to sell to.
- A rival chief means to kill the envoy at the Thousand Fires and blame Calder. Ulagh asks the party to stop it, or
  to make sure it happens, depending on what they tell him about Aldmoor.
- The khan's dead mare's foal has been stolen and taken to Calder. He wants it back, and the thief's name.
