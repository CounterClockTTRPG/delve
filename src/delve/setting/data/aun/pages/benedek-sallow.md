Benedek Sallow is the Lord Warden of the [[Free City of Hask]], elected by the nine guildmasters of the Ford Council
and now in his fourteenth year in office. He is fat, clever and corrupt, and Haskers will tell you all three things
about him with a kind of pride, because he is also very good at the one thing the city needs: keeping Hask out of
other people's wars.

## The man

Sallow is about sixty, enormous, bald, soft-voiced and always eating. He was born in [[Wetside]], the son of a
rope-maker, and rose through the Ropers' guild by being the most agreeable man in any room. He wears plain brown
like a clerk, and a single ring with the city's foot-seal on it. He remembers everyone's name, their children's names
and what they owe.

He lives at Sallow House on [[Tollside]], which has the best kitchen in the Reaches and three food-tasters. Two are
for poison. The third is because he likes to hear someone else enjoy the food first.

## How he rules

- **He sells to everyone.** Iron from the [[Margravate]], wool from [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]], silk from the
  [[Lantern Principalities]], and a hearing to anyone with a gift. In the [[Seven Years' War]] Hask sold to all three
  sides, and Sallow, then a young guildmaster, arranged half of it.
- **He takes everyone's money and does what he likes.** Every ambassador in Hask believes the Warden is in their
  pocket. He is in all of them.
- **He keeps the small gods sweet.** He has paid the [[Ferryman]]'s tithe in person every [[Ferry Night]] of his
  office, and he tells the House of the Scale, politely, that Hask's shrines are a city matter.

## At the table

Sallow is affable, curious and never hurried. He meets adventurers more often than he should, because they bring him
news before his spies do. He pays well for errands and never puts anything in writing. He will lie to a crew only if
the truth would cost him; he prefers to tell them part of it.

::: dm
Sallow's election falls due on Ferry Night 1188, and he is in more danger than he looks. The Theocracy is backing the
Draper guildmaster against him; the [[Ember Court]] is backing Sallow, which he knows will become a leash. His votes
are held together by the [[Widow]], his sister Clemence, who runs the [[Quiet Hand]] and whom he loves, fears and
pretends not to know.

He suspects the Margravate is about to move on Calder and wants to be the man both sides need to talk to when it
does.

**Hooks.**
- Sallow hires the crew to carry a private offer to [[Bertil III]]'s court in [[Aldmoor]], and the same week asks
  them to deliver a gift to a Margravate envoy at the Gilded Eel.
- One of his food-tasters dies. The poison was in a dish Sallow had sent back. He wants to know who, and he does not
  want the Watch to know there was a who.
