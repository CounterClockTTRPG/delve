The Free Companies are the mercenary bands of the Reaches: a few dozen companies, from thirty spears to two thousand,
that fight for [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]], the [[Lantern Principalities|Lantern princes]], Hask's merchant houses
or anyone else who pays. Their soldiers are [[Karsk]]'s people, and they keep a code that the Red Hand's priests call
honour and everyone else calls a price list.

## The code

- **The red hand.** A contract is sworn by both parties pressing a hand dipped in red ochre on the parchment. A
  company that breaks a red-hand contract is outlawed by every other company.
- **No sides between contracts.** A company fights its enemy of last season this season without ill will, and drinks
  with it in winter quarters.
- **Pay first.** A month in advance, always. A company not paid on the day sits down where it stands, in the field
  or not.
- **Spare the surrendered.** A soldier of a Free Company who throws down his weapon is held for ransom, not killed.
  Levies and militia get no such promise.
- **Karsk's share.** A tenth of all plunder goes to the Red Hand's temple. The priests keep a very good count.

## Well-known companies

- **The Red Lanterns.** The richest company, founded by Lanterner exiles after the [[Heirs' War]]; red lamps
  on their banners, heavy horse and crossbows, and a contract with Prince Severin of [[Corvanne]] they have held for
  eleven years.
- **The Ninefold Company.** Nine banners under one captain, each banner its own small company with its own vote.
  Pikes, and very stubborn. They held the causeway at [[Redmere]] in 1110 and have dined out on it since.
- **The Hungry Dozen.** Twelve founders, now about two hundred men and women. Cheap, fast and unpleasant; the company
  sent to burn a thing, not to hold it.
- **Orlemont's Shields.** House Orlemont's own small company in [[Hask]], hired out to guard caravans and collect
  debts. Not much loved.

## Between wars

In winter, and between contracts, the companies come to Hask. Their captains rent the upper rooms of Wetside inns,
recruit at the [[Drowned Toe]], and lend their spare hands to adventuring crews for a share. Half-orcs, Hold-folk and
landless Calderish younger sons are common in their ranks. A crew that hires a Free Company veteran gets a hard,
reliable fighter who will not die for them.

::: dm
**The coming war.** Every company knows a war is coming in 1188: the [[Grey Legions]] on the Calder march, a zealous
Hierarch, an old king with no heir. Every captain is choosing a side by choosing a contract. The [[Ember Court]] is
quietly paying several of them retainers to stay out of Calder's service; the [[Sunward Church]] is paying others to
stay out of the Lantern princes'.

**Hooks.**
- The Ninefold Company's nine banners are splitting over a Margravate contract. Four want it. The vote is on
  [[Ferry Night]], and someone is buying votes.
- The Red Lanterns' captain wants a crew to find out what Corvanne's prince really plans to do with them. He has
  started asking about Hask's ford.
- A Hungry Dozen deserter begs the party for protection. He saw what the Dozen was paid to burn, and who paid them.
:::
