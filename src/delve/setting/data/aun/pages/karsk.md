Karsk does not care who wins. He cares that people stand when they are afraid, that they fight when they have sworn to,
and that they keep the few rules soldiers have. He is the god of the [[Free Companies]], of the levy and the sergeant,
and of the man who keeps his feet in a tavern brawl. His symbol, the red open hand, means two things on purpose: the
hand that strikes and the hand held out in surrender.

## What his people do

- **The hand.** A company paints a red hand on its shields and its standard. A soldier who means to surrender holds up
  an open hand; one who kills a man after the hand is up is outcast in every company that hears of it. Most do keep
  this rule, which surprises civilians.
- **First blood.** Before a battle, a soldier pricks a thumb and presses it to the shield. After, they wash the shield
  in running water and say the names of the company's dead.
- **The Muster.** On 1 Karsk the companies parade, settle their accounts, and hire. In [[Hask]] the hiring is done
  outside [[Drowned Toe|the Drowned Toe]], loudly.
- **Taboos.** Running before the order; breaking a contract mid-campaign; striking a priest of [[Velis]] who has come
  for the dead; refusing a fair challenge before your comrades.
- **Sayings.** "Open hand, closed account." "Karsk counts the ones who stayed." Soldiers greet each other by touching
  open palms.

## His clerics

A cleric of Karsk is a company's chaplain, paymaster and bone-setter. They keep the contract, the muster roll and the
pay chest, because the god of war turns out to be very particular about wages. They fight in the line, heal the
wounded at night, and judge disputes between soldiers. Outside the companies they are hired as arbiters of duels. A
Karskite adventurer is valued in a crew for one reason above prayers: they make the others more ashamed to run.

::: dm
The Margrave forbids the worship of Karsk in the [[Grey Legions]]: soldiers there obey him, not a god who asks for
courage. His officers have found red hands painted inside the barracks at [[Vaelholt]], and they are hunting the
painters.

Hooks:
- A Free Company has broken a contract mid-campaign, and its own chaplain wants the captain brought before a Karskite
  court. The captain is paying well to keep that from happening.
- A deserter from the Grey Legions wants to swear into a Free Company in Hask. He brings maps of the Calder march, and
  the Ember Court wants him dead before the Muster.
:::
