The Greenmaw is the jungle of the south-east, five hundred miles of it between the [[Glass Waste]] and the Sunder Sea.
The name is the sailors': from the sea it looks like a green mouth, the trees coming right down to the water and the
rivers running out of it brown and warm. Inside it is heat, rain, rot, flowers the size of shields, and temples that
were old when the [[Thaumarchy]] was young. It has no king and no roads, and the gold comes out of it anyway.

## The land

- **The coast.** Mangrove, mudflat and a few river-mouth trading posts held by Southron merchant families. The largest
  is Fevermouth, a stockade, a wharf, a temple of [[Marrah]] and a graveyard bigger than the town.
- **The rivers.** Brown, slow, crowded with crocodiles and small gods. The tribes travel by canoe; outsiders hire
  them or drown.
- **The deep green.** Trees two hundred feet tall, a roof of leaves that lets through no sun, a floor of mud and roots.
  Ruins appear without warning: a stair, a stone serpent's head, a carved wall swallowed by fig roots.
- **[[Zhaumal]].** The great ruined city, some hundred and thirty miles inland, where the [[Serpent Oracle]] speaks.

## The tribes

The jungle peoples are many tribes, each with its own river, its own small gods and its own feuds, and all of them
obey the Oracle when it speaks. Outsiders call them by what they see: the Mud-Painted of the coast rivers, who trade;
the Tree-Burners of the eastern hills, who don't; the Quiet People, who are never seen and leave carved sticks across
paths that mean "turn back". The tribes are not savages and dislike being treated as such. They have long memories of
Thaumarch slave-takers: the Thaumarchy held the coast and taxed the rivers, but the deep green it only raided.

## Dangers

- **Fever.** Most foreigners take it in the first month; one in four dies of it. Southrons chew a bitter bark called
  black-bark against it, which helps a little and turns the teeth black.
- **Serpents.** Common, large, and sacred. Killing one in sight of a tribe is a declaration of war.
- **The ruins.** Old temples are trapped, flooded, full of bats, and in a few cases still in use by something.
- **The traders.** Fevermouth's merchant families trade fairly with the tribes and murder each other.

## What draws people

Gold, dust from the rivers and worked gold from the ruins; pepper, cloves and dyes; serpent-venoms for the
Lantern poisoners of [[Tessarine]]; and the answers of the Oracle, for those who will make the journey and pay its
price. Hask's House Velloure takes most of the spice north through [[Amaranthe]].

::: dm
**The jungle remembers.** The serpent-temples date from the Elder Days, when the gods walked. The Greenmaw is one of
the few places where a great god once stood on the ground and left a mark; the tribes believe the gods will return
there first. They are not entirely wrong.

**Hooks.**
- A Fevermouth merchant family hires the party to bring back a rival who went upriver to find "a temple full of gold".
  He found it. He will not leave it.
- The Tree-Burners have begun clearing a hill in a perfect circle. The Oracle has said nothing, which has never
  happened before.
- An [[Ember Court]] agent is in Fevermouth buying serpent-venom by the barrel, more than any poisoner could use.
:::
