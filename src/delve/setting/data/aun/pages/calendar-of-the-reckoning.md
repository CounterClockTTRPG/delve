Years in the Reaches are counted from the night of the [[Unmaking]], **0 RY** (Reckoned Year). Earlier dates are
counted backwards, **BU**, "before the Unmaking". It is now **1188 RY**. A date is written "4 Isaine 1188 RY", and
people say "the fourth of Isaine".

## The year

Twelve months of thirty days, each named for one of the [[Great Gods]], and five days at the year's turn that belong to
none of them: 365 days in all.

| Season | Months |
|---|---|
| Midwinter | the [[Hollow Days]] (5 days) |
| Winter | **Vormas**, **Velith** |
| Spring | **Isaine**, **Ashen**, **Marrah** |
| Summer | **Aurelmas**, **Karsk**, **Fennick** |
| Autumn | **Hollin**, **Oruun**, **Seremark** |
| Winter | the Nameless Month |

The last month was once [[Nhul]]'s. Its name is not said; people call it "the Nameless Month", "the last month" or, in
the [[Northern Holds]], "the Long Dark". Writing its true name on a document voids the document in most courts.

## Feast days

- **Hollow Days**, the five days before 1 Vormas: unlucky; doors shut, no journeys begun. See [[Hollow Days]].
- **1 Isaine**: [[Isaine's Morning]], the spring festival and the start of the campaign year.
- **15 Aurelmas**: the [[Sun's Judgement]], midsummer.
- **30 Hollin**: [[Harvest Home]].
- **1 Oruun**: [[Ferry Night]] in [[Hask]].

Each god's church keeps its own lesser holy days as well, and the churches disagree about which are greater.

## Local reckonings

- The [[Sunward Theocracy]] counts years from the crowning of Anselm I as well, and dates its laws "in the year of the
  Scale".
- The [[Lantern Principalities]] still count from the founding of the [[Thaumarchy]] in private, which makes the year
  about 2688, and think everyone else provincial.
- The [[Horse Steppe]] clans count by winters and khans: "the third winter of [[Ulagh Khan]]".
- [[Elves]] do not count years aloud.

::: dm
Use the calendar to set mood: the Nameless Month is when the [[Open Mouth]] is boldest; the Hollow Days are when
nobody will travel, which makes them the best time for anyone who wants to move unseen.

- A Lanterner noble who dates a letter by the Thaumarch count is making a claim to the old empire, and an educated
  reader will take it as one.
:::
