Ilse Varrow is the Provost of the [[Collegium of the Lamp]], the head of [[Hask]]'s seven licensed wizards, and the
most learned and least patient person in the city. She is about seventy, small, bony and fierce, with ink-stained
fingers, one milky eye and a voice like a door slammed in a corridor. She has been Provost for twenty-two years and
has outlived three rivals, two of them by natural causes.

## What she does

Varrow collects scraps of Thaumarch writing: metal leaves, glass tiles, inscribed brass, broken lenses. She has the
largest collection of them west of the [[Glass Waste]] and has read more of the old script than anyone living. What
it cost her is plain: the eye, her friends, a husband who left in her fortieth year and whom she didn't notice
leaving for a month.

She buys from crews who come up from the [[Undercroft]], and she pays badly and asks precise questions: which level,
which wall, which way the find was facing when you picked it up. A crew that answers well is remembered. A crew that
lies is remembered better.

## What she's like at the table

- Short-tempered with fools, which is most people.
- Never hurries a reading. Will keep a crew waiting three days for a verdict on a find and charge them for the wait.
- Has no small talk at all, but will speak for an hour about a Thaumarch verb.
- Fears the House of the Scale and despises it. Treats [[Magdal Crane]] with icy courtesy, and Crane returns it.
- Can cast spells, rarely and at cost. She has not done so in public for eleven years, and Hask still talks about the
  last time.

::: dm
Varrow knows one of the [[Seven Lanterns]] lies under the [[Pale Tower]], and that every Thaumarch work carried out of
the Undercroft weakens the wards on its vault. She has measured it by the Last Lamp's light. She has told no one,
because the Collegium would split over it and the inquisitors would close the Undercroft, or worse, try to open it.

She wants to reach the Lantern before the wards fail and the [[Ember Court]] or a Lantern prince gets there first.
She is not sure, any more, whether she wants to seal it or use it.

**Hooks.**
- Varrow hires a crew to map the Lamp-Deep and bring back rubbings of every door, and to tell nobody, not even the
  rest of the Collegium.
- She asks a crew to find out which of the seven is selling the Collegium's translations, and to bring her proof, not
  a body.
:::
