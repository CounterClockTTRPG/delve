The Grey Legions are the army of the [[Margravate]], and the only true standing army in the Reaches. Other realms raise
their levies in spring and send them home for harvest; the Margrave's soldiers are paid, fed, drilled and kept all year
round, and have been for three hundred years. They wear grey cloaks over black iron, carry the same spear, the same
shield and the same rations, and march in step. Other soldiers laugh at them until they meet them.

## Who serves

- **The levies.** Margravate peasants, taken one son in four at sixteen, for twenty years. They are trained hard,
  paid in coin, and allowed to marry at thirty. Many prefer it to the farm.
- **The orc legions.** Tribes of the eastern [[Hornwall]] who serve the Margrave under their own chiefs for pay, iron
  and land. The biggest are the Burnt Ear and the Tusk-Breakers. They are fierce, hard to command and, under Grey
  officers, horribly effective.
- **The officers.** Picked from the levies by merit, not birth, which scandalises Calder's knights. They obey without
  fear of the enemy, because they are more afraid of the Margrave. Every officer above the rank of captain has met him
  once, alone, and none of them speaks of it.

## How they fight

A legion is about three thousand: spears in front, crossbows behind, orc skirmishers on the flanks, a small company of
heavy horse held back. They build a fortified camp every night of a march, even in friendly country. They take
prisoners, count them and send them east to work; they hang deserters, their own and others', at crossroads. They
win by being where they said they would be, on time, every time.

## In 1188

In 1187 four legions mustered on the Calder march, at the old border of the [[Seven Years' War]], with orc legions
behind them. They have built camps and roads and have not crossed. Calder's barons are arguing about who should
command. A fifth legion guards the Margrave's dig in the [[Glass Waste]].

::: dm
**Why they wait.** The muster is real, but it is also a screen. The Margrave cannot afford a war until he knows what
the Vael Dig holds; the legions on the march keep Calder, the Theocracy and Hask looking west while the real work goes
on east. Marshal Hane does not know this and is eager to cross.

**The officers' meeting.** Every officer the Margrave meets is shown, once, what the [[Ember Crown]] is doing to him.
They are not afraid of him because he is cruel. They are afraid because they have seen what he is willing to bear.

**Hooks.**
- A Grey deserter, a levy with ten years served, has fled to Hask with his wife and child. The Legions want him back
  for a hanging; he says he knows the date of the crossing.
- The Burnt Ear chief wants to break with the Margrave and offers a crew good pay to carry his message to King
  [[Barrek Stonesong]] of [[Deephorn]].
- A legion's pay-chest, iron-bound and sealed, has gone into [[Redmere]] with its wagon. The Legions are dredging. So is
  everyone else.
