Tollside is the east bank of [[Hask]], the rich ward, built on a low rise that the spring floods don't reach. It has
stone streets, glass windows, gutters that drain, and a Watch that patrols in pairs and salutes the right people. The
city's money lives here and wants to be left alone with itself.

## What is here

- **The Council Hall**, a squat building of grey stone with nine doors, one for each guild, so no guildmaster need
  walk behind another. The bell in its tower rings the day's toll prices at noon.
- **The guildhalls** of the nine great guilds. The [[Ferrymen's Guild]] hall at the top of the Toll Stair is the
  oldest and the ugliest; the Spicers' is the newest and smells of cloves for a street around.
- **The money-tables**, a covered arcade where the Moneyers weigh foreign coin and take a tenth. Bring silver from
  [[Candor]] and they'll bite it; bring gold from [[Vaelholt]] and they'll bite it twice.
- **The merchant houses**: **Tesk** (river barges and warehouses, old Hask money), **Orlemont** (loans, brokers,
  the buying of strange objects with no questions), **Velloure** (silk, spice and dyes from the
  [[Lantern Principalities]]). Each house is a fortress with a shop on the ground floor.
- **Sallow House**, the Lord Warden's residence, which has a famous kitchen and three food-tasters.
- **The Gilded Eel**, the merchants' tavern: eel in green sauce, good wine, and deals done in low voices. Adventurers
  are served if they have washed.

## Getting on in Tollside

Tollside judges by shoes. Muddy boots mean Wetside; Watchmen will ask your business. A crew with something to sell
should go to Orlemont's brokers by the side door, after dark. A crew looking for work should wait outside the Gilded
Eel at noon, when merchants come out with too much wine and an urgent problem.

::: dm
House Orlemont buys finds from the [[Undercroft]] and sells the best of them, quietly, to the [[Ember Court]], whose
agents keep a house two doors down disguised as a wool factor's. Orlemont's head, an old woman who never leaves her
counting-room, knows exactly what the Margrave's diggers are looking for in the [[Glass Waste]], and is pricing it.

**Hooks.**
- A Tesk barge-master hires a crew to find out why three of his barges have come in light, every time from the same
  stretch of river below the [[Lady of the Weir]]'s water.
- A Velloure factor wants a parcel collected from a ship in Wetside and carried to Tollside without the Watch looking
  in it. It's a Lantern prince's poison, ordered by a guildmaster for his rival before the election.
:::
