The Ferrymen's Guild is [[Hask]]'s oldest guild and its richest: the only body allowed to carry paying passengers
across the [[Ambre]] at [[Hask Ford]], and therefore the reason Hask exists. Its guildmaster sits first on the Ford
Council and speaks first in every debate. Its guildhall at the top of the Toll Stair is a squat, ugly tower of
river-stone, the oldest building in the city, and nobody would dare call it ugly to a ferryman.

## The guild

- **Ferrymaster Dunnet Reed** leads it: a grey-whiskered Riverfolk halfling who has crossed the river more than
  forty thousand times and says he remembers each one. He is cautious, slow to speak and very hard to bribe, which in
  Hask makes him a curiosity.
- **The masters**, about sixty, each own a licensed ferry, marked with a bronze foot on the bow.
- **The mates**, several hundred, crew them. Most are Riverfolk; the guild has taken halflings since its first
  charter, because they are good with boats and the [[Ferryman]] seems to like them.
- **The toll-clerks** at the stairs count the copper and the passengers and pass both to the guild.

## Rules and customs

- Every ferry throws its first coin of each crossing to the Ferryman.
- No ferry crosses without a master aboard, and no master crosses drunk (both rules are kept better than most).
- A ferry that loses a passenger to the river stops work for three days and its master pays the family.
- On [[Ferry Night]] the guild leads the city's barges to midstream and pays the Ferryman's tithe, the Ferrymaster
  himself pouring it from the bow.

## Power

Every toll is the guild's, minus the city's share. It owns warehouses on both banks, lends to the Council, and can
stop the city dead by tying up its boats; it has done so twice, and won both times. When the guild and the Lord
Warden disagree, the Lord Warden usually finds he was wrong.

::: dm
Someone in the guild has been skimming the tithe. For three years, a little copper has gone missing from the vault
under the guildhall between the counting and Ferry Night, replaced with lead toes dipped in copper. Reed suspects; he
does not know who. If the tithe is short this year, he believes the Ferryman will take the difference in lives.

**Hooks.**
- Reed hires a crew quietly to find the skimmer before Ferry Night, without telling the Council, the Watch or the

- The Theocracy's mission is offering ferry masters gold to carry passengers with a sun-blessing instead of a coin.
  Two have agreed. One has drowned.
