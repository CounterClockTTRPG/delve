Asheth is the forge: fire put to work, metal made useful, a thing made right. She is the god of smiths and of every
guild that swears its apprentices in, and the dwarves' favourite of the twelve [[Great Gods]]. Her church is rich,
quiet and conservative, run by guildmasters as much as priests, and in [[Hask]] it is said that no one gets onto the
Ford Council without her temple's nod.

## What her people do

- **The Relighting.** Through the [[Hollow Days]] every forge is banked low, never out. On 1 Ashen it is raked, fed and
  brought to full heat with a prayer, and the first thing made is a single nail, given to the temple. Asheth's temples
  have barrels of them.
- **Guild oaths.** Apprentices and masters swear to their guild with a hand held over the forge, close enough to blister.
  The scar is shown at guild meetings; a master's palm is a thing of some pride.
- **The maker's mark.** Every honest piece of work is marked. Selling unmarked work, or work with a hidden flaw, is a
  sin as well as a crime.
- **Taboos.** Letting a forge go out without rite; quenching a blade in anything but water, oil or the smith's own
  sweat; mocking a crafter's work in their workshop.
- **Sayings.** "Hot or not at all." "She tests in the fire." A good piece of work is "Asheth-true."

## Her clerics

A cleric of Asheth can shoe a horse, mend a mail shirt, and find the weak point in a door. They wear leather aprons over
their armour and carry hammers, and their healing feels like a burn going cool. They are lawful in the guildsman's way:
contracts, standards, rules about who may make what. In a party they are the one who appraises the loot, mends the
gear, and grumbles about the quality of every lock. They are fascinated by Thaumarch craft and are the first to say a
[[Brass Servants|brass servant]] should be studied, not smashed.

::: dm
Asheth's church holds that the brass servants of the [[Thaumarchy]] are her work, made by craftsmen through her art,
not by sorcery, and therefore hers to keep. They buy every stopped servant they can, and in a locked hall under their
temple in [[Templegate]] they have managed to make one move a finger.

Hooks:
- A guildmaster's scarred palm is a fake, burned on by a hired man. If it comes out, his guild vote is void, and the
  vote that elected [[Benedek Sallow]] is one short.
- Asheth's temple wants a brass servant out of the [[Undercroft]] whole, and pays double for one with its head
  attached. The last crew to try found one that did not want to come.
:::
