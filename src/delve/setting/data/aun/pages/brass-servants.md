The **brass servants** of the [[Thaumarchy]] carried water, swept floors, guarded doors, rowed galleys and fought
wars. On the night of the [[Unmaking]] they all stopped at once, wherever they were. They are found in ruins still
standing at their tasks: a doorkeeper with one hand raised, a cook bent over a cold stove, a soldier halfway through a
blow.

## What they are like

Most stand a little taller than a man, built of dull brass plates over a frame of darker metal, with smooth faces
that have no mouths and two eye-holes of clouded glass. The finer ones were made in the likeness of the Thaumarch
they served. Smaller ones, the size of a dog or a child, did fetching and carrying. Some were enormous: the [[Glass Waste]] has a pair of legs that stand forty feet high, with nothing above the hips.

Every servant has a House sigil stamped behind the left ear, and an opening in the chest, a hand's breadth across,
that is always empty.

## What they are worth

- For scrap, a few gold pieces: Thaumarch brass does not take a dent from a hammer, and smiths of [[Asheth]] prize it
  for tools.
- Whole, to the [[Collegium of the Lamp]], a great deal more. [[Ilse Varrow]] has three in the Collegium cellars and is
  always buying.
- In the [[Lantern Principalities]], a whole servant standing in a hall is a mark of rank.

## Superstitions

- Never speak to one. They might hear.
- Never stand in front of one with its hand raised.
- In [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]], farmers who plough up a brass hand bury it again at once, palm down.

::: dm
Some wake. The empty opening in the chest held a fragment of bound power; a servant that gets one back, from a
spell, a Thaumarch lamp or a nearby [[Seven Lanterns|Lantern]], takes up exactly the task it was doing on the night of
the Unmaking, and will not stop.

- In the [[Undercroft]], near the Pale Tower Lantern, brass servants are waking one by one: a doorkeeper that asks a
  password in a dead tongue, a cook that wants someone to serve, a soldier finishing its blow.
- The Margrave's diggers have uncovered a brass hand the size of a barn in the Waste, attached to something larger.
  If it is a servant and it wakes, it will resume its last task; the [[Grey Legions]] would like to know what that
  was first.
- A servant made in its master's likeness answers to that master's House name; Lanterner nobles know the names.
:::
