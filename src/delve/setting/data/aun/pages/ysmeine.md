Ysmeine is the Lady of the [[Thornwold]], the ruler (if that is the word) of its [[Elves]]. She is very old. She
remembers [[Ishavel]] when its streets were lit, and the night it burned, and she has said so to perhaps a dozen
humans in twelve hundred years. Most people who live beside the forest have never seen her; many doubt she exists. The
toll-keepers of the Thornway speak of her as if she were standing just behind them.

## The Lady

Those who have met her describe her differently: tall or slight, young or old, dressed in grey or in green. All agree
on her eyes, which are the colour of thorn-wood, and her voice, which is quiet and has no hurry in it at all. She
speaks in riddles, not to be clever but because she answers the question you should have asked, not the one you did.
She laughs at odd moments. She forgets that humans die.

## What she does

- Keeps the rules of the Thornway, and the forest's peace, as she has since before Calder was a kingdom.
- Refuses every embassy. Kings of [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]] send one at their crowning; it is met at the forest's
  edge, given wine and a riddle, and sent home.
- Sometimes sends for a human. The summons is a thorn twig left on a pillow. Those who answer it come back changed,
  and those who refuse it have bad luck until they go.
- Watches the [[Seven Lanterns]] and those who seek them.

## Reputation

To Calderish peasants she is a fairy-tale queen, kind to children and cruel to woodcutters. To the
[[Sunward Church]] she is a pagan witch. To the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] she is the last living witness of the
[[Thaumarchy]], and every scholar in [[Hask]] would give an eye for an hour of her time; one, in 1092, did.

## At the table

Ysmeine is a source of wonder, warning and very old knowledge, delivered slant. She does not give quests; she gives
riddles, and the quest is working out what she meant. She is not on anyone's side.

::: dm
**What she remembers.** Ysmeine knew Ysarre the Bright, the last Thaumarch-Queen, and warned her against the
[[Great Binding]]. She carried something out of the burning city at the [[Unmaking]] and buried it in the forest's heart.
She will not say what, but she is afraid of the Margrave's dig in the [[Glass Waste]], because she knows what else is
buried there.

**Hooks.**
- A thorn twig appears on a player character's pillow.
- Ysmeine asks the party to carry a riddle to [[Teodric Vael]]. It is a warning. He will understand it, and not thank
  the messengers.
- A Collegium scholar pays the party to bring Ysmeine a list of questions. She answers only one, the one not on the
  list.
:::
