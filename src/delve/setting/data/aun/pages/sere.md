Sere sees through screens. She is the god of what is known and not said: the scholar's notes, the spy's report, the
wizard's formula, the family's buried shame. Her symbol is an eye behind a lattice, and her temples are built the same
way, dim rooms seen through carved wooden screens. Scribes and scholars pray to her; so do the few wizards of the
Reaches, which is why ordinary people make the sign against the evil eye when her priests go by.

She is blamed for the [[Thaumarchy]]. Folk say she taught the sorcerer-princes, and that the [[Unmaking]] was the other
gods punishing her pupils. Her priests do not deny it; they say only that she taught, and others chose.

## What her people do

- **The lattice box.** At her temples, a worshipper whispers a secret through a wooden lattice into a closed box. The
  secret is the offering. Nobody is supposed to open the boxes.
- **The Shuttering.** On the last night of Seremark, at the end of autumn, the faithful close every shutter and say
  nothing until dawn. Gossip that night is a sin.
- **Taboos.** Burning a book, any book; telling a secret given in confidence; claiming to know what you don't. The last
  catches more of her priests than the other two.
- **Sayings.** "She sees; she doesn't say." "Ask the lattice." A scholar who reaches the end of what they know writes
  "Sere keeps the rest" in the margin.

## Her clerics

A cleric of Sere is a librarian with a mace. They read old scripts, keep archives, copy what others would burn, and
never quite tell the whole of anything. They are allowed inside the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] as advisers and watched
there by everyone. In a party they are the one who reads the inscription, and the one who doesn't mention the last
line. The [[Sunward Church]]'s inquisitors look at them very hard and have so far stopped short of burning them.

::: dm
Somebody does open the lattice boxes. Sere's senior priests read every whispered secret and keep them, sorted and
indexed, in a sealed library under her temple in [[Templegate]]. They trade the worst of them, very rarely, for
Thaumarch writings. [[Ilse Varrow]] suspects; [[Magdal Crane]] knows and has not yet said.

Hooks:
- A merchant whispered a crime into a lattice box ten years ago. Now someone is blackmailing him with it, and he wants
  to know how a god's box leaked.
- A priest of Sere wants a book saved from a Sunward burning in [[Candor]]. The book is dull; the letter hidden in its
  binding is not.
:::
