The Quiet Hand is [[Hask]]'s thieves' guild, though it would not use the word. It calls itself a society for the
regulation of quiet trades: theft, fencing, smuggling, forgery, the begging-pitches of the Stews, and the protection
of anyone who pays for it. It is the tenth guild of Hask, and in some years the richest. It is at war with no one,
openly.

## How it works

- **The Widow** heads it. Nobody knows who she is. She is never seen, only quoted.
- **The Fingers** are her lieutenants, five of them, each with a ward: the [[Stews]], [[Wetside]], the Hards and the
  river, [[Tollside]], and the roofs. A Finger is the person to ask for anything the Hand sells.
- **The licensed** are the thieves, fences and forgers who pay the Hand a third of their take for the right to work.
  Each carries a **knuckle**, a small bone token with a notch for every year paid.
- **The unlicensed** are found in the river with a stone in their right hand.

## The Hand's rules

1. No killing in the course of business, unless the business is killing, which costs more.
2. No robbing a house that pays the Hand. A brass hand nailed above a door means it pays.
3. No working on Tallow Lane, at the Ferryman's shrine, or on [[Ferry Night]].
4. No talking to the Watch, the House of the Scale or the Collegium.
5. The Widow's word ends an argument.

## Dealing with the Hand

A crew back from the [[Undercroft]] with something they'd rather not show the tollkeeper can sell it through a
Finger, at a fair price minus a fair cut. A crew that needs a lock opened, a door watched, a forged pass to
[[Vaelholt]] or a man found can hire it the same way. A crew that crosses the Hand will notice: first the beggars stop
talking to them, then the ferry-mates, then their landlady.

The Hand keeps a clean peace in the Stews and a dirty one everywhere else. Hask's Watch takes its money and leaves
it alone. So, mostly, does the Lord Warden.

::: dm
The [[Widow]] is Clemence Sallow, the Lord Warden's widowed sister. She took the Hand twenty years ago by poisoning
the old head at his own table, and she has used it since to keep her brother in office. Two guildmasters on the Ford
Council owe her their lives or their debts. The Hand's real rivals are the [[Ember Court]]'s agents, who are buying
up its fences one by one, and the [[Open Mouth]], which has been killing its beggars.

**Hooks.**
- A Finger offers the crew a licence for nothing, if they'll steal a ledger from the House of the Scale.
- The Finger of the roofs has gone missing. The other four each think one of the others did it; one of them is
  right, and in Ember Court pay.
:::
