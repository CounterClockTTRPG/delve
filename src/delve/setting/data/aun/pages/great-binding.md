The **Great Binding** was the last work of the [[Thaumarchy]], and its worst idea.

## Ysarre the Bright

The last Thaumarch-Queen, **Ysarre the Bright**, came to the throne of [[Ishavel]] young and is said to have been the
greatest sorcerer the empire ever raised. She found an empire that still had to pray: for harvest, for safe seas, for
the dead to rest, for the sun to rise kindly. Prayers were answered at the gods' pleasure, not the empire's. Ysarre
proposed to end that.

## The Lanterns

Over forty years she and the [[Thaumarchy|Seven Houses]] forged the [[Seven Lanterns]], one for each House. Each was to
hold a share of the binding, so that when all seven were lit together the [[Great Gods]] would be drawn into them and
made to serve. Grain would grow by order. Storms would turn at command. The empire would never need to pray again.

The Houses worked on their Lanterns in secret from each other, and each tried to make its own Lantern the one that
held the most. Some Thaumarchs objected. They are not remembered by name; the Houses saw to that.

## The closing night

The Binding was to close on a single night, with the seven Lanterns lit together in Ishavel. Nobody living knows how
far it got. What everyone knows is what happened next: the [[Unmaking]].

## What people say now

- The [[Sunward Church]] teaches that the Binding was the sin of pride, and that [[Aurel]] judged it. They do not say
  why Aurel's judgement burned the farmers as well.
- [[Sere]]'s priests teach that knowledge was not the sin; the use was. Few people listen.
- In the [[Lantern Principalities]] Ysarre is still spoken of with awe, and sometimes with pride.
- Peasants say "as bright as Ysarre" of someone too clever to live.

::: dm
The Binding nearly worked. For an hour at least, some gods were held. That hour is why they withdrew afterwards: they
do not trust the world any more, and the [[Small Gods]] grew in the room they left.

- Scraps of the Binding's working survive. Anyone who reads one aloud draws a god's attention, briefly and entirely.
- The [[Ember Crown]] was made with Binding craft on a small scale, to hold a lesser power. It is proof that the
  method can still work, which is why the [[Margravate]] digs in the Waste.
- Ysarre's body was never found. The Lantern princes each hold a "relic" of her; none is genuine.
:::
