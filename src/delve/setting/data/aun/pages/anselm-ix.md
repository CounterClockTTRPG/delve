Anselm IX is the Hierarch of [[Aurel]]: head of the [[Sunward Church]] and ruler of the [[Sunward Theocracy]]. He was
born Orlan Vey, a tanner's son from the coast, and raised to the office in 1186 at thirty-two, after the death of the
old Hierarch Anselm VIII. He is the youngest Hierarch in three centuries, and the first in living memory chosen for his
preaching rather than his family.

## The man

Thin, sunburnt, with cropped black hair, a hoarse voice and burn scars on both hands from the tannery vats. He wears a
plain white robe and a brass scale on a cord; the golden regalia stays in its chest in [[Candor]]. He eats bread and
olives, sleeps four hours, and walks the streets of the capital at noon without a guard.

When he preaches, crowds weep. He talks about the gods' anger at the [[Unmaking]], about mortals who tried to make the
gods their servants, and about the small gods as the leftover rot of that age: demons who live on offerings that
belong to heaven. He believes all of it.

## What he does

- Has ordered every small-god shrine in the Theocracy burned within five years. Inquisitors report progress monthly.
- Has doubled the Church's missions abroad, including the House of the Scale in [[Hask]].
- Preaches openly against the [[Margravate]] as a land ruled by a dead man held up by sorcery, and calls for a holy
  war. Calder's barons listen with interest.
- Has stopped the old Hierarchs' custom of buying peace from the pirates of [[Gullhaven]].

## Reputation

The poor of the Theocracy love him. Its bishops, who chose him because they thought a preacher would be easy to
steer, are frightened of him. Abroad he is a name to worry about: in Hask, in Calder's castles and in every village
with a shrine at the well.

## At the table

Anselm IX is not a hypocrite, which makes him harder to deal with. He can be bargained with only in his own coin:
proof, piety and enemies of the faith. Adventurers who bring him a cultist of the [[Open Mouth]] will be rewarded;
those who protect a small god will be hunted.

::: dm
**He wants a war.** Anselm believes a war against the Margrave will unite the Reaches under Aurel's scale, and he is
looking for a cause. He also has a secret he does not know he has: his mother kept a hearth-god in the tannery wall,
and as a boy he fed it. It still lives in the ruins of the tannery and remembers him by name.

**Hooks.**
- An inquisitor offers the party a fortune to find the tannery god before the Hierarch's enemies among the bishops do.
- Anselm, walking the streets at noon, is attacked; the party saves him (or are blamed). Either way he wants to know
  who sent the killer. The bishops did.
- He sends the party to Hask with a gift for [[Magdal Crane]]: a sealed box with orders to start a war.
:::
