Deephorn is the greatest of the dwarven holds of the [[Hornwall]], cut into a horn-shaped peak above the source of the
[[Ambre]]. From outside it is a gate of carved stone in a cliff, two towers and a smoke-plume. Inside it goes down for
a mile, in halls, forges, cisterns, mushroom-farms and mines, and it is older than the [[Thaumarchy]]. Its king is
[[Barrek Stonesong]].

## The hold

- **The Horn Gate.** The great door, carved with the faces of every king of Deephorn. There is a blank face at the end
  of the row, waiting for the next.
- **The Hall of Asheth.** A forge-temple at the hold's heart, where a fire has burned without stopping since before
  humans had iron. [[Asheth]]'s priests tend it; the best smiths of the Reaches work at its edge.
- **The Long Book.** A library, really: the record of every debt, oath, insult and kindness done to Deephorn since its
  founding. Grudges are entered in red. Strangers are entered on arrival. Many of the red pages concern the
  [[Margravate]].
- **The Cold Halls.** The tombs, lower down, where the dead lie in ice-cut niches. Dwarves honour [[Vorm]] there.
- **The Outer Market.** The only part of the hold most humans see: a hall inside the gate where Hold-folk, Hask and
  Calderish traders buy dwarven steel, tools and gems, and sell grain, wine, wool and timber.

## Custom

- A visitor gives his name, his father's name and his business at the gate. All three are written in the Long Book.
  A false name is a grudge.
- Dwarves never sell a weapon they made to someone they do not know; a trader must be introduced by someone already in
  the Book, which makes introductions a trade of their own.
- Debts are inherited. A human whose grandfather cheated Deephorn will be told so.
- Prices: a dwarven sword, 60 gold and an introduction; a dwarven lock, 20 gold, with the key's twin kept in the hold.

## The lost mines

The eastern mines of Deephorn, called **Garrowdeep**, ran far under the eastern Hornwall. In 1163 orc tribes in the
Margrave's service broke in and drove the dwarves out. The Margrave now mines iron there. Deephorn has not forgotten,
and every dwarf in it can tell you how many dwarves died, by name.

## At the table

Deephorn is a place to buy the best steel in the Reaches, to be hired for a job dwarves will not do themselves, or to
settle (or inherit) a grudge. It is courteous, exact and slow, and does not forgive.

::: dm
**The king's plan.** Barrek Stonesong is quietly hiring outsiders, especially Hask crews, to raid Garrowdeep, map
the Margrave's workings and find a way in for a dwarven army. He will not risk his own people until he is sure. He
pays in steel.

**Hooks.**
- The party's names are found already written in the Long Book, in red, beside a grudge two hundred years old. They
  must find out who wrote them, and why.
- A smith of the Hall of Asheth has made a sword that will not stop being hot. He needs it carried out of the hold
  before it melts the anvil.
- A merchant in [[Hask]] wants an introduction to the Book, and will pay the party to get themselves entered first.
:::
