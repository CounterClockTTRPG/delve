The Sunward Church is the church of [[Aurel]], the Sun-Judge, and the most powerful church in the Reaches. It spread
from the south-west coast around 500 RY, took the crown of the south-west in 612 when Hierarch Anselm I made himself
its king, and has ruled the [[Sunward Theocracy]] from [[Candor]] ever since. Outside the Theocracy it keeps missions
in every city of size; in [[Hask]], its mission is the House of the Scale, under Inquisitor [[Magdal Crane]], and its
temple in [[Templegate]] is the newest and largest there.

## What it teaches

Aurel judges. Every oath is sworn before him, every soul weighed by him at death, every law a shadow of his. The
world went wrong when the [[Thaumarchy]] tried to bind the gods; it is put right by obedience, order and judgement.
The Church teaches that the [[Small Gods]] are demons that grew in the dark the great gods left, feeding on the
offerings that should have gone to Aurel. They are to be starved, and their shrines burned.

## How it is ordered

- **The Hierarch**, head of church and state, raised for life by the prelates. Now [[Anselm IX]], young, brilliant and
  zealous.
- **The prelates**, a few dozen, who govern the Church's provinces and the Theocracy's cities.
- **Sun-priests**, who keep the temples, sing the noon office, and judge disputes in Aurel's name.
- **The Inquisitors of the Scale**, who hunt, in order of zeal, wizards, small gods and the servants of [[Nhul]]. An
  inquisitor answers to the Hierarch alone and may summon any priest, magistrate or soldier of the Theocracy to help.

## Customs

- Sunward temples, houses and towns are whitewashed every spring. A dirty wall is a sign of a careless soul.
- All work stops at noon for the office of the Sun. In Candor, a cart in the street at noon is turned over.
- Oaths are sworn with a hand in sunlight; an oath sworn at night or indoors means nothing to a Sunward court.
- At the [[Sun's Judgement]], midsummer, Church courts sit in every city of the Theocracy and sentence the year's
  heretics. The burnings are public.

## Its rivals

The priests of [[Hollin]] and [[Velis]] tolerate small gods, and the Church despises them for it in sermons and needs
them in practice, since peasants will not give up either. [[Fennick]]'s people bargain with small gods, and the Church
fines them. The [[Lantern Principalities]], with their court sorcerers, are an open scandal, and the [[Margravate]] an
abomination: a man kept alive by a Thaumarch relic. The Church fought him in the [[Seven Years' War]] and has not
forgiven the stalemate.

::: dm
**The new Hierarch** is turning the Church from a power into a crusade. Anselm IX has doubled the inquisitors, begun
buying Drowned Coast captains, and is preaching openly against small gods in Hask and Calder. He wants a war, and he
does not much mind with whom.

**The Church's private fear.** A few old prelates keep count, and by their count Sunward miracles have grown rarer
for a century while the small gods grow stronger. They suspect, and do not say, that burning shrines may be starving
the wrong god. Whether they are right is the DM's to decide.

**Hooks.**
- An inquisitor hires the party to find a small god's shrine in the Hask sewers. The shrine is [[Mother Gutter]]'s.
- A sun-priest in a Calder village has been leaving bread at the well-god's stone at night. He begs the party not to
  tell anyone, and an inquisitor is due in two days.
- The Church offers a pardon for any crime to a crew that brings back proof of what the Margrave found in the
  [[Glass Waste]].
:::
