Nhul is hunger without end: the rot in the grain, the worm in the wound, the mouth that eats and is never full. He is
the twelfth of the [[Great Gods]] and the one nobody prays to in daylight. His worship is forbidden in every realm of
the Reaches, and on this, at least, the [[Sunward Church]], the jarls and the Lantern princes agree.

His month, the last of the year, is not called by his name. People say "the Nameless Month" and make a small fist,
closing the mouth. The [[Hollow Days]] that follow are thought to be his too, when doors are shut and fires kept and
nobody begins a journey.

## What people do

Ordinary people do things to keep Nhul away, not to please him.

- **The closed fist.** Saying his name, or any word for him, is followed by closing the hand. Children who say it on a
  dare have their mouths washed out with salt.
- **Salt at the threshold.** A line of salt under the door in the Nameless Month keeps out "the hungry". Rot found in
  the larder is burned, not thrown away.
- **No drawn mouths.** Painting or carving an open mouth is forbidden in most cities. Masons carve gargoyles with
  closed lips, and in [[Hask]] the sewer grates are made with bars across, so that no drain looks like a mouth.
- **Sayings.** "Shut it." (Said to anyone who mentions him.) "Nothing feeds the Nameless." A miser is "a little
  mouth."

## His clerics

There are no lawful priests of Nhul. His clergy are the cult of the [[Open Mouth]], hidden in cellars and sewers,
feeding the Mouth with the living. A player character cannot be a cleric of Nhul in any game meant to last. Those who
serve him grow thin however much they eat, and their prayers are answered generously, which is the worst sign of all.
In Hask, [[Mother Gutter]]'s beggars hunt them, and a cultist caught by the beggars is rarely handed to the Watch.

::: dm
The cult teaches that at the [[Unmaking]] every god but Nhul answered the [[Great Binding]] in anger, and that Nhul
answered by eating: that the burning of [[Ishavel]] was his feast, and the [[Glass Waste]] his empty plate. Whether
this is true is the DM's to decide. The cult believes that the [[Seven Lanterns]], lit again, would bind every god but
one.

Hooks:
- A baker in [[Wetside]] is selling bread that never seems to go stale. It is very popular. It is also how the cult marks
  who to take.
- The party find an open mouth carved inside an old Thaumarch door in the [[Undercroft]], under the sign of one of the
  Seven Houses. Someone very old drew it, long before the cult existed.
:::
