Aldmoor is the royal city of [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]]: grey stone on a long ridge above the river Alde, ringed by
walls built for a larger city than the one inside them. At the top of the ridge stands **Shepherd's Keep**, the
castle of the kings since [[Bertil III|Bertil's]] ancestor Aldric built its first tower in 341 RY. At the bottom are
the wool-yards, the tanneries and the smell, which reaches the keep on a south wind.

## The city

- **Shepherd's Keep.** The royal castle. By ancient right the king's flock grazes the outer bailey, so visiting envoys
  pick their way through sheep to reach the throne. Aldric's crook hangs above the throne in an iron cage.
- **The Wool Hall.** A vast timber hall by the river where the spring and autumn fairs are held. Hask merchants buy
  fleece here by the cartload; prices set in the Wool Hall are argued over in [[Hask]] a week later.
- **Barons' Row.** The fortified townhouses of the great lords, each with its own gate, guards and grudge. Since the
  king fell ill, the Row has been full, and the guards have doubled.
- **The Hollin Church.** The oldest temple, low and thatched, where the kings are crowned. Next door the new temple of
  [[Aurel]], white and twice the size, built with Sunward money after the [[Seven Years' War]].
- **Velis's Yard.** The city graveyard on the north slope, where the old kings lie in plain graves under turf. Their
  sheep graze it too.
- **The Fair Field.** Outside the east gate: tournaments in summer, hangings in winter, the horse market all year.

## Custom

- Every citizen may graze six sheep on the common below the walls. Fights over which six are frequent.
- Knights entering the city must leave their lances at the gate-house and pay a copper for each; the lances are
  returned when they leave, if they leave.
- The king's crier reads news at the Wool Hall door every morning. Lately he reads that the king is well.

## At the table

Aldmoor is a city of courtiers, wool-merchants, idle knights, spies and anxious servants. Adventurers come for
patronage, for the summer tournament, or because a baron on Barons' Row needs something done that no one in his
household can be seen doing.

::: dm
**The king is dying.** Bertil III has not left his bedchamber since the Hollow Days. His physician, a quiet man paid
by Baron Wystan Morrow, reports to his master before he reports to the Crown Council. The crier's news is written by
the chamberlain, who has not decided whom to back.

**Hooks.**
- The crook has gone missing from its cage and been replaced by a copy. Whoever holds the real crook at the crowning
  will have a strong argument. The party is hired to find it, by two different people.
- A Calderish knight who lost everything in the lists hires the party to help him win it back in the [[Undercroft]],
  then to help him win it back at the tournament, by cheating.
- Sheep are dying in the outer bailey, one a night, drained of blood. The keep's servants blame the [[Open Mouth]].
:::
