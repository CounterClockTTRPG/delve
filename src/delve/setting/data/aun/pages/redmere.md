Redmere is a wide, shallow lake on the border between [[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]]'s march and the [[Margravate]],
ringed by reed-beds, bog and black peat. Its water is the colour of weak tea in summer and of rust in autumn, from the
iron in the peat. Since 1110 nobody in either country calls it iron.

## The battle

On 9 Karsk 1110 RY, in the [[Seven Years' War]], the armies of Calder and the [[Sunward Theocracy]] met the
[[Grey Legions]] on the lake's western shore. They fought from dawn into the reeds and out into the water, and by
evening some eleven thousand men were dead and neither side could advance. The war ended in stalemate the next
year. The border was drawn where the armies stood, and so it runs through the middle of the lake.

## The place now

- **The Stakes.** A line of tarred posts across the water, marking the border. Calderish boats keep west of it,
  Margravate boats east. Eel-fishers on both sides set their traps on the line and argue about it.
- **The Grey Mounds.** Long, low barrows on the western shore where the priests of [[Velis]] buried the dead after the
  battle: all the dead, of every side, in the same graves, as the Grey Mother wants. A small Velis chapel stands among
  them.
- **The peat.** Peat-cutters still find the war in the bog: helmets, spearheads, a hand in a gauntlet, perfectly kept.
  Relic-dealers from [[Hask]] buy them.
- **Staithe.** The one village on the Calder shore: a jetty, a tavern called the Nine Eels, and a Calderish border keep
  under Lord Hamo Strake's banner.

## Custom

- No one rows on Redmere on the anniversary of the battle. Those who do hear the drums.
- Fishers throw the first eel of the season back, "for the lads".
- The Velis chapel's priest will bury anything brought to her from the bog, with the full rite, and charges nothing.

## At the table

Redmere is a frontier, a graveyard and a place where the next war will probably start: the Grey Legions mustered
across the water in 1187 and are camped there still. Adventurers come to spy, to smuggle, to dig, or to lay a ghost.

::: dm
**The drowned company.** A company of Theocracy knights, about two hundred, was driven off a hidden shelf into deep water at
dusk and drowned in full armour. They were never buried, because they were never found. They walk the lakebed, and on
the battle's anniversary they come ashore looking for the rite Velis owes them. The Margrave knows; he wants them for
his Legions.

**Hooks.**
- A peat-cutter has found a knight in the bog with a sealed letter from the Hierarch of 1110 in his gauntlet. Several
  people want it, including the present Hierarch.
- The Grey Legions are paying fishermen to sound the lake's depths. The party is asked by Lord Strake to find out why.
- Someone is stealing bodies from the Grey Mounds, one a month.
:::
