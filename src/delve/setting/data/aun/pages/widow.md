The Widow is the head of the [[Quiet Hand]], [[Hask]]'s thieves' guild, and nobody knows who she is. Her Fingers
speak for her; her orders come in writing, on black-edged mourning paper, sealed with a plain thumbprint in grey wax.
She has ruled the Hand for about twenty years, since the night its old head died of something he ate at his own
table.

## What Hask says about her

- She is old. She is young. She is three people. She is a man.
- She sits in a different house every night, and the beggars of [[Mother Gutter]] carry her letters.
- She was widowed by the river, and that is why she never crosses it.
- She has never broken her word, which is more than can be said for the Ford Council.

All of the stories agree on one thing: the Widow does not forgive. People who cheat the Hand are found in the Ambre
near the tanneries, with a stone closed in their right hand.

## Dealing with her

A crew will never meet the Widow unless she wants them to, and then they won't know they have. Her messages arrive
in odd ways: a note tucked into change at the [[Drowned Toe]], a child who recites a sentence and runs. She hires
crews for jobs the Hand can't be seen to do, pays exactly what she promised, and remembers anyone who talks.

::: dm
The Widow is **Clemence Sallow**, the Lord Warden's younger sister, about fifty-five, small, grey, plainly dressed,
known in Tollside society as a pious widow who gives to the Grey House of [[Velis]] and plays cards badly. Her husband,
a Tesk factor, drowned on a ferry in 1166; she believes it was murder disguised as the [[Ferryman]]'s work, and took the
Hand to find out whose. She found out, and that man is now one of the guildmasters who votes for her brother because
she lets him live.

She guards her brother's office more fiercely than he does. She regards [[Magdal Crane]] as the most dangerous person
in Hask, and she is right.

**Hooks.**
- A crew that does well for the Hand is invited to a card evening at a pious widow's house on Tollside. Someone there
  will ask them a careful question.
- The Widow wants proof that the Draper guildmaster takes Sunward gold before Ferry Night. She will pay in licences,
  secrets and silence.
:::
