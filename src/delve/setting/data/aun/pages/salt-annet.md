Salt Annet is the strongest captain on the [[Drowned Coast]]: four ships of her own, a dozen more that sail when she
asks, and the last word at the Captains' Table in [[Gullhaven]]. She is about fifty, short, broad, grey-haired and
brown as a nut, with a voice that carries from one end of the Mole to the other. She got her name, people say, because
she was born on a salt boat, because she cures her enemies like fish, or because she once drank a pint of seawater on
a bet. She lets all three stories stand.

## Her ships

Her flagship is the *Low Water*, a long, shallow-drafted raider that can cross sandbars no galley dares and hide in
coves at the bottom of the tide. Her other ships are the *Second Thoughts*, the *Debt* and the *Mother's Patience*.
Her crews are paid on time, flogged rarely and hanged for theft from each other. They are the best-run crews on the
coast, and other captains hate them for it.

## How she works

Annet takes ships from the [[Sunward Theocracy]] and the [[Lantern Principalities]], and leaves Gullhaven's own trade
alone. She has never burned a village and has hanged two captains who did. She is a devout follower of the
[[Drowned Bell]]: she will not sail the morning after it rings, and she leaves a tenth of every prize in the water
at the Bell Chapel. She prays to [[Marrah]] too, but only aloud and only when other people are listening.

She is not cruel. She is thorough. A man who cheats her gets one warning, and the warning is his ship.

## Dealing with her

- Annet will hire anyone useful for one voyage. She tests new hands by sending them up the mast in harbour and
  watching who helps them down.
- She pays in Lantern wicks, [[Ferryman's Toe|Hask toes]], pearls, or favours. The favours are worth the most and cost the most.
- She hates the Sunward Church with a thoroughness that frightens even her crews. Never mention an inquisitor kindly
  in her hearing.

::: dm
**The truth.** Annet was a novice of the [[Sunward Church]] in Candor until she was nineteen, when her inquisitor
uncle burned her mother for leaving bread at a river shrine. She has spent thirty years taking her revenge one ship
at a time. Her uncle, Inquisitor Gaspard Lume, is still alive, now a prelate in [[Candor]], and she wants him.

She also knows that [[Anselm IX]] is buying her captains, and is quietly letting him do it, to find out which ones will
sell.

**Hooks.**
- Annet hires the party to go into Candor as harmless travellers and bring her uncle out alive. She is very precise
  about "alive".
- A Sunward agent offers the party a fortune to sink the *Low Water* at anchor. Annet already knows about the offer
  and wants to see what they do.
- The Drowned Bell rang and Annet sailed anyway. It is the first time in thirty years. Her crew wants to know why;
  so does she.
:::
