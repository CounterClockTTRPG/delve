Old Mazzar is the best-known guide in the [[Glass Waste]], and the most expensive, and the least sober. He works out of
Shardsend, on the Waste's south-west rim, from a corner table in the tavern that has no name, and he has been going
into the glass for longer than most [[Glass-Pickers]] have been alive. He is somewhere past sixty, lean as a strap,
burnt nearly black by the sun, with pale Lanterner eyes and hands so scarred they look like knotted rope. He is missing
two toes, and will tell you he paid them to the [[Ferryman]].

## His stories

Mazzar talks. He has slept in the Bowl of [[Ishavel]]. He has seen the [[Glass Saint]] face to face, and she looked
like his mother. He has walked through a hall of a hundred standing [[Brass Servants]] and heard one of them
breathing. He was a prince's son in [[Tessarine]], a sailor out of [[Gullhaven]], a soldier in the
[[Seven Years' War]], and a bridegroom four times. All of his stories are lies, and every one of them has a true part
in it, and the art of hiring Mazzar is working out which part.

## Hiring him

- Mazzar charges ten silver a day, twice a usual guide's, and half the finds. He does not haggle; he drinks until you
  pay.
- Once in the glass, he is sober, quiet and very careful. Clients who have walked with him say they have never been so
  safe or so frightened.
- He knows cisterns no other picker knows. He keeps the oath and will not tell where they are, but he will take you to
  them.
- He will not go within ten miles of the Margrave's dig. He will not say why.

## At the table

Mazzar is the guide a party hires when they want to go somewhere nobody else will take them. He is good company, a
terrible liar in the sense of being a constant one, and the only man in Shardsend who will not try to cheat them on
the Waste. In town he will cheat them freely.

::: dm
**The true part.** Mazzar was a digger for the [[Margravate]] forty years ago, on an earlier and smaller dig in the
same place as the Vael Dig. The diggers found the top of the same buried thing, and the Margrave's men killed the crew
to keep it quiet. Mazzar alone got away, walked out across the Waste with no water, and was led to a cistern by
something he still calls "her". He has lied about everything since, to hide one story that is true.

He knows the buried thing is a brass hand, and roughly what it is attached to. He knows it moved once, forty years
ago, when the crew broke into the chamber.

**Hooks.**
- A grey-wax letter (see [[Ember Court]]) arrives for Mazzar while the party is drinking with him. He goes pale and
  asks them to leave for the Waste tonight.
- Mazzar will guide the party anywhere, free, if they will go to the dig with him and see whether the hand has
  closed.
- A rival picker swears Mazzar is not Mazzar at all: the real Mazzar died in the Waste forty years ago, and the man in
  the corner came walking out wearing his name.
:::
