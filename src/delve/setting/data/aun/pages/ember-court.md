The Ember Court is the household of [[Teodric Vael]], the Undying Margrave, and its reach goes far beyond his hall in
[[Vaelholt]]. Officially it is a court like any other: chamberlains, secretaries, cupbearers, tutors. In practice it
is the finest network of spies and assassins in the Reaches, with people in every great city, and it is the reason the
[[Margravate]] always seems to know what its enemies will do before they do.

## What it does

- **Tutors.** The Court's most famous work. For two hundred years it has trained excellent tutors, men and women of
  real learning and gentle manners, and offered them at a fair price to noble houses all over the Reaches. They teach
  languages, history, mathematics and the sword. They are very good. Their pupils grow up admiring order, efficiency
  and a certain lord in the east.
- **Spies.** Clerks, servants, merchants' factors, ferrymen, priests. Most are paid. Some believe. A few don't know
  whom they are working for.
- **Assassins.** Rarely used, never careless. The Court prefers a fever, a fall from a horse or a bad oyster to a
  knife, and a scandal to any of them.
- **Messengers.** A chain of post-houses and couriers runs from Vaelholt to [[Hask]], [[Aldmoor]] and the
  [[Lantern Principalities]]. Its letters travel faster than anyone else's.

## Marks and manners

Court letters are sealed with grey wax with a pinch of ash pressed into it. Its people call each other "embers" and
the Margrave "the Fire". A Court agent who is caught does not talk; the Court is known to look after the families of
those who don't, and to remember the families of those who do.

## The Steward

The Court is run day to day by the Steward of Embers, Cassia Dree, a thin, quiet woman of about sixty who was
herself a tutor in Calder for twenty years. She knows the name of every ember in the Reaches, and is said to have
taught three of the barons now waiting for King [[Bertil III]] to die.

## In Hask

The Court's people in Hask are many and well hidden. The Lord Warden, [[Benedek Sallow]], knows of some, lets them be, and sells them
selected truths. The [[Quiet Hand]] knows of others and charges them rent.

::: dm
**The Court's real task** in 1188 is succession. The Margrave knows the [[Ember Crown]] is failing. The Court has been
told to find, at any price, anything that might replace it: a Thaumarch relic, a [[Seven Lanterns|Lantern]], an answer
from the [[Serpent Oracle]], or the secret of the Vael Dig in the [[Glass Waste]]. Dree has begun to think the Fire
will go out, and has quietly begun to plan for after.

**Hooks.**
- A beloved tutor in a Hask merchant house is unmasked as an ember. The family wants him dead; the children want him
  saved; he wants to defect, and he has a list.
- The party intercepts a Court letter sealed with grey wax and ash. It is addressed to one of them, by name, and
  offers a job.
- Cassia Dree sends a polite note asking a party member to tea. She knows why they left home.
:::
