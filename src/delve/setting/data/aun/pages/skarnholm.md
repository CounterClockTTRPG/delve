Skarnholm is the chief port of the [[Northern Holds]]: a town of turf-roofed longhouses, tarred boat-sheds and smoke at
the head of a deep fjord, under cliffs where the gulls nest in thousands. It is the largest place in the Holds, which
means it has a market, a harbour wall and more than one street. Once a year it fills with jarls, their crews and their
grudges, for the [[Hold Moot]].

## The town

- **The Jarl's Hall.** A longhouse a hundred paces long, its roof-beams carved with the deeds of Skarnholm's jarls. The
  newest carvings are of [[Sigrun Halfhand]]. A skald is always in it, and usually drunk.
- **The Moot-Stone.** A flat grey rock on the shore below the hall, worn smooth. Whoever stands on it may speak and not
  be interrupted; whoever stands on it and lies, the Hold-folk say, will drown within the year.
- **The Ship-Sheds.** Long sheds along the shore, each holding a longship over the winter. A jarl's power is counted
  by the sheds he fills.
- **Vorm's House.** A temple of black stone above the town, cold inside even in summer. Its priests mark the days of
  the ice, and say when the ships may go.
- **The Steading Market.** Fish, furs, walrus ivory, amber, whale oil, slaves, and southern goods brought back from
  raids: Calderish wool, steppe saddles, the odd [[Ferryman's Toe]] worn on a string as a charm.

## Custom

- Weapons are worn openly in Skarnholm, but drawing one within sight of the Moot-Stone is paid for as a wound, whether
  anyone is cut or not.
- A newly arrived ship's master must give the jarl a gift before unloading. The size of the gift is remembered.
- At the Moot, every man and woman who can hold a spear may speak. Most do, at length.
- Prices: a bed in a longhouse, 1 copper and a story; a pair of skis, 5 silver; a seat at the jarl's high table, a
  deed worth singing.

## At the table

Skarnholm is where southern adventurers come to buy furs and amber, to sign on with a raiding crew, to buy a seat on
a ship north, or to talk a jarl into something. It is crowded, generous, quarrelsome and quick to violence.

have brought Sigrun Halfhand a map of the [[Hornwall]] passes and a promise that the [[Grey Legions]] will not

**Hooks.**
- A man stood on the Moot-Stone, swore an oath, and drowned the next week in calm water. His family wants to know who
  pushed him, or whether the stone did.
- A jarl's longship has come home with no crew aboard, only a [[Brass Servants|brass servant]] at the steering oar.
- A skald hires the party as witnesses for his account of a raid; the jarl he is singing about would prefer a
  different account.
