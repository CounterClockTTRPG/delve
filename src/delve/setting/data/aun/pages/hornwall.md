The Hornwall is the great mountain chain of the north, running eight hundred miles from the moors north of
[[Kingdom of Calder|Calder]] to the edge of the [[Margravate]]. It is the wall between the [[Northern Holds]] and
the south: grey peaks, snow nine months of the year, glaciers in the high valleys and wind that strips a cloak off a
mule. [[Vorm]] is said to sit on its highest peak. The [[Ambre]] rises in its southern flank.

## The range

- **The west.** Lower peaks, sheep-paths and bandit valleys. Calderish shepherds summer their flocks here and pay the
  [[Gnomes|gnome]] hill-clans for the right.
- **The middle.** The highest and coldest part, and the dwarves' country. [[Deephorn]], the great dwarven hold, sits
  above the source of the Ambre.
- **The east.** Broken, volcanic country of black rock and hot springs, held by orc tribes who serve the Margrave and
  send their sons to the [[Grey Legions]]. Dwarves once mined here.

## The passes

- **The Ambre Gate.** The river's gorge under Deephorn, the one easy road from the Holds to the south. The dwarves
  hold a tower at each end and charge a toll of one silver a head, one gold a horse, and a song or a curse from anyone
  who has neither.
- **Ketter's Notch.** A high goat-path in the west, open two months a year. Smugglers use it. Its namesake is said to be
  still up there, frozen upright.
- **The Red Stair.** The eastern road, cut in steps by the Thaumarchs and cracked at the [[Unmaking]]. Orcs hold it,
  and the Margrave's patrols use it.

## Dangers

Avalanches in spring, snow-blindness in summer, wolves all year. Ruined Thaumarch way-towers stand empty along the old
roads; travellers shelter in them and sometimes find they are not empty. Higher up are things the dwarves will not
name, and caves that breathe.

## Custom

- Every herder's hut is left open, with dry wood inside. A traveller who uses the wood leaves wood, or is cursed by the
  hut's small god, who is usually a stone by the door.
- Dwarves expect visitors on their mountains to give their name and business to the first dwarf they meet. Those who
  do not are noted in the Long Book of [[Deephorn]].

## At the table

The Hornwall is good for crossings, sieges of passes, lost mines, frozen ruins and orc country. Adventurers cross it,
guard caravans through it, or are hired by Deephorn to go where dwarves have stopped going.
