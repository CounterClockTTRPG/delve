Marrah owns the sea and everything that falls into it. She gives fish and wrecks with the same hand, and sailors pay
her the way they pay a harbour master they don't trust: on time, in full, and with a curse under the breath. Nobody
calls her good. Everybody on the coast pays.

Her temples stand on harbour walls from [[Skarnholm]] to [[Gullhaven]], low and wet, open to the weather on the sea
side. Her month, Marrah, comes at the end of spring, when the storms are worst and the fishing fleets go out anyway.

## What her people do

- **The hook.** Before a voyage, a sailor throws an iron hook into the sea "so she has something to hold." A ship that
  sails without throwing one is thought to be asking to be caught.
- **The first fish.** The first fish of any catch goes back, alive if possible. Fish that come back to the net twice
  are hers, and no one will eat them.
- **The first storm.** The first great storm of her month is her holy day. The priests stand on the harbour wall in it
  and sing; the drowned of that storm are counted her chosen.
- **Taboos.** Counting the waves aloud; naming a ship after a person who is still alive; swimming to save a drowning
  stranger without first throwing something of yours into the water. (Sailors do it anyway, and then throw a boot.)
- **Sayings.** "She lends, she doesn't give." "Paid and damned." A sailor home from sea says "She let me go," never
  "I came back."

## Her clerics

Marrah's priests are called hook-priests and are mostly old sailors with salt-ruined hands. A cleric of Marrah reads
weather well, sails better than most captains, and is welcome on any ship that can pay. They are capricious in the
manner of their goddess: kind one day, ready to leave a man in the water the next if the omens say so. They sneer at
[[Small Gods]] of the sea such as the [[Drowned Bell]], and the pirates who pray to the bell sneer back.

::: dm
The hook-priests keep charts of the wrecks of the [[Thaumarchy]] along the [[Drowned Coast]] and the Sunder Sea
side of the [[Lantern Principalities]]. They sell the charts to salvagers for a tenth of what is raised, and mark a
few as "hers, untouchable." Those few are the ones worth having.

Hooks:
- A hook-priest wants the party to recover a temple's bronze hook, stolen and sold to a Gullhaven captain who keeps it
  as a luck charm. Since it went, the harbour has had no catch.
- A ship comes into [[Hask]] up the [[Ambre]] with a crew who all say "I came back." They did not throw a hook. Nothing
  has happened to them yet.
:::
