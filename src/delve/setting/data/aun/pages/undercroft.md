The Undercroft is the dungeon under the [[Pale Tower]]: levels of Thaumarch halls, stairs, shafts, cisterns and
vaults cut into the rock below the bluff, deeper than anyone has been and come back to say. It is [[Hask]]'s great
place for adventurers to dig, die and get rich, in roughly that order of likelihood. Crews have been going down for
three hundred years and coming up with brass, glass, gold, Thaumarch writing and fevers. There is always more.

## The levels, as the Drowned Toe tells it

Nobody agrees on how many there are. Crews speak of them by name:

1. **The Cellars.** The tower's foundations, picked clean long ago, now home to rats, squatters, Leantown's rubbish
   and whatever has wandered up from below. Good for training a new crew, and for losing a careless one.
2. **The Brass Galleries.** Long halls lined with alcoves, each holding a stopped [[Brass Servants|brass servant]],
   tall, still and green with age. Most have been stripped. A few still have their glass eyes. Crews walk the
   galleries in single file and do not touch.
3. **The Wet Halls.** Here the [[Ambre]] seeps in. Chambers flooded to the knee, to the neck, to the ceiling; a sunk
   library of stone shelves whose books are pulp; things in the water that have learned what lamplight means.
4. **The Lamp-Deep.** Few crews have reached it. Those who did speak of dry, warm halls lit with a light that comes
   from nowhere, and of doors that open only for a Thaumarch voice.

Below the Lamp-Deep there are stairs. Some crews have gone down them.

## What comes up

- Thaumarch coins of a pale gold that won't scratch, worth twice their weight to the [[Collegium of the Lamp]].
- Brass parts: fingers, gears, eyes. Smiths pay for the metal, which is harder than steel.
- Glass: lenses, beads, rods that hum. The Collegium pays for these and asks where.
- Writing, on metal leaves or glass tiles, which is the one thing everybody wants.
- Once in a generation, something that works.

## The rules of the hole

- Pay the Lord Warden's tenth at the Lean Gate on the way up. Smuggling finds past the tollkeeper is a fine; doing it
  twice is the gibbet.
- Chalk your way. A crew that doesn't is found by the next crew, usually by smell.
- Never go down on the [[Hollow Days]]. Crews who have say the Undercroft goes deeper then.
- What you find in the Undercroft is yours. Whatever finds you is also yours.

::: dm
The Undercroft was the binding-works of one of the Seven Houses: the place where its Lantern was forged and fed. That
Lantern of the [[Seven Lanterns]] lies at the bottom, in a vault below the Lamp-Deep, still lit and still holding a
sliver of what it bound. Its light runs up through conduits to the tower top and to the Last Lamp in the
[[Lamp Quarter]]. Each Thaumarch work carried out weakens the old wards around it; when enough are gone, the vault
opens, for anyone.

The deeper [[Brass Servants|brass servants]] are not stopped but waiting for an order from a Thaumarch voice. The
Margravate's diggers in the [[Glass Waste]] have found something that speaks with one.

**Hooks.**
- The [[Open Mouth]] has a way into the Wet Halls from Hask's sewers and is using the drowned library as a temple.
  Crews have started finding chalk marks that aren't theirs, in the shape of a mouth.
- [[Ilse Varrow]] wants a crew to carry a glass tile down to the Lamp-Deep, set it in a certain door, and come back
  without asking what it does. She will pay in gold and won't say for how much longer the door will stay shut.
- A Lantern prince's envoy wants the vault found for him, and he has brought a map that is older than any in the
  Drowned Toe.
:::
