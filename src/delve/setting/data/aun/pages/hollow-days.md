The **Hollow Days** are the five days at the turn of the year, outside the twelve months, belonging to none of the
[[Great Gods]]. They fall at midwinter, just before 1 Vormas. In folk belief they are [[Nhul]]'s, and nobody wants
to be out in them.

## Customs

- **Doors shut.** Houses bar their doors at dusk on the first day and open them only by day. Knocking on a door after
  dark in the Hollow Days is rude at best; at worst, someone answers it with a spear.
- **Fires kept.** The hearth must not go out for all five days. Families take turns to watch it. A cold hearth on the
  sixth morning means a death in the house within the year.
- **No journeys begun.** A trip started in the Hollow Days is cursed. Travellers already on the road keep going and
  stop at the first roof. Inns fill and prices treble.
- **No debts called, no oaths sworn.** Contracts dated in the Hollow Days are void in most courts of the Reaches.
- **Gifts to the small.** People leave food out for the [[Small Gods]] of the house and the street, since the great
  gods are not watching.

## Around the Reaches

- In [[Hask]] the ferries do not run after dark, and the [[Ferrymen's Guild]] closes its boats with a brass chain;
  the [[Ferryman]] does not like being disturbed.
- In the [[Northern Holds]] families sit up with their dead, and songs are sung to [[Grandmother Frost]].
- In the [[Sunward Theocracy]] the churches hold vigil all five nights and ring bells at each hour to keep Nhul away.
- In [[Tallow Lane]], [[Old Tallow]]'s candle-makers give a candle to every house that asks, and do a roaring trade.

## At the table

A crew that wants to travel in the Hollow Days will find no ferry, no guide and no inn with room. Anyone met on the
road is either desperate or up to something.

::: dm
The [[Open Mouth]] holds its great rites in the Hollow Days, when the watchers are behind shut doors. [[Mother Gutter]]'s
beggars are out in force on these nights, and they need help.

- Someone the PCs know vanishes on the third Hollow Night, and their door was opened from inside.
- An enemy who must move goods or people unseen will do it now; the Watch is thin, drunk and indoors.
- In the Theocracy, an inquisitor uses the vigil to see who does not come to church.
:::
