The glass-pickers are the people who go into the [[Glass Waste]] and come back. They are guides, scavengers, diggers
and sometimes graverobbers, a loose brotherhood of a few hundred men and women from every people of the Reaches,
bound not by a guild or a master but by a handful of oaths and the plain fact that nobody else will drink with them.
They are recognised by their scarred hands, their black gauze veils, and their boots, which are soled three times.

## The oaths

Every picker swears the same five oaths at a cistern, with water in their mouth:

1. **Water is shared.** A picker with water gives to a picker without. A picker who hoards water in the Waste may be
   left in it.
2. **First hand.** What a picker's hand touches first is theirs, unless they are on another's contract.
3. **Cisterns are secret.** The way to a cistern is told only to another sworn picker, and never written down.
4. **The dead face east.** A picker who dies in the Waste is laid on the glass facing [[Ishavel]], and left. Their
   finds go to whoever lays them out.
5. **The guide decides.** On a contract, the guide's word on the route, the pace and when to turn back is law. A
   client who will not turn back is left to go on alone.

A picker who breaks an oath is "cracked". No picker will guide, trade with or share water with them again.

## Where they work

Most pickers work out of **Shardsend**, a camp-town of tents, sod huts and one stone well on the Waste's south-west
edge, three days' ride from [[Tessarine]]. Shardsend has two taverns, a dealer's row where finds are weighed, a shrine
to the [[Glass Saint]], a shrine to [[Fennick]] and a burying-ground with no bodies in it. Smaller camps lie along the
western rim, near the [[Margravate]]'s border, where the Grey patrols take a toll.

## Hiring one

A picker guide costs five silver a day, and a share of the finds (a third is usual, half for the deep Waste). Pay
the cistern-price besides: a picker who takes clients to a secret cistern charges for the water and for keeping the
secret. The best known guide is [[Old Mazzar]], who is also the most expensive and the least sober.

::: dm
**Pickers and the Margrave.** Since the Vael Dig began, the Margrave's men hang any picker found near it, and offer
gold to any who will guide their surveyors elsewhere. A few have taken the gold; the brotherhood has not decided yet
whether that cracks them. The quarrel is splitting Shardsend in two.

**The burying-ground** has no bodies because pickers who die outside the Waste are carried back into it. Ghosts of
pickers walk the glass, and still keep the oaths: a dead picker will share water, if you are sworn.

**Hooks.**
- A client of a picker crew refused to turn back and went on alone. He has come back two months later, unharmed,
  with a full waterskin and no memory of where he found it.
- A cracked picker offers to guide the party past the Margrave's patrols for a third of the usual price.
- Shardsend's stone well has started to taste of salt. The pickers want to know why before they have to leave.
:::
