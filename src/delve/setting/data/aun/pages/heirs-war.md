The **Heirs' War** is the decade in the 730s RY when the **Lantern Kingdom**, the realm of the Thaumarch kin in
the south, broke into rival princedoms. The pieces are the [[Lantern Principalities]] of today.

## The Lantern Kingdom

The kin of the [[Thaumarchy]] who survived the [[Unmaking]] fled south during the [[Ash Centuries]] and settled the
lower [[Ambre]] and the Sunder Sea shore. They kept their Seven Houses, their ceremony, their pale eyes and very little
of their magic. For some four hundred years they were ruled by a single crown, passed among the Houses by an election
so intricate that its rules filled a library.

## How it broke

In the 730s the king of the day died without a son, and his will named no heir. Seven cousins came forward, one from
each House, each holding a document proving the other six forged. At the same time each let it be known that their
House held one of the [[Seven Lanterns]]. The electors could not decide which was the stronger claim, a will or a
Lantern, and the matter was settled the Lanterner way: with poison, marriages, sudden duels and one very long dinner.

By the end of the decade there were seven princes, each with a capital, a court and a claim to the whole. Three came
out strong: [[Corvanne]], [[Tessarine]] and [[Amaranthe]]. The other four survive by marrying into those three and
selling their votes in every quarrel.

## What it left

- Every Lantern prince still claims to be the rightful heir of the whole kingdom, and dates documents accordingly.
- Every prince claims a Lantern. No two agree on which is real.
- The laws of the Lantern Kingdom were never repealed, only ignored. A visitor can be fined in any princedom under a
  statute from 640 RY about the colour of boots.
- The phrase "a Lantern dinner" means a meal where someone will not reach dessert.

::: dm
The old election rules are still technically in force and could reunite the Principalities, if someone held a true
Lantern and enough electors' seals. The seals were scattered as keepsakes; a few are in Hask pawnshops.

- One of the seven cousins' documents was real. It is in the [[Undercroft]], carried there by a fleeing clerk
  who never came out.
- The Margrave has quietly bought two of the weak princes, and would like a third.
:::
