Humans are most of the Reaches, and most of its trouble. They came late, lived in tribes in the Elder Days, built the
[[Thaumarchy]] and lost it, and have spent twelve centuries since quarrelling over the pieces. An elf would say they
are short-lived, quick to learn and quicker to forget. A human would say that is easy for an elf to say.

Every realm thinks its own folk are the sensible ones. A traveller learns the rest by their bread, their oaths and
what they call strangers.

## The peoples

- **Calderish.** Fair, broad and slow to hurry: farmers, shepherds and knights of the western valleys of the
  [[Kingdom of Calder]]. Proud of an old crown and a long memory of being important. They eat dark bread and mutton,
  swear by [[Hollin]] and their fathers, and call everyone east of the [[Thornwold]] "river-people", meaning sharp.
- **Haskers.** Mixed from everyone who ever crossed the [[Ambre]] at [[Hask]], and quick-tongued about it. A Hasker
  can bargain in four languages and insult you in six. They swear by [[Fennick]], carry a [[Ferryman's Toe]] for luck,
  and believe nothing they are told for free.
- **Hold-folk.** Tall, pale, red or flaxen: raiders, fishers and skalds of the [[Northern Holds]]. They keep feuds for
  generations and count a good death as an achievement. They worship [[Vorm]] and lay their dead in
  [[Grandmother Frost]]'s ice. A Hold-born adventurer will want a saga made of anything they do.
- **The steppe clans.** Dark, tattooed horse people of the [[Horse Steppe]] under [[Ulagh Khan]]. They give their dead
  to the sky, judge a stranger by how they sit a horse, and trade and raid with Calder in about equal measure.
- **Lanterners.** Olive-skinned, dark-haired and pale-eyed, of the [[Lantern Principalities]]: the blood of the
  Thaumarchs, and they never let you forget it. Ceremonious, clever, fond of poison and of laws nobody else
  understands. Pale eyes are thought in the north to be a sign of a sorcerer, which annoys Lanterners and pleases them.
- **Southrons.** Dark and quick, of the [[Greenmaw]] coast and the Sunder Sea ports: sailors, traders and spice-sellers,
  at home in three harbours and loyal to the ship more than the city. They pay [[Marrah]] and curse her in the same
  breath.

Sunward folk of the [[Sunward Theocracy]] are mostly Calderish in blood, whitewashed in manner: grave, tidy, and
inclined to report their neighbours.

## At the table

Humans in Aun live far from magic. A farmer may see one miracle in a lifetime and talk about it for the rest. Most have
never met a wizard, an elf or a dwarf, and expect the worst of all three. Adventurers are thought one step from
bandits. A human character from a village will be sharper about [[Small Gods]] than any priest: they grew up leaving
milk for one.

::: dm
Lanterner pale eyes really are Thaumarch blood, and once in a generation a Lanterner child is born who can hear the
[[Seven Lanterns]] when one is near: a hum like a struck glass. The Lantern princes know this and keep such children
close. One ran away to Hask and works the ferries.

Hooks:
- A Calderish knight and a Hold raider in the same crew owe each other blood from a raid twenty years ago. Neither knows
  yet.
- A steppe clan wants a Calderish baron's son as a hostage-guest for a year, by old custom, to seal a horse trade. The
  boy does not want to go; his father has already agreed.
:::
