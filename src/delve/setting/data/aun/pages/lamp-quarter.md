The Lamp Quarter is the north end of [[Hask]]'s west bank, a tangle of tall, leaning houses along the Old Cut where
the city keeps its learning, its wizards and its stranger trades. Booksellers, copyists, apothecaries, alchemists,
map-makers, astrologers and dealers in old things live here, mostly above their shops and mostly in debt.

## The Last Lamp

The quarter is named for a single Thaumarch street-lamp at the corner of the Old Cut and Quill Street: a slim post of
pale metal, taller than two men, topped with a glass flower that has glowed a soft white every night for twelve
hundred years. It went on burning when [[Ishavel]] fell. Nobody lights it and nobody can put it out. Lovers meet
under it, scholars argue under it, and the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] takes its name from it and its moths from it
too. Touching the post is forbidden by city law and by common sense: it is cold enough to take the skin off.

## What is here

- **The Collegium of the Lamp**, a narrow house of seven storeys built around the Lamp's corner, where Hask's seven
  licensed wizards work, quarrel and pretend not to need money.
- **Quill Street**, the booksellers' row. Most of the stock is ledgers, almanacs and lives of the saints. Some of it
  is Thaumarch scraps sold as genuine, a few of which are.
- **The Stinks**, the alchemists' yard, where the windows are paned with oiled paper because glass doesn't last.
- **The Old Cut**, the silted Thaumarch canal, crossed here by plank walks. The debt-gangs dredging it sell what they
  find to Quill Street before the Watch can tax it.

## Customs

Lamp Quarter folk speak softly, sell slowly and remember everything. A scrap of Thaumarch writing is worth more here
than anywhere else in the Reaches, which means more fakes are sold here too. There is a saying: "Buy in the Lamp
Quarter, read in the dark." The inquisitors of the House of the Scale walk Quill Street every day, buying nothing.

::: dm
The Last Lamp is fed from below, by a conduit that runs, through drains and the Old Cut, all the way to the
[[Undercroft]]. Its light dims a little each time a Thaumarch work is carried out of the [[Pale Tower]]. [[Ilse Varrow]]
has kept a record of its brightness for forty years; it has never been as dim as this spring.

**Hooks.**
- A bookseller on Quill Street has a page in Thaumarch script that the Collegium, [[Magdal Crane]] and an Ember Court
  buyer all want. He has three days of courage left. The page is a list of the Seven Houses' Lanterns, and one line
  says where.
- An alchemist's apprentice is selling "lamp-oil" scraped from the base of the Last Lamp. It glows, and those who
  burn it dream of a burning city.
