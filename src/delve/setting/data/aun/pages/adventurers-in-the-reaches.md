In the Reaches an adventurer is not a hero. It is a trade, somewhere between a grave-robber, a caravan guard and a
bounty hunter, and respectable people rank it a little above bandit and a little below tanner.

## Crews

Adventurers work in **crews** of four to eight: a few blades, someone who can open locks, someone who can read old
script, and, if they are lucky, a priest. A crew has a name, a share-out agreed in advance (the usual is equal shares,
a double share for whoever found the job), and a debt to somebody. Crews form and break at the [[Drowned Toe]] in
[[Hask]], where the job board is a wall of nails hung with notes, maps and the names of the dead.

## The work

- **Digging.** The [[Undercroft]] under the [[Pale Tower]] has made more crews rich and more crews dead than anywhere
  else in the Reaches. Smaller ruins dot the [[Kingdom of Calder|Calderish]] downs and the edge of the [[Glass Waste]].
- **Guarding.** Caravans along the [[Ambre]] and the old roads hire crews by the day.
- **Hunting.** Bounties posted by the Watch, the merchant houses or a desperate village with a thing in its well.
- **Errands for the powerful.** Every faction in Hask hires crews for work it would rather not be seen doing.

## Selling

Finds are sold to the [[Collegium of the Lamp]] (anything with writing on it), to House Orlemont's brokers (coin,
gems, plate), or to fences licensed by the [[Quiet Hand]]. Selling a Thaumarch piece outside these channels is legal
in Hask and usually fatal.

## Customs

- A crew drinks to its dead on the night it comes back, with one cup left full on the table.
- A [[Ferryman's Toe]] carried in the boot is the commonest luck charm. Crews going down the Undercroft drop one in
  the river first.
- Nobody says the name of [[Nhul]] underground.
- A crew that leaves a member behind alive is shunned at the Toe for a season.

::: dm
Adventurers live between powers. Use that. Every big find draws offers, threats and spies.

- The [[Ember Court]] pays crews through a cut-out (a Tollside wine merchant) to bring Thaumarch finds east instead of
  to the Collegium.
- The [[Sunward Church]] hires crews to burn small-god shrines in the countryside and pays in absolution and silver.
- The [[Open Mouth]] sells false maps of the Undercroft at the Toe; the crews who follow them are not found.
