The Stews are the southern end of [[Wetside]], where the [[Ambre]] bends east and the lanes get narrower, darker and
more interesting. Brothels, dice dens, pit-fights, cheap wine, cheaper lodgings, fences, forgers and fortune-tellers:
everything Hask officially disapproves of and quietly taxes. The Watch comes in only to collect.

## What is here

- **Mother Kettle's**, the worst and best tavern in the city: a great iron pot of stew on the boil since anyone can
  remember, a toe a bowl, and nobody asks what's in it. Mother Kettle is enormous, deaf in one ear and impossible to
  cheat. Knife fights happen here roughly every night; she charges for the floor.
- **The Rattle**, a dice-hall in a sunken barge hull, where you can bet on anything: dice, cocks, rats, how long a
  drunk will last in the river.
- **The pits** behind the old fish-market, where half-orcs, deserters and the occasional desperate adventurer fight
  for purses. A rat-catcher's terrier holds the record for kills.
- **Lantern-lane**, the street of brothels, named for the red lamps in the windows and not, its residents insist, for
  any prince.

## The Hand's peace

The Stews belong to the [[Quiet Hand]]. Its rules are simple and better kept than the city's: no killing a paying
customer, no robbing a licensed house, no thieving without a Hand licence, and no talking to the Watch. Break them
and you will be found floating near the tanneries with your right hand closed around a stone.

A newcomer who wants to do business in the Stews (fence a find, buy a forged pass, hire a lockpick) asks for a
**Finger**: one of the Hand's local lieutenants. Mother Kettle can point one out, for a price.

::: dm
Mother Kettle is one of the Widow's oldest friends and her eyes in the Stews. The pot has been on the boil since the
year she came to Hask; what she put in it first is a matter of rumour.

**Hooks.**
- A pit-fighter has started winning every bout: a quiet man with grey skin who doesn't bleed. He's a woken
  [[Brass Servants|brass servant]] wearing a dead man's face, and he has come to Hask looking for whoever woke him.
- The Rattle's owner has lost the barge's deeds to a stranger at dice. The stranger is an [[Ember Court]] agent, and
  he now owns the Hand's best counting-house.
:::
