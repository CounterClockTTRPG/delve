**Aun** is the world. Nobody alive has seen much of it. The lands people know are called **the Reaches**: one long
continent, about 1,600 miles from west to east and 1,000 from north to south, with the cold **Iron Sea** along its
northern shore and the warm **Sunder Sea** to the south. What lies past either sea is a matter for sailors' lies.

It is **1188 RY**, the Reckoned Year, counted from the night of the [[Unmaking]]. A campaign begins on 1 Isaine, in
early spring, on [[Isaine's Morning]].

## The shape of the Reaches

- **The middle.** The [[Ambre]] runs south from the [[Hornwall]] to the Sunder Sea, and on it sits the [[Free City of Hask]], rich and crooked, where the only crossing for a hundred miles is a ferry. Upriver stands the leaning
  [[Pale Tower]], and under it the [[Undercroft]].
- **The west.** The old [[Kingdom of Calder]], sheep and castles and a king with no heir; the [[Horse Steppe]] beyond
  it; the whitewashed [[Sunward Theocracy]] in the south-west; and past that the [[Drowned Coast]] of smugglers and sunk
  towns.
- **The north.** The fjords and longships of the [[Northern Holds]], behind the wall of the [[Hornwall]], where the
  dwarves keep [[Deephorn]].
- **The south.** The [[Lantern Principalities]], heirs of the old empire, perfumed and poisonous.
- **The east.** The iron [[Margravate]] of the Undying Margrave, and beyond it the [[Glass Waste]], where [[Ishavel]]
  burned. Along the south-east coast lies the jungle of the [[Greenmaw]].
- **Between.** The elves' [[Thornwold]], between Calder and Hask, and the marsh of [[Redmere]], where the [[Seven Years' War]] stalled.

## What everyone knows

- Once there was an empire of sorcerers, the [[Thaumarchy]]. It tried to put the gods in harness and the gods burned it.
  Everything since has been living in the wreck. See [[Great Binding]] and [[Ash Centuries]].
- The [[Great Gods]] are twelve and far away. Their churches are near, rich and quarrelsome.
- The [[Small Gods]] are close: the god of the ford, of the well, of this one street. You pay them, or else.
- Magic is rare, costly and watched. See [[Magic in the Reaches]].
- People who go into ruins for money are called adventurers, and respectable folk count the spoons after they leave.
  See [[Adventurers in the Reaches]].
- The year has twelve months named for the great gods and five unlucky days that belong to none of them. See
  [[Calendar of the Reckoning]] and [[Hollow Days]].

## At the table

Distances are long and roads are poor. The old Thaumarch roads still run straight where they have not cracked, and
travellers prefer them even where they are haunted. A day's walk is about twenty miles on a good road, twelve in rough
country. News from the far side of the Reaches is a season old and usually wrong.

::: dm
The world is in a held breath. In 1188 three things are about to tip: the Margrave's diggers have found something
large in the Glass Waste, the young Hierarch wants a holy war, and the King of Calder will die without an heir. Hask
sits in the middle of all three and wants none of them.

- Start the players in [[Hask]] at the [[Drowned Toe]]; every thread in the Reaches passes through the ferry.
- Any old Thaumarch thing they find can be tied to the [[Seven Lanterns]], and every faction will want it.
:::
