Orcs live in tribes in the eastern [[Hornwall]] and in the [[Margravate]] below it: big, grey-green, tusked and hard as
the mountains they come from. To most of the Reaches they mean one thing: the [[Grey Legions]], where the Hornwall
tribes march for the Undying Margrave. That is true, and it is not the whole truth.

## What they are like

- **Tribes.** Each tribe is a few hundred strong, ruled by whoever can hold it, and named for a mountain, an ancestor
  or a grievance. Tribes feud, ally, marry and break apart as fast as human baronies, and with less paperwork.
- **The Margrave's.** Since the early years of the Margravate, most eastern tribes have served [[Teodric Vael]]. He
  pays in iron, meat and land, and he does not lose. Orcs respect that more than any oath.
- **Hard.** Orcs are strong, endure cold and hunger well, and fight with a frankness that humans call savagery and orcs
  call not wasting time. They despise cowards and liars, and they see most humans as both.
- **Faith.** Orcs honour [[Karsk]] and [[Vorm]] and the small gods of their peaks and passes. In the Grey Legions,
  worship of Karsk is forbidden, so they keep their faith quiet.

## Half-orcs

Half-orcs are common in Hask's [[Wetside]] and in the [[Free Companies]]: children of soldiers, raids, trade and
occasionally love. They are hired as dockers, bouncers, guards and soldiers, and are paid less than humans for it.
Most were raised among humans and know orc ways by hearsay. Some go east to find out, and come back with stories.

## Customs

- An orc gives a stranger one chance. Insult them once, and you are warned; twice, and it is a fight.
- Tribes mark their warriors with scars on the forearm, one for each battle survived. Old orcs have none left to mark.
- Orcs eat together, from one pot, standing. Sitting to eat is for the wounded and the old.
- Saying: "Strong enough." (Praise. The highest there is.)

## At the table

An orc or half-orc character will be stared at everywhere, thought a deserter in Calder and a spy in Hask, and hired
by every Free Company that sees them. They know the Grey Legions from the inside, or know someone who does. The
[[Sunward Church]] counts orcs as beasts and half-orcs as sins; the Free Companies count them as soldiers, which is
the better deal.

::: dm
The Margrave's hold on the tribes is weaker than it looks. The eastern mines he took from Deephorn are worked by orc
slaves from tribes that refused him, and the tribes that serve him know it. Some chiefs are waiting for a sign that he
can die. If the [[Ember Crown]] falters in public, the Hornwall tribes will turn within a season.

Hooks:
- An orc envoy comes secretly to Hask asking for a meeting with [[Barrek Stonesong]]'s people. The dwarves would sooner
  kill him, and the [[Ember Court]] would like that very much.
- A half-orc dockhand in Wetside has been named heir to a Hornwall tribe. Three cousins are coming to dispute it.
:::
