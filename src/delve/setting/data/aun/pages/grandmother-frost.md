Above the fjords of the [[Northern Holds]], in a high valley, a glacier comes down from the mountains like a slow white
river. The Hold-folk call it Grandmother Frost, and it keeps their dead. Look into the ice on a clear day and you can
see them: men and women in their best clothes, laid in rows, some a few years old and some so deep that their faces are
only shadows. She does not let them rot. She does not let them go.

She is the best loved of the north's [[Small Gods]]. [[Vorm]]'s priests call her his daughter; the Hold-folk call her
their grandmother and treat her the way they treat their real ones: with love, fear and gifts.

## Customs

- **The Carrying.** Those who die in the year are kept in the cold until midwinter, then carried up to the glacier in a
  long procession and laid on the ice. Over the next years the ice takes them in.
- **Visiting.** Hold-folk go up to the ice to talk to their dead, bringing a cup of ale to pour on the glacier. Grief is
  done there, not at home.
- **Gifts.** The dead are laid with one thing they loved: a knife, a cloak pin, a comb. Stealing from the ice is the
  worst crime in the Holds, and the thief is laid on it alive.
- **Taboos.** Burning the dead; burying them in the earth (southern priests of [[Velis]] have had arguments); lighting a
  fire within sight of the glacier.
- **Sayings.** "She has him." "Gone to Grandmother." A long life is "a slow walk up the valley."

## Her priests

The ice-wives keep the glacier, guide the Carryings and talk to the dead for those who can't. In her valley they can
hear the dead answer, and walk barefoot on ice without harm. In [[Skarnholm]] their gifts are weak, and south of the
[[Hornwall]] they are gone. A Hold-born cleric of Vorm who has served the Grandmother will always want, at the end, to
be carried home.

::: dm
The glacier is growing, and quickly. Vorm's priests have measured it. The dead inside it are moving, slowly, all of them
turning their faces south. The ice-wives know and say nothing yet.

Some very deep in the ice are not Hold-folk at all: they wear the robes of the [[Thaumarchy]].

Hooks:
- A Hold family's ancestor was stolen out of the ice, with the sword laid by him. The sword turned up in [[Hask]] for
  sale; the family wants both back, and the thief laid on the ice.
- A jarl wants an ice-wife to ask his dead father whether to join [[Sigrun Halfhand]]'s raid south. The father's answer
  is not what she reports.
