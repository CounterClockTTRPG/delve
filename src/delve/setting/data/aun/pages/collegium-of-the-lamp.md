The Collegium of the Lamp is [[Hask]]'s society of licensed wizards: seven of them, never more, in a narrow
seven-storey house in the [[Lamp Quarter]] built around the Last Lamp. In a world where magic is learned slowly from
scraps of Thaumarch writing and paid for in years, sanity and friends, seven wizards in one city is a great many.
The Collegium knows this, and so does everyone else.

## Licence and leash

Under Hask's law, anyone who works magic in the city without a Collegium licence hangs on Gallows Hill. A licence is
granted by the seven, voting, and costs a year's apprentice labour, a sworn oath to the city and a fee that would
buy a barge. It can be taken back by the same vote. A licensed wizard wears the Collegium's seal, a small brass
disc, at the throat, and the Watch is told to look for it.

The Collegium in turn is licensed by the Ford Council, which can close it, and watched by the House of the Scale,
whose inquisitors would like to. The Collegium's members walk carefully, sign everything and are polite to priests.

## What it does

- **Buys Thaumarch writing**: metal leaves, glass tiles, scraps of inscribed brass, anything with the old script on
  it, from crews, booksellers and dredgers. It pays poorly, in silver, and asks where it was found.
- **Identifies finds** from the [[Undercroft]] for a fee: what it is, roughly what it does, whether it will kill
  you. The answers are cautious and sometimes right.
- **Serves the city**: seals the Council's documents against forgery, tests the Moneyers' gold, and keeps the Last
  Lamp under watch.
- **Quarrels.** Over every scrap. Seven wizards, seven theories of the [[Thaumarchy]], and one budget.

## The seven

The Collegium is headed by its Provost, [[Ilse Varrow]], who is old, sharp, short-tempered and has been Provost for
twenty-two years. The other six include Master Teofil Asp, who studies the [[Brass Servants]] and has a brass hand
of his own; Dame Marit Selwyn, the youngest, who reads the old script better than Varrow and knows it; and old
Orben Thale, who has not left his room in nine years and whose meals come back eaten.

## Dealing with the Collegium

A crew with a find should expect to be underpaid and overquestioned. A crew with a wizard among them should expect a
visit: the Collegium wants to know every spell-user in the city, licensed or not, and would rather it be them who
finds out than the House of the Scale.

::: dm
Ilse Varrow knows one of the [[Seven Lanterns]] lies at the bottom of the Undercroft, and that each Thaumarch work
carried out weakens its wards. Half the Collegium wants it reached and studied; half wants the Undercroft closed. Marit
Selwyn sells copies of the Collegium's translations to the [[Ember Court]], to pay a debt to House Orlemont.

**Hooks.**
- A Collegium apprentice has run off with a glass tile and is hiding in the [[Stews]]. The bounty is good; the tile is
  humming.
- A player wizard is offered a licence for one errand: carry a sealed case to a Margravate officer at [[Redmere]] and
  bring back what he gives you.
