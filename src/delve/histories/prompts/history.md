## The record

The material below is the record of the campaign as it was played: the characters and their fates, what they did,
what the DM narrated and the NPCs said, the people they met, the factions, the quests, and the party's earlier
history when it had one.

Be faithful to the record. Who was there, who lived and who died, who was met, what was done and what was found are
as they were played. You may compress and leave out what doesn't matter, but you invent no outcome, no person, no
place and no item the record doesn't hold. When the record holds an earlier history, the history you write covers it
too, condensed: nothing from an earlier adventure that still matters is lost.

Dice and game terms (hit points, THAC0, rounds, saving throws, levels, armour class, reputation numbers) never appear.
Tell what happened and how things stand, never the mechanics of it.
