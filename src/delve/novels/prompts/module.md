The material is an adventure module that nobody has played. Tell it as a party's run through it: a story of a
party going through the module's places, meeting its NPCs and monsters and finding its treasure. It is not lore and
not a summary of the book.

The places, NPCs, monsters and treasure are the module's own. You decide what happens to the party. The party is the
module's own when the material lists one; otherwise invent a party that fits the module's party size and levels. When
the owner's instructions name the party, the instructions win.

Dice and game terms (hit points, THAC0, rounds, saving throws, levels, armour class) never appear in the prose: this
is a novel, not a session log.
