An evening of play is a **session**. Most sessions mix three things:

- **Exploring**: going somewhere and finding out what is there. Say where your character goes and what they look at.
  Careful players search rooms, listen at doors and test the floor.
- **Talking**: the DM plays everyone you meet. You can ask, bargain, lie, threaten or befriend. Not every problem
  needs a sword.
- **Fighting**: when it comes to blows, play goes in rounds. Each round, everyone says what they do, and
  **initiative** decides who goes first.

## Between sessions

At the end of a session the story stops wherever you are, even mid-dungeon, and the next session picks up there.
Between sessions characters keep what they earned: treasure, experience, scars.
