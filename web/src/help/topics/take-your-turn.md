The box under the story speaks to the DM. Write what your character does and press **Act** (or Enter). Shift-Enter
starts a new line.

## One beat at a time

At a table of several players, the DM answers everyone at once. A turn is a beat: each player acts, then the DM
answers. The beat closes when everyone who is here has acted, when the host closes it, or when the beat timer, if the
host set one, runs out.

- While the beat is open, **Acts so far** shows your act and the others', marked draft.
- Choose **Change** to put your act back in the box; **Replace my act** then sends the new one.
- **Pass** says your character does nothing this beat.
- The names under the story show who is typing and who has acted.

Alone at a table, the DM answers each act as you send it.

## More than one character

If you play several characters, the button beside **Act** and **Ask the DM** says who acts, such as **Acting for:
whole party**. Choose it to act for one character, or **Several…** to tick some.

## A secret from the table

Tick **Only the DM** before you act. The other players don't see your act, and the DM tells you alone what comes of
it.

## While the DM answers

**The DM is answering…** shows under the story, with the time since the beat closed. You can write meanwhile: your
words stay in the box, and you act again when the DM is done.

Before a session starts, and on a break, Act waits. You can still use [table talk](/help/table-talk) and
[ask the DM](/help/ask-the-dm).
