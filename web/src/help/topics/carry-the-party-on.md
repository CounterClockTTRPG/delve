A campaign keeps its one module. When the party has finished it and should go on to another adventure, carry it on
into a new campaign.

1. On the campaign's page, choose **Carry the party on**. It is also in the campaign's **…** menu under
   **Campaigns**.
2. Give the **New campaign's name** and pick the **Module**. Drafts aren't offered.
3. Check the **Ruleset**, **Setting** and house rules. They start as the old campaign's.
4. Under **Who goes**, untick anyone who stays behind. The living characters start ticked; the dead are marked
   **dead**.
5. Choose **Carry the party on**.

The new campaign gets a copy of each character, their possessions, and the party's coin and loot pile. The old
campaign is kept as it is and set to **Complete**, and each page links to the other.

## The party's history

Claude then writes the party's history from the old campaign, in two versions:

- **The DM's**, which the DM reads every session.
- **The table's**, which holds only what the whole table was told. Players read it under the play screen's
  **History** tab.

Both are in the **History** section of the new campaign's page. Choose **Edit** to change them, or **Write again**
to have Claude start over; that replaces your edit.
