The players' characters together are **the party**. They travel together, help each other and share what they find.
A party usually has three to six characters, each good at something different, so they need each other.

The DM is everything that isn't the party: the weather, the innkeeper, the dragon, the locked door. The DM isn't
against you. A good DM wants the party to have a story worth telling, and that means danger the party can sometimes
beat.

## Adventures and campaigns

- **The adventure** (also called a **module**) is the situation the DM prepared: a ruined keep, a missing caravan, a
  dungeon under a town.
- **A campaign** is one party's run through it, over many evenings of play.
