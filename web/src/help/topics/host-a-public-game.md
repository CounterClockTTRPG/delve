A table is private until you say otherwise: only people with its invite link can join. To let others find it, open the
campaign's page and its **Table listing**, on the **Overview** tab.

1. Under **Who can find the table**, choose **Public**. Accounts on this Delve can now find it in **Find a table** and
   ask to join; the invite link still works.
2. Set **Places for players**, or leave it empty for no limit. You and anyone watching take no place.
3. Write **About this table, for players**: how you play together and whether newcomers are welcome. Only players
   read it; the DM never does. Tell the DM how to play in the custom tone instead: on the play screen, **Table
   options**, **Tone**, **Custom**.
4. Choose **Save the listing**.

**Find a table** also shows players the house rules in effect, how many places are taken and, once you set a
schedule, when the table plays next.

Requests to join show on the play screen, in the side column, where you let in whoever you want at your table: see
[Inviting players](/help/invite-players).
