Open **Preferences** in the menu. **Profile** is what the other players at your tables see of you; **This browser**
is how Delve looks here.

## Profile

- **Display name** and **Pronouns**.
- **Avatar**: one of twelve emblems, or **Upload a picture** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 2 MB). A picture shows
  instead of the emblem.
- **Colour**: the mark beside your lines in table talk.
- **Your language**: the language the story and the DM's answers come to you in.
- **About me**: what the other players read when they click your face at the table.
- **Note for the host**: only the hosts of your tables read it.
- **Time zone**: lets your hosts see what time it is where you are.

Choose **Save** when you are done.

Under **Account**, **Change your password**, see **Where you're logged in**, and **Log out everywhere** if you left
Delve open on a device you no longer have.

Under **Email**, add an address: only you see it. Delve sends it a link; **Confirm the address** from that link,
and until you do Delve sends nothing else there, and an address you had stays in use. **Send the link again** if it
didn't come. Choose whether you get **Email from my hosts** and **Email about replies on the board**; each of those
emails also has a link that turns its kind off. **Email** shows once the owner has set up email; if you log in with
Google, Google's address is yours when you have none.

On the **Board**, choose **Follow** on a topic, or tick **Email me about replies** as you post, to get an email
when someone replies there. A busy topic sends you one email, and the next only once you have been back to read it.
**Stop following** ends it. Both show once the owner has set up email.

## This browser

**Look** sets the colours and fonts: **Delve today**, **Gold and wood** or **Dark dungeon**. The other choice,
**Show "Did you know?" while the DM thinks**, shows a fact of the campaign's world while you wait. Both hold for this
browser only, and "Did you know?" never shows on a phone.
