A campaign is one table's game: a module, its characters and the story so far. You are the host of every campaign
you start.

1. Open **Campaigns**.
2. Give it a **Name** and pick the **Module**. Drafts aren't offered; mark a draft ready first. **Filter the modules**
   narrows a long list.
3. Check the **Ruleset** and the **Setting** (or **No setting**). They start as the module's details say.
   The rulesets are those this Delve has, such as OSRIC 3.0; each brings its own rules,
   monsters and spells.
4. Under **House rules**, tick the bundles you play with, and any **Single rules** besides.
5. Choose **New campaign**. Its page opens.

## Characters

The characters are made in play, with the DM. On a new campaign's page, choose **Open the table** and start the
session there. On the play screen, a first act such as "Make us a party of four" will do. If the module has pregenerated
characters, the play screen offers them; choose **Ask the DM to make them**.

Players who join later pick a free character, or ask the DM to make one for them.

## The campaign's page

The page has tabs:

- **Overview**: the banner, which you can paint again with a hint; **At the table** (voice and initiative); the
  **Table listing**; **When you play**; and the campaign's **House rules**.
- **Party**: the characters, their full sheets, the seats and the portrait style.
- **Maps**: each map's state, the campaign's own maps and its world maps.
- **World**: **Factions and clocks**, **Quests** and **Possessions**, which the DM moves in play and you can edit.
- **DM log**: what the DM did each beat, and what it cost.

**Status** sets it **In progress**, **Paused** or **Complete**. When the module has changed since the campaign
started, **Upgrade to the latest revision** brings the change in; until then the campaign plays its own copy.
