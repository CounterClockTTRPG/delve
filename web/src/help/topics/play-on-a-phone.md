On a phone the play screen shows the story and the box under it. Everything else is in the bar along the bottom: tap
a name to open its screen, and **Back to the story** (or the same name again) to close it.

- **Map**: the dungeon map, with what your characters have found so far.
- **Party**: the party's cards. Tap one for its sheet.
- **Table**: [table talk](/help/table-talk) and its box, raised hands, voice and who is at the table.
- **World**: the world map as the party knows it, with the quests, factions and possessions the characters know of.
- **Setting**: the campaign's world to read up on, under **Lore**, **Facts** and **Maps**.
- **History**: where the party has been, when the host carried it on from an earlier adventure.

A dot on **Map**, **Party** or **Table** means something changed there since you last looked: hit points, a new room,
a new line of talk.

## On a wider screen

From 600 pixels wide the same panels sit beside the story, in three tabs: **Party**, **Maps** and **Table**. **Maps**
holds the **Map**, **World**, **Setting** and **History** tabs. Table talk stays at the foot of the side column,
whichever tab is open.
