A setting is the world a campaign plays in. Delve comes with its own, **Aun**, and has the others your Delve has
installed; a campaign picks one, or none, when it starts. The DM looks up the setting's places, realms, gods and people as it plays.

Open **Settings** and pick one. Its page has tabs:

- **Lore**: the setting's pages. **Search the lore**, or **Browse by kind**.
- **Calendar**: its months, seasons and festivals. Days pass on this calendar in play.
- **Facts**: short lines about the world, the book's and your own (**Add the fact**). While the DM thinks, the play
  screen shows one as **Did you know?**.
- **Proposals**: what the DM proposed in play.
- **Maps**: the setting's world maps, which every new campaign of the setting copies.

## Change the lore

**Write a page** adds one; **Edit** changes one of the book's. Your pages are tagged **Added on this install** or
**Changed on this install**, and **Revert to the book's page** undoes your changes.

A page's text is Markdown. Link to another page with `[[Page]]` or `[[Page|label]]`. Tick **DM only** to hide the
whole page from players, or hide part of it between two lines:

```
::: dm
The baron is the cult's leader.
:::
```

Players read the setting under the play screen's **Setting** tab, without anything DM only.

## The DM's proposals

When play invents something, such as a new inn or a change to a lord's fate, the DM can propose it. It holds at once
in the campaign that made it. Under **Proposals**, **Accept** makes it part of the setting for every campaign;
**Edit** it first if you like, or **Reject** it, and it stays in its own campaign only.
