## Send the invite link

1. Open the campaign on the play screen. **Invite** is in the side column, under **Go**; it starts open while you are
   alone at the table.
2. Choose **Copy** and send the link, or let a friend scan the QR code.
3. Whoever opens it makes an account or logs in, chooses **Play** or **Watch**, picks a free character or asks the
   DM to make one, and goes **To the table**.

**New link** makes the old link stop working. Use it if a link got out.

**Who may watch**, on the campaign page under **At the table**, decides what **Watch** does: **Anyone with the link**
takes a seat to watch at once; with **Only those you let in**, watching is a request you answer, as a player's is at
a public table; with **Nobody**, the link offers no watching. Those watching already stay.

<p class="note">If your browser is on <code>localhost</code>, other devices can't use the link. Open Delve on this
machine's network address instead.</p>

## Let in requests to join

A [public table](/help/host-a-public-game) gets requests from accounts on this Delve. They show under **Requests to
join** in the side column, each with its note. Reply in the thread, then **Accept** (they get a player's seat) or
**Decline**, with a reason for them if you like. A full table can't accept a new player: free a place, or raise the
places in the table listing.

## Seats and characters

Open **The host's controls** in the side column. Under **Seats**:

- **Make co-host** gives a player the host's controls. **Make player** and **Make observer** change a seat's role.
  Observers read the story and the table talk, and never act.
- **Hand over the host** makes someone else the host; you become a co-host.
- **Remove** takes someone off the table, and frees their characters. With the invite link they could join again, so
  make a new link if they shouldn't.

Under **Who plays whom**, give any character to any seat, or to **Nobody**.

## Accounts you make yourself

In **Preferences**, open the **Players** tab and choose **Open Players**. **Add a player** makes an account and
gives you a link to hand its player; the link works once, for 24 hours. Accounts that asked to sign up wait there
too, to **Let in** or **Turn away**: see [Accounts and sign-up](/help/accounts-and-sign-up).
