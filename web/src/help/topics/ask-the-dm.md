Not everything is an act. To ask how surprise works, or what your character remembers of a place, ask the DM out of
the story.

1. Above the box under the story, choose **Ask the DM**. The line above the box says **Ask the DM: only you see the
   answer**.
2. Write your question and press Enter, or the **Ask the DM** button.

Ctrl-Enter (⌘-Enter on a Mac) asks the DM from either mode, so you don't need to switch.

The question and its answer show in the story marked **Only you**. The DM answers from the rules, what your characters
carry and what they saw lately, never from the adventure's secrets. The story doesn't move on.

## Sharing the answer

If the answer helps everyone, choose **Share with the table** under it. It then shows in table talk, under your
question. On a desktop, a right-click on the answer shares it too.

An observer, who watches but doesn't play, can ask the DM too.
