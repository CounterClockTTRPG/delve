The story is everything said in play, oldest first: the players' acts, the DM's narration, the lines of the people you
meet beside their portraits, and the pictures the DM shows.

- **Only you** marks what the DM told your characters alone. The other players don't see it.
- **Recap** is the DM's summary of a session, written when the session ends. It opens the next one.
- **translating…** marks a paragraph still in English. The story comes in your language once each paragraph is
  translated.
- A picture enlarges when you click it. The strip at the top of the screen holds every picture of the story, newest
  first.

The story follows new entries while you are at the bottom. Scroll up and it stays where you are; **Back to the latest**
takes you down again. Scrolling to the top loads earlier entries, down to **The start of the story**.

On a desktop, a right-click on an entry offers **Copy the text**.

## A party carried on

When the host carried your party on from an earlier adventure, a **History** tab tells where the party has been. It
holds only what the whole table was told.

## The look

The swatch in the header changes the look: **Delve today**, **Gold and wood** or **Dark dungeon**. It holds for this
browser only, so each player picks their own.
