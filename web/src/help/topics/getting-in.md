There are three ways in. Which you use depends on how the host and the owner of this Delve set things up.

## With an invite link

A host sends you a link to their table. Open it, make an account (or log in if you have one), then pick a character
and choose **To the table**. You can join to **Play** or only to **Watch**.

## By asking for an account

If sign-up is open, choose **No account? Ask for one** on the login page. Write a line in **Who you are** so whoever
lets you in knows you. Your account waits until the owner or an organiser lets you in; then you log in.

## With Google

If the owner set up Google login, the first time choose **Sign up with Google** on the sign-up page: your account then
waits to be let in as any other does. After that, **Continue with Google** on the login page logs you in.

<p class="note">Sign-up closed? Ask the owner or the host of your table for an invite.</p>
