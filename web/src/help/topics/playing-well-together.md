title: Playing well together
audience: newcomer
summary: A few habits that make the game better for the whole table.
order: 6

---
- **Say what you want, not only what you do.** "I search the desk; I'm looking for the mayor's letters" helps the DM
  give you something worth finding.
- **Ask.** If you don't know what's around you, or what your character would know, ask the DM. That's what the DM is
  for.
- **Share the stage.** Leave room for the quieter players, and make your character want things that bring others in.
- **Be bold.** The best stories come from characters who try things. Failing can be the most memorable part of an
  evening.
- **Your character isn't you.** They can be afraid, rude or wrong, and so can the others'. Keep it a game everyone
  enjoys; if something isn't fun for someone, say so out of character.
