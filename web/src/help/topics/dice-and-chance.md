When what you try might fail, such as swinging a sword, leaping a chasm or resisting a poison, a die decides. The
dice are named by their sides: a **d20** has twenty, a **d6** six, and so on. "2d6" means two six-sided dice, added
up.

- **Attacks** are a d20 roll. The higher the roll, and the better your character, the likelier the hit. **Damage** is
  another roll, and it depends on the weapon: a dagger a d4, a long sword a d8.
- **Saving throws** are a d20 rolled to escape the worst: a dragon's breath, a trap's poison, a spell.
- **Ability checks** test one ability: roll a d20, and at or under your Dexterity you keep your footing.

## The DM rolls

You won't need to remember any of this. The DM rolls in Delve and tells you the result. Failure is part of the story
too: a missed jump might leave you hanging off a ledge rather than ending the game.
