Once you have an account, **Find a table** lists the public tables on this Delve that take players. Each shows what
it is about, the house rules in effect and how many places are taken.

Ask to join one, and its host answers. When they let you in, the table appears in **My tables**; choose **Play** to
sit down.

A full table still lets you join to watch.
