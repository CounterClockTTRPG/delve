A schedule tells everyone when the table plays. Each person sees the times in their own time zone, so a table across
countries needs no sums.

## Set the schedule

On the campaign's page, **Overview**, find **When you play**.

1. Choose **How often**: **Every week**, or every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
2. Pick the **First session**. Its weekday becomes the table's day.
3. Set when it **Starts** and **Ends**. An end before the start runs past midnight.
4. Check the **Time zone**. It starts as your browser's. A change to or from summer time keeps the local time.
5. Choose **Save the schedule**.

Below the form, the rule and the next sessions show in your own time. **No schedule** removes it.

## Move or skip the next session

On the play screen, the host and co-hosts see **Next session** in the side column.

- **Move** it to a new date and time, in the schedule's time zone. It keeps its length, and shows as moved.
- **Skip** it; the one after becomes the next.
- **Undo the move** puts a moved session back.

With mail set up on this Delve, **Tell the players by email** sits above the buttons. Tick it, add a line if you like,
then **Move** or **Skip**: everyone else at the table who has a confirmed address, and hasn't switched these emails
off, gets the new time in their own time zone, with your line. Undoing a move sends nothing. Delve says how many were
told.

## Where players see it

The next session shows in **My tables**, at the head of the play screen while the table waits and, for a public
table, in **Find a table**.

The schedule only says when to come. You still start each session yourself: see
[Running a session](/help/run-a-session).
