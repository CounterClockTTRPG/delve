## Getting a character

When you join a table to play, **Pick your characters** lists the characters nobody plays yet. Tick one or more, or
describe the one you want under **Or ask the DM to make one** ("a halfling thief, say"). Then choose **To the table**.
A request for a new character is your first act: the DM makes it with you in play, and it is yours as soon as it is
made. Ask the DM, the question box, doesn't make characters.

Before the host starts the session, the table can't act yet: your request waits in your box under the story. Send it
with **Act** once the session has started.

If you sit at the table without one, **You play no character yet** shows under the story. Choose **Choose or ask for a
character** to pick one or ask for one.

## The party

The **Party** tab beside the story lists the party in marching order, front first. Each card shows the character's
portrait, hit points and AC, class and level, any conditions, and who plays it. The party's coin is under the cards,
after **Purse:**.

Characters who fell, retired or left show under the cards in one line each, such as "Fallen: Tansy"; open it to
reach their sheets.

## The sheet

Click a party card to open its sheet: abilities, hit points, AC, THAC0, XP and move, saving throws, attacks,
proficiencies, thief skills, inventory and load, spells and background. **ready to level** beside the XP means the
character has enough for the next level.

You don't edit the sheet. The DM keeps it up to date as you play: damage, spells cast, treasure found. Close it with ×
or Escape.

## Portraits

Delve draws each character's portrait, in the style the host chose for the campaign. The portrait shows how the
character is: badly hurt, frightened or down. Click it to see it larger.
