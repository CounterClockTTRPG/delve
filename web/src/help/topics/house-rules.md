House rules are the changes your table makes to the ruleset. They live in one library under **House rules**, and
each campaign picks the ones it plays with. The DM gets each rule at the moment it applies.

A fresh install has none. **Import the example rules** adds the rules ttrpg2 played with, for you to edit.

## Write a rule

Choose **New rule**, then:

- **Title** and **Text (Markdown)**: the rule in your words. **Preview** shows it rendered.
- **Kind**: **General**, **Race**, **Class**, **Item** or **Behaviour**.
- **When it applies**: such as **Character creation**, **Combat** or **Always**. This is when the DM is given the rule.
- **Ruleset** and **Setting**: tie the rule to one, or leave them empty for any. A campaign on another ruleset or
  setting leaves the rule out.
- **Subject**: the race, class or item it is about, such as lizardfolk.
- **Enabled**: untick it to switch the rule off everywhere without deleting it.

Choose **Save rule**. An edit applies at once to every campaign that uses the rule.

A heading that starts with "DM Notes" begins a part for organisers only: in **Rulesets**, anyone else reads the rule
without it.

## Bundles

A bundle groups rules you choose together, such as your usual table. Choose **New bundle**, give it a **Title**, pick
each rule under **Add a rule** and choose **Add**, then **Save bundle**. Deleting a bundle keeps its rules in the
library.

## Choose them for a campaign

A new campaign ticks its bundles, and any **Single rules** besides, under **House rules** on the **Campaigns** form.
Later, change them on the campaign's page, **Overview**, **House rules**, and choose **Save house rules**.

Players see the house rules in effect in **Find a table**, and a campaign's race, class and item rules beside the
book's in **Rulesets**.
