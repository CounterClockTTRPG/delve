Delve runs old-school fantasy roleplaying games in the browser. Claude is the Dungeon Master: it describes the world,
plays everyone you meet, and follows the rules and the adventure the host picked. You and the other players are the
party.

- **A table** is one game: an adventure, its characters and the story so far. You can sit at several tables.
- **The host** (an organiser) sets a table up, chooses the adventure and the house rules, and lets players in.
- **A session** is one evening of play. The story so far is kept, so the next session goes on where you stopped.

You play by writing what your character does. The DM answers, rolls what needs rolling, and keeps your sheet up to
date. New to roleplaying games? [Start here](/help/what-is-a-roleplaying-game).

## Finding your way

**Help** in the menu (a **?** in the corner when you sit at a table by a link) shows the help about the screen you
are on. The play screen, Preferences and, for hosts, the campaign pages each have a short tour too: it is offered the
first time you open the screen, and **Help** plays it again. Know Delve already? **Skip the tours**, and none
is offered again.
