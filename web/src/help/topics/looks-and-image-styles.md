Two settings change how Delve looks, and they belong to different people.

## The look: each browser's own

Delve comes in three looks: **Delve today**, **Gold and wood** and **Dark dungeon**. A look changes colours, fonts and
textures, never where things are. Maps and printed pages keep their own colours.

Pick one under **Preferences → This browser → Look**, or with the swatch in the play screen's header. It holds for
this browser only, so each player at a table picks their own, and you can't choose it for them.

The same tab has **Show "Did you know?" while the DM thinks**, also for this browser only.

## The image style: the whole install's

**Image style**, under **Preferences → Backends**, sets the look of every painted picture: portraits, scenes,
possessions and banners, in every campaign. Organisers can change it too.

- **From the campaign's tone** lets each campaign's tone choose the painter. This is where Delve starts.
- A preset, such as **Heroic oils (Elmore and Caldwell)** or **Ink and watercolour (DiTerlizzi)**, shows a sample
  picture and its words.
- **Custom…** takes your own words, up to 1,000 characters.

Choose **Save** at the foot of the tab. Pictures painted from then on use the new style; earlier ones stay as they
were.

The image style is for pictures that Replicate or assetgen paint. The free portraits that portraitgen draws follow
each campaign's **Portrait style** instead, which an organiser sets on the campaign's page.
