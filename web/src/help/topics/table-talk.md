**Table talk** is for the people at the table, out of the story: it never goes to the DM as an act, so plan, joke
and argue there. (Ask the DM does see recent talk, to answer a question about it.) It sits at the foot of the side
column, with a box of its own; on a phone it is on the **Table** screen. While you are alone at the table, as in a
solo campaign, there is nobody to talk to: the talk is left out, and comes back as soon as someone else sits down.

Write a line and press Enter or **Send**. **Quote** on a line puts it at the top of your box, to answer it. The faces in its header
show who is at the table; click one to see their profile.

## Raising a hand

To have a word before anyone acts, choose **Raise hand** in the talk's header. You may add your point; then choose
**Raise hand** again. Everyone sees your hand at the top of the side column, and the other players see a line by
their Act box. Choose **Lower hand** when you have had your say. The host can lower it too.

## Stepping away

Need a few minutes? Choose **Step away** in the talk's header and say what your characters do meanwhile: **Follow the
table's setting**, **Are played cautiously by the DM**, **Stay in the background** or **Sit the rest of the session
out**. The table plays on without waiting for you, and the others see you as **Stepped away**. When you return,
choose **I'm back** by the act box, or simply act.

## Voice

If the host allows voice, **Join voice** is in the header (on a phone, on the **Table** screen). Your browser asks for
the microphone; without one you join **Listening only**. **Mute** and **Leave voice** are beside it. Nothing is
recorded.

## When the session starts

Before a session the story says **Waiting for the table**, with how many players are here. You can talk and ask the
DM, but you act only once the host starts the session. On a break the story says **On a break** and waits where it
stopped. Only the host and co-hosts start, pause and end a session.

If you have to go for good, **Leave the table** under **At the table** frees your characters for others.
