# The Sunken Abbey

An abbey the marsh took back. This text holds every construct the editor
writes, in the form it writes it.

## Approach {#approach}

The causeway is *slick with weed* and **half under water**. Brother Ansel
(*[[npc:brother-ansel]]*) waits at the gate; he serves
[[faction:grey-lantern]] and says so.\
He will not cross the threshold.

## A1. Gatehouse {#a1}

> The gate hangs from one hinge. Beyond it, a courtyard of black water
> laps at the steps of the chapel.
>
> Something moves under the surface.

Ansel stops at the arch and points.

> "The abbot drowned them all," he whispers. "Every one of them, in **one**
> night."

What the party finds here:

- A rusted portcullis winch
  - its chain is gone
  - its crank turns, with a shriek
- A lantern, still warm
- Footprints that lead into the water

To open the chapel doors:

1. Turn the winch three times.
2. Pull the left ring.
3. Say the abbot's name ([see the archive](text:archive#names)).

| Roll | Result | Notes |
| --- | :---: | ---: |
| 1-2 | Eels | 2d4 of them |
| 3 | Ansel's brother | *drowned*, see [[npc:brother-hugh]] |
| 4-6 | Nothing | the water is still |
